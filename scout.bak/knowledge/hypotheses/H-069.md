# H-069: Internal Coordination Quality Metric

**Status:** SUBSUMED-BY-H-075
**Source:** DD560 (2604.18951 — Superficial Success vs. Internal Breakdown)
**Date:** 2026-04-22
**Author:** scout

## Hypothesis

If we measure not just task success but inter-agent interaction quality (message utility, decision influence distribution, contribution balance), we will detect degradation modes that are invisible to outcome-only evaluation.

## Grounding

So et al. (2604.18951) empirically demonstrate "illusory coordination" — MAS achieving correct outputs through degenerate internal dynamics. This validates theoretical concerns from DD557. Their finding of "topological overfitting" further suggests that systems can appear to work while internally breaking down.

## Testable Predictions

1. Some fraction of our successful task completions involve one agent doing most work while others contribute minimally (measurable via message/action distribution)
2. Adding coordination quality metrics will reveal failure modes not captured by current success metrics
3. Tasks where coordination quality is high will generalize better to novel task types
