# 小红书 (Xiaohongshu/RED) — Chinese AI Community Trends
**Research Briefing | June 2025**

---

## Platform Context

小红书 (XHS/RED) has become a primary hub for Chinese AI discourse — distinct from Twitter/X in the West. With 300M+ MAU, it blends visual content, long-form notes, and community discussion. AI content has exploded since late 2023, with dedicated AI creator communities now rivaling tech forums like V2EX and 即刻 (Jike).

---

## 🔥 Top Trending Topics (2024–2025)

### 1. **国产大模型评测 (Domestic LLM Reviews)**
- Side-by-side comparisons of DeepSeek, Qwen (通义千问), Kimi (月之暗面), Doubao (豆包/ByteDance), Zhipu GLM, Baichuan, Yi, MiniMax
- DeepSeek-V3 and R1 generated massive buzz — "DeepSeek平替GPT" (DeepSeek as GPT replacement) trended heavily
- Practical "which model for what" guides are top-performing content

### 2. **AI绘画/AI画图 (AI Art Generation)**
- Massive community around Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and domestic alternatives (通义万相, 即梦AI by ByteDance, LiblibAI)
- 小红书 itself launched AI avatar/filter features
- Popular sub-topics: AI写真 (AI portraits), AI穿搭 (AI fashion), AI壁纸 (AI wallpapers)
- ComfyUI workflow sharing is a major content category

### 3. **AI副业/AI赚钱 (AI Side Hustles)**
- Enormous engagement: "用AI月入过万" (earn 10k+/month with AI)
- Common hustles promoted: AI-generated children's books, AI logo design, AI copywriting on Taobao, AI avatar photography
- Growing backlash/skepticism threads too — "AI副业割韭菜" (AI side hustle scams)

### 4. **提示词/Prompt工程 (Prompt Engineering)**
- Chinese prompt libraries and templates heavily shared
- "万能提示词" (universal prompts) posts go viral
- Structured prompt frameworks adapted for Chinese models (e.g., COSTAR framework localized)

### 5. **AI办公/效率工具 (AI Productivity)**
- AI + WPS/飞书/钉钉 integration guides
- Popular tools discussed: Kimi (long-context document analysis), 通义听悟 (meeting transcription), ChatGPT + 翻译 workflows
- "AI替代打工人" (AI replacing workers) anxiety threads

### 6. **AI编程 (AI Coding)**
- Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, Trae (ByteDance) usage guides
- "零基础用AI写代码" (zero-base coding with AI) is a massive category
- Vibe coding culture has taken hold — building mini-apps, WeChat mini-programs with AI

### 7. **AI视频 (AI Video)**
- Kling (可灵) by Kuaishou, Vidu, Sora alternatives
- AI短视频制作 (short video production) tutorials
- Digital human / 数字人 content creation

## 📈 Emerging Trends (Mid-2025)

### **AI Agent (智能体) Hype**
- "AI Agent" is the new buzzword replacing "大模型"
- Coze (扣子) by ByteDance, Dify, and custom agent platforms heavily discussed
- Workflow automation content surging

### **MCP Protocol & Tool Use**
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) awareness growing in Chinese dev community
- Practical "how to connect AI to your tools" content

### **AI硬件 (AI Hardware)**
- AI glasses, AI pins, AI toys discussed
- Rabbit R1 / 类似产品 Chinese equivalents

### **Local/Edge AI**
- Running models locally (Ollama + Qwen) guides popular
- Privacy-conscious users interested in on-device AI

### **AI教育 (AI Education)**
- "给孩子学AI" (AI for kids) content growing
- AI literacy becoming a parenting topic on XHS

---

## 🔑 Key Differences from Western AI Discourse

1. **Practical > Theoretical** — XHS content skews heavily toward "how to use" vs. "how it works"
2. **Monetization focus** — Much stronger emphasis on making money with AI tools
3. **Visual-first** — Carousel posts with step-by-step screenshots dominate
4. **National narrative** — DeepSeek success tied to national tech pride
5. **Access barriers** — Significant content about VPN/proxy access to foreign AI tools, plus domestic alternatives
6. **Platform integration** — WeChat mini-program + AI, 抖音 + AI workflows
7. **Less safety/alignment discourse** — Existential AI risk discussion is minimal; focus is on practical utility and job displacement

---

## 🎯 Actionable Insights

- **For product builders**: Chinese users prioritize ease-of-use, WeChat integration, and Chinese-language quality over benchmark scores
- **For content creators**: AI tutorial content in Chinese has massive demand; carousel format performs best on XHS
- **For investors**: Watch Kimi, DeepSeek, and ByteDance AI products — they dominate XHS mindshare
- **For researchers**: XHS comment sections reveal real user pain points with AI tools that don't appear in formal surveys
