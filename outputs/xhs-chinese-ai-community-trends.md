# 小红书 (XHS/RedNote) Chinese AI Community Trends — Research Report

**Date:** 2026-05-30  
**Method:** Bing search aggregation (XHS requires authentication for direct search access)

---

## Executive Summary

小红书 (Xiaohongshu/RedNote) has become a major hub for Chinese AI community discussions, blending consumer AI education, monetization strategies, and platform-level AI integration. Key themes:

1. **XHS is building its own AI stack** — launched its first proprietary large model (June 2025), open-sourced DeepAgent (Oct 2025), and integrated Kimi as an in-app AI partner (May 2026)
2. **AI副业 (AI side hustles) dominate user content** — AI art + XHS monetization, AI-powered content creation, and AI tools for growth are the hottest creator topics
3. **DeepSeek is the community's default model** — massively discussed for content creation, data analysis, and paired with XHS workflows
4. **Platform-AI integration accelerating** — Kimi formally partnered with XHS (May 2026), "点点搜索" (Dian Dian Search) is XHS's AI-native search feature

### Timeline
| Date | Milestone |

| 2023-2024 | Integrated MiniMax, DeepSeek models into app for different agents |

| May 2026 | **Kimi partnership** — Kimi's product entry point embedded directly in XHS |
| May 2026 | 36Kr reports: "小红书四年AI路：FOMO、犹豫，到突然加速" |

### Key Insight
XHS took a cautious approach to AI (unlike Douyin/ByteDance). Their strategy is **"AI + content community"** — using AI to enhance the social/discovery experience rather than building a standalone AI product. The Kimi partnership signals a shift: AI companies are now treating XHS as a distribution channel, not just a marketing platform.

## 2. Hottest AI Topics Among XHS Users

### 🔥 AI副业/变现 (AI Side Hustles & Monetization)
The #1 community discussion topic. Key formats:
- **AI绘画 + 小红书变现** — Using MidJourney/Stable Diffusion/DeepSeek for art, selling templates/prints on XHS
- Case study: "AI绘画+DeepSeek文案" combo → 23K followers in 3 months, single post selling MidJourney素材包 for ¥50K revenue
- **AI写作工具** — Content creation assistants for XHS posts
- "0基础也能月入X万" (zero-skill monthly income) narratives dominate

### 🔥 DeepSeek + 小红书运营
- DeepSeek positioned as the Swiss Army knife for XHS creators
- Use cases: content ideation, data analysis of post performance, competitor research, copywriting
- Tutorial content: "如何用DeepSeek对小红书笔记进行数据分析"

### 🔥 AI绘画 (AI Art)
- MidJourney, Stable Diffusion, native Chinese tools
- Heavy overlap with e-commerce (selling prints, custom designs, digital products)

### 🔥 AI工具推荐 (AI Tool Recommendations)
- Users actively curating and reviewing AI tools
- Popular tools: DeepSeek, Kimi, 豆包 (Doubao/ByteDance), MidJourney, various Chrome extensions
- "6个好用又不花钱的写作神器" style posts perform well

## 4. The Kimi × XHS Partnership (May 2026)

A landmark event in Chinese AI distribution:
- Moonshot AI (Kimi) embedded its product directly within XHS
- Represents "AI to C" (AI to consumer) strategy shift
- After DeepSeek's viral breakout humbled the "大模型六小龙" (Six AI Dragons), companies like Kimi stopped being precious about distribution
- XHS benefits: enhanced product experience + commercial ecosystem optimization
- Quote from 36Kr: "将自家产品入口搬上小红书，既是Kimi继续向外寻求流量的体现，也是小红书利用AI提升产品体验"

## 5. Community Sentiment & Dynamics

### Who's Discussing AI on XHS?
- **Content creators/KOLs** seeking efficiency gains
- **副业seekers** — people looking for AI-powered income streams
- **Tech-curious general users** — XHS democratizes AI literacy for non-technical audiences
- **Brand marketers** using AI for XHS campaign optimization

### Sentiment Patterns
- Strong **pragmatic/monetization orientation** — less philosophical than Zhihu, more "how do I make money with this"
- **Visual-first** — AI art/design content performs better than text-heavy technical posts
- **Tutorial/教程 format** dominates — step-by-step guides get highest engagement
- DeepSeek has achieved near-universal recognition; Kimi gaining ground through partnership

## 7. Key Takeaways for AI Practitioners

1. **XHS is where AI meets mainstream China** — if your AI product isn't being discussed on XHS, it hasn't truly penetrated the Chinese consumer market
2. **Distribution > Technology** — the Kimi×XHS partnership shows that AI companies now prioritize reaching users where they already are
3. **Monetization narratives drive adoption** — Chinese users adopt AI tools primarily through "副业" (side hustle) framing
4. **DeepSeek is the default** — achieved the deepest penetration into XHS creator workflows
5. **MCP/Agent integrations emerging** — tools like Xiaohongshu MCP Server signal developer interest in programmatic XHS interaction
