## Abstract

Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have enabled breakthroughs in mathematical discovery, exemplified by AlphaEvolve, a closed-source system that evolves programs to improve bounds on open problems. However, it relies on ensembles of frontier LLMs to achieve new bounds and is a pure inference system that models cannot internalize the evolving strategies. We introduce *ThetaEvolve*, an open-source framework that simplifies and extends AlphaEvolve to efficiently scale both in-context learning and Reinforcement Learning (RL) at test time, allowing models to continually learn from their experiences in improving open optimization problems. ThetaEvolve features a single LLM, a large program database for enhanced exploration, batch sampling for higher throughput, lazy penalties to discourage stagnant outputs, and optional reward shaping for stable training signals, etc. ThetaEvolve is the first evolving framework that enable a small open-source model, like DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B, to achieve new best-known bounds on open problems (circle packing and first auto-correlation inequality) mentioned in AlphaEvolve. Besides, across two models and four open tasks, we find that ThetaEvolve with RL at test-time consistently outperforms inference-only baselines, and the model indeed learns evolving capabilities, as the RL-trained checkpoints demonstrate faster progress and better final performance on both trained target task and other unseen tasks. We release our code publicly.¹

The recent development of the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) has enabled them to contribute to new scientific findings, like mathematical discovery (Romera-Paredes et al., 2024; Charton et al., 2024; Wagner, 2021; Fawzi et al., 2022). A notable recent example is AlphaEvolve (Novikov et al., 2025; Georgiev et al., 2025), which uses pre-designed evaluators together with frontier LLMs to iteratively modify and improve candidate programs toward optimizing task-specific objectives. Through this evolutionary process, AlphaEvolve has discovered solutions that match or improve the best-known results for several open mathematical optimization problems. AlphaEvolve is well-suited for problems that aim to construct specific mathematical objects to improve their certain quantitative properties, such as arranging a fixed number of circles within a unit square to maximize the sum of radii (Friedman, 2012) (referred to as `CirclePacking` in our work).

今天我们分享解读的是一篇来自**微软研究院、华盛顿大学、CMU**等顶尖机构联合发表的重磅论文——《ThetaEvolve: Test-time Learning on Open Problems》（ThetaEvolve：在开放问题上的测试时学习）。不久前，谷歌DeepMind的AlphaEvolve系统利用大模型在多个开放数学问题上取得了突破，震惊了科学界。然而，AlphaEvolve是一个闭源系统，依赖于昂贵的、多个前沿大模型的"合奏"，并且模型自身无法从这个演化过程中学习。

为了打破这一局面，这支强大的研究团队提出了**ThetaEvolve**，一个开源的、经过简化和扩展的演化框架。它不仅能高效地扩展上下文学习，更关键的是，它**首次将强化学习（RL）引入到这个"测试时"（test-time）的演化过程中**，让模型能够从解决开放问题的经验中持续学习和进化。

ThetaEvolve的核心创新包括：使用**单个LLM**而非模型集成、一个**更大的程序数据库**以增强探索、**批量采样**以提高吞吐量、**懒惰惩罚**以避免停滞，以及可选的**奖励塑造**来稳定RL训练。

最令人瞩目的是，ThetaEvolve使得一个**开源的8B小模型**（DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B），在"**圆堆积**"和"**第一自相关不等式**"这两个开放问题上，**刷新了已知的世界纪录**！这颠覆了人们普遍认为只有前沿闭源大模型才能在这些任务上取得突破的认知。此外，研究还证明，经过RL训练的模型确实"学会"了演化能力，在未见过的任务上也表现出更快的进步和更好的性能。

1. 引言

AlphaEvolve通过一个演化式的程序搜索过程，在多个开放数学问题上取得了突破。其核心流程是：

1 维护一个存储了高质量或多样性程序的**程序数据库**。

2 在每次迭代中，从数据库中采样一些程序作为上下文，构建一个prompt。

3 将prompt输入一个**LLM集成**，生成改进后的"子程序"。

4 对子程序进行评估，并将其添加回数据库。
