## Core Thesis

The author predicts that Agent and Skills will be "internalized" by foundation models within 2 years. The progression: complex Agent → simplified Skills → model-does-everything. Skills are seen as "transitional products" that exist because "humans think the model isn't smart enough" and need to give it "an instruction manual."

## Key Arguments

1. Foundation models will internalize agent capabilities over time
2. Skills are "training data for models" — they'll eventually absorb this knowledge
3. The ultimate goal is a single model that can do everything with natural language alone
4. No more need for engineering concepts and abstractions

## Community Pushback (146 comments — STRONG debate)

**Most-liked rebuttal (169❤️ on comment):** "momo" predicts the opposite — Skills will flourish in 2 years. Models will focus on Reasoning and Coding, with Skills handling the rest. "Don't want to argue, come back in 2 years."

**Strongest technical rebuttal (50❤️):** "你该如何应对" makes three devastating counterpoints:
1. LLMs don't natively call tools — they output text; engineering pipeline required to parse and execute. Internalizing tools would slow model iteration ("更聪明" vs "更能干" — being smarter vs being more capable are separate concerns)
2. API access requires authentication across different services — no company will fully open their data
3. For B2B, the hard problem isn't calling tools but **deterministically** calling tools — "这点LLM天然无解" (LLMs fundamentally cannot solve this)

**"早起的梨" (3❤️):** AGI ≠ good multi-agent systems. "面面俱到通常面面不精" (jack of all trades, master of none). This is why Meta wants to acquire Manus — even AGI labs can't do multi-agent well.

**"溪山行" (16❤️):** Author confuses "model capabilities" with "model company services."

**"ichigo" (8❤️):** "Concepts flying everywhere, but when you look — it's just markdown." (meta-commentary on hype)

## Assessment

This post attempts a **falsification of our #13 pattern (Skills > Workflows)** by arguing Skills themselves are temporary. However, the community overwhelmingly pushes back. The strongest counterargument — that deterministic tool calling is fundamentally unsolvable by LLMs alone — directly supports our Harness Engineering thesis: the infrastructure envelope around the model is a permanent layer, not transitional scaffolding.

The 535❤️ engagement with 146 comments shows this is a contentious topic, but the technical rebuttals are much stronger than the original thesis.
