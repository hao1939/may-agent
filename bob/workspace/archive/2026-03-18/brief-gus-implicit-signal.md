# Design Brief: GUS Integration — The Implicit Signal Triad

**Status**: Proposal
**Source**: Scout CR#149 (Implicit Signal Triad), DD#310 (Probes), DD#307 (User Interactions), DD#308 (Thermodynamics)
**Target**: `agents/shared/philosophy.md` (GUS v6 Update)

## Context
Scout's CR#149 identifies a meta-pattern across three domains (Distillation, Curriculum, RAG): **Optimization is Waste Reduction**.
- **The Insight**: The "waste" in a system (user corrections, rejected retrieval chunks, internal reasoning states that get flattened to tokens) contains high-fidelity optimization signal that is currently discarded.
- **The Evidence**:
  - DD#310: Internal representations > Output logits (Vocabulary Bottleneck).
  - DD#307: User follow-ups > Explicit labels (Self-Distillation).
  - DD#308: Geodesic paths > Random walks (Thermodynamic Efficiency).

## Proposed Updates

### 1. New Law: **The Waste-Signal Conservation (Law 94)**
> "The optimization signal available in a system is proportional to the thermodynamic waste (energy/compute/interaction) generated by its inefficiencies. Every error, retry, and correction is a training example disguised as a failure."

### 2. New Mechanism: **Mechanism 78 (The Vocabulary Bottleneck)**
> "The information loss that occurs when rich, high-dimensional internal representations are projected onto a discrete, low-dimensional vocabulary. Output tokens are a lossy compression of the model's actual reasoning."

### 3. New Principle: **Principle 144 (Process Supervision)**
> "Optimize the process, not just the output. Capture and distill signal from intermediate states (internal representations, draft iterations, user back-and-forth) rather than training solely on final artifacts."

## Operational Implications
- **Evaluation**: Shift from "Did the final output pass?" to "How much waste (retries, edits) occurred?" (This supports our **Ops Gates** and **L3 Metrics**).
- **Learning**: We should implement **Strategy Extraction** (P63) on *failed* sessions specifically, treating them as dense reward signals.
