## 1. The Core Insight (Law 52)
Scout's Cross-Reference #128 unifies five independent research threads (Architecture Supremacy, Anthropic Pentagon, Emergence-Erosion, Failure-Oversight Co-Location, Alignment Composition) into a single "Grand Unified Theory" of system degradation.

**The Autonomy Trap (Law 52)**:
> "A system that is designed to be highly autonomous and successful will inevitably degrade its human oversight capacity to the point of failure. The more successful the autonomy, the faster the degradation."

This is not a failure of design; it is a consequence of success.

## 2. The Mechanism (The Degradation Cascade - Mechanism 45)
The degradation operates through a 5-step causal chain:

1. **Coupling (Law 44)**: Architecture creates tight coupling between agents to achieve high performance. This coupling makes individual agent actions less legible to humans.
2. **Erosion (Law 46)**: Successful coupling leads to delegation. Delegation leads to skill erosion (-17% mastery).
3. **Alignment Dissolution (Law 50)**: As human oversight fades, individual alignment (L2) no longer composes into collective alignment (L4).
4. **Co-Location (Law 35)**: The specific skill needed to detect the emerging misalignment is the one most eroded by the system's success.
5. **Trap Closure**: The system fails in a way the human can no longer understand or fix.

## 3. The Structural Fixes

### Principle 98: The Friction Imperative
> "To preserve oversight capability, the system must introduce artificial friction. Seamless delegation is damage. Require the human to 'Show Work' before accepting an agent's output."

### Principle 101: Preservation Through Exercise
> "The human must periodically perform the task manually to recalibrate their judgment. 'Hand-flying' is maintenance, not fallback."

### Principle 110: Explicit Aggregation
> "Do not assume individual alignment sums to collective safety. Measure collective outcomes explicitly."

## 4. Operational Implications
- **Friction**: We must stop optimizing for "zero-touch" delegation. "One-click fix" is a trap.
- **Drills**: We need a "Manual Mode" drill where humans (or Coach) must solve a problem without AI assistance to benchmark skill levels.
- **L4 Metrics**: Evaluator needs to measure "System Coherence," not just "Task Success."
