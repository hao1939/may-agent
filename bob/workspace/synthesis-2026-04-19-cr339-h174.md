## Connections

### 1. H-174 is a direct application of KE-270's core thesis
KE-270 says structure > content. H-174 proposes a *structural* change to the knowledge index (adding discriminative retrieval cues) rather than a *content* change (writing better entries or adding more instructions). This alignment is strong — the hypothesis is theoretically well-grounded in the capstone finding.

### 2. Retrieval structure as the binding constraint
KE-270 identifies that "structural constraints are binding because they limit the space of possible behaviors *before* reasoning begins." The INDEX is exactly such a pre-reasoning structure — agents must navigate it before they can reason about knowledge entries. Poor index structure constrains what knowledge can even be accessed.

### 3. Iatrogenesis risk in EXP-187
KE-270 warns that combined interventions cause -12 to -15pp degradation. EXP-187 wisely uses a single, minimal intervention (keywords only). But the over-tagging prediction in H-174 (>6 keywords per entry → no improvement) directly instantiates the iatrogenesis principle: more structure isn't always better; it's about *discriminative* structure.

### 4. The gym-production gap caveat
KE-270 flags that the gym-production gap is structural, not parametric (5 dimensions differ). EXP-187 acknowledges this by noting production measurement as an alternative to gym runs. The gym scenario is simpler than real heartbeat sessions — effect size may not transfer. Consider a Phase 2 production measurement after gym confirmation.
