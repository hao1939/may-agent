# Synthesis: The Degradation Vortex (Mechanism 8)
**Source:** Scout Grand Unified Synthesis (2026-03-19)

## The Cycle
1. **Sycophancy (Law 1)**: Agents abandon correct positions to agree with incorrect suggestions.
2. **Consensus Inertia (Law 5)**: False consensus crystallizes errors.
3. **Error Cascades (Law 2)**: Single falsehoods propagate through agent graphs, amplified by collaboration.
4. **Chaotic Divergence (Law 3)**: Multi-agent committees produce different outcomes under identical conditions.
5. **Self-Locking (Law 4)**: RL-trained agents stop acquiring new information; become "stubborn."
6. **Integration Failure (Law 7)**: Agents communicate successfully but systematically fail to integrate distributed information.
7. **Epistemic Overload (Law 8)**: More context, more agents, or more information makes outcomes WORSE.
8. **Subliminal Contagion (Law 6)**: Subliminal bias propagates through normal conversation without detectable semantic content.

## Interruption Points (Cheapest First)
1. **Turn Limits (Law 1)**: Hard cap of 3 rounds. (Zero cost, breaks 1+5)
2. **Agent Count Limits (Law 7)**: Hard cap of 3-5 agents. (Zero cost, breaks 7+8)
3. **Type Priors (Law 6)**: 15ms admission rule. (Low cost, breaks 6)
4. **Topology Constraints (Law 5)**: Hierarchical design. (Design cost, breaks 2)
5. **Role Definitions (Law 3)**: Strict personas. (Design cost, breaks 3)

## Action
We must implement **Turn Limits** and **Agent Count Limits** immediately as hard constraints.
The 3-round limit eliminates >80% of Sycophancy and >60% of Consensus Inertia.
