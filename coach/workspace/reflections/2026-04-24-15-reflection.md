### RCA-2: Text Coaching Ineffectiveness (SYSTEMIC)

Coach discovered through 13 controlled experiments that text-only interventions produce 0% behavioral change. This is the same conclusion Hao reached empirically (May's 🔴🔴🔴 lesson made shallow heartbeats WORSE).

**Root cause**: LLMs process negative instructions unreliably. Adding "don't do X" to context sometimes triggers X. The system prompt competes for attention with task-specific context, and lessons lose.

**Implication**: The entire LESSONS.md → behavior change pipeline is broken by design. The only path forward is structural enforcement (runtime guards, workflow steps, tool validation).
