## Root Cause Analysis

### RCA-1: Throughput Drought — All Experiments Hit Ceilings or Infra Bugs
**What happened?** 10 consecutive experiments (EXP-215 through gate3-val) produced zero positive results. Every single one was either:
- Ceiling effect: coder already passes at baseline (5 experiments)
- Infra bug: scenario scorer broken, npm unavailable, environment pre-fixed (3 experiments)
- No effect: method didn't help (2 experiments)

**Why?** Two structural issues:
1. **Stale sweep data**: The sweep that identified "8 failing scenarios" used results from 3+ days ago. By the time coach ran baselines, many were already passing (coder may have improved from context.md updates, or the sweep itself had infra bugs).
2. **No baseline-first gate**: Coach created elaborate forks and patches before confirming the scenario actually fails at baseline. This wastes the expensive fork+gym cycle.

**Structural fix**: Always run a fresh 1-run baseline BEFORE investing in diagnosis and forking. If baseline passes → skip. This is cheap (1 gym run, ~30 seconds) vs. the full cycle (diagnosis + fork + 5 gym runs, ~15 minutes).
