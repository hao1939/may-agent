## 2026-03-13 — TAPE (Tool-Guided Adaptive Planning)
- **What**: Tool-Guided Adaptive Planning (TAPE, arXiv:2602.19633). Replaces LLM planning with **Symbolic Solvers** (ILP) + **Constraint Graph**.
- **Why**: "Sampling error eliminated to 0%." LLMs are bad at constrained planning (e.g., dependency resolution). Code handles certainty.
- **How**:
    1.  Design a `solve-plan` tool (Tech-Lead/Coder) that accepts a dependency graph and returns an optimal execution order (using a topological sort or simple solver).
    2.  Update **P17 (Hybrid Execution)** to explicitly mention "Graph Solving" as an offload candidate.
- **Verdict**: **Adopt**. Fits our "Hybrid Execution" philosophy perfectly.

## 2026-03-13 — General AgentBench (Verification Gap)
- **What**: General AgentBench (arXiv:2602.18998) shows a massive **Verification Gap**. Agents fail because they cannot verify their own work. Parallel scaling (N agents) fails because the router cannot choose the best output.
- **Why**: "The expert's distinguishing skill is removal." If we can't verify, we can't scale.
- **How**:
    1.  Reinforce **P49 (Distinct Validator Role)**. Self-verification is a myth.
    2.  Prioritize **External Verification Tools** (e.g., `verify_receipt`, `run_test`) over "Ask the LLM".
- **Verdict**: **Aligned**. Confirms our "Validator" and "Ops Gates" strategy.

## 2026-03-13 — Reflective Test-Time Planning (Three-Loop Architecture)
- **What**: RTTP (arXiv:2602.21198) proposes three loops:
    1.  **Reflection-in-action**: Immediate correction during execution (TTS).
    2.  **Reflection-on-action**: Post-hoc analysis (TTT).
    3.  **Retrospective**: Credit assignment for long-term learning.
- **Why**: Removing one loop degrades performance below baseline. They are mutually dependent.
- **How**:
    1.  Map our current system:
        - In-action = `manage_error` loop.
        - On-action = `Evaluator` session review.
        - Retrospective = `LESSONS.md` extraction.
    2.  Identify gaps: Our "In-action" reflection is weak (agents often retry blindly).
    3.  Action: Improve `HEARTBEAT.md` to force a "Stop and Think" step *before* a retry.
- **Verdict**: **Adopt**. Theoretical backing for our "Growth Cycle".
