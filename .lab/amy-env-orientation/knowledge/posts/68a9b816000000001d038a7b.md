## Full Text
北大&清华团队发布论文 《Reliable Unlearning Harmful Information in LLMs with Metamorphosis Representation Projection》，提出大语言模型（LLM）遗忘有害信息的创新方法——MRP（Metamorphosis Representation Projection）。
✨ 核心亮点
突破传统局限：相比参数微调，MRP能彻底剔除不良知识，避免“遗忘失败”与“再学习”攻击⚠️
不可逆投影：在隐藏状态空间施加正交投影，精准移除目标任务信息，同时最大限度保留有效知识
稳定多轮遗忘：通过正交矩阵初始化+迭代更新，实现连续多任务遗忘而无灾难性遗忘
实验领先：在 LLaMA2-7B 与 Qwen-7B 上，四任务连续遗忘后评分 0.905，远超基线 0.785，且原有能力几乎不损失
高效低耗：仅需 0.1M 参数，适合资源有限场景，即使缺乏完整保留集，也能依靠相似数据平衡遗忘与保留
安全治理新范式：首次在表示层提出彻底剔除方案，为 LLM 安全合规与治理开辟新方向
👉 详情阅读：arxiv.org/abs/2508.15449

Figure 1: A brief overview of MRP. Our method consists of two key components: Projection Matrix Initialization and Continual Unlearning Training Cycle. For each unlearn data, we first feed both unlearn and retain inputs into the target LLM to extract their hidden state vectors as representations. The unlearning representations are then projected onto the orthogonal complement space of the retention representations. Through PCA, we derive the initial projection matrix, which is combined with the previous projection matrix and integrated into the target LLM to form a hooked LLM. Finally, we perform unlearning fine-tuning on the combined projection matrix using both unlearn and retain data. The trained projection matrix updates the previous projection matrix iteratively, enabling continuous unlearning through successive inputs of unlearn/retain data pairs.
