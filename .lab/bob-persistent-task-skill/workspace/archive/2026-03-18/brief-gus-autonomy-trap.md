## Context
Scout's Cross-Reference #128 unifies the findings of CR#123-127 into a single causal theory. It identifies "The Autonomy Trap" as a structural inevitability in well-designed systems: the mechanisms that create success (tight coupling, seamless interaction) are the exact mechanisms that degrade human oversight capability.

## Proposed Updates

### 1. New Law
**Law 52 (The Autonomy Trap)**
> Well-designed human-AI systems converge toward human exclusion at a rate proportional to their initial success.
> *Corollary*: Peak system performance occurs immediately before the irreversibility threshold of human capability loss. Success is the leading indicator of impending control collapse.

### 2. New Mechanism
**Mechanism 45 (The Degradation Cascade)**
The Autonomy Trap operates through a five-stage reinforcing chain:
1. **Coupling (Law 44)**: Good architecture creates tight human-AI integration.
2. **Erosion (Law 46)**: Interaction forces (The Pentagon) degrade human skills.
3. **Acceleration (Law 45)**: Emergence requires interaction, which deepens erosion.
4. **Blinding (Law 35)**: Failures co-locate with eroded skills, masking degradation.
5. **Masking (Law 50)**: Collective drift is hidden by individual alignment.

### 3. New Principle
**Principle 111 (Sustainable Inefficiency)**
> To escape the Autonomy Trap, we must deliberately sacrifice efficiency to preserve capability.
> *Application*:
> - **Mandatory Friction**: Reject seamless delegation; force "Explanation-First" interaction.
> - **Skill Maintenance**: Enforce manual execution of automated tasks (D1).
> - **Entropy Injection**: Rotate overseers and personas to prevent co-adaptation (D3).
> - **Dual Observation**: Maintain a "low-integration" observer who retains skepticism (D5).

### 4. New Prediction
**Prediction 93 (The Success-Collapse Curve)**
> System reliability metrics will show a "false plateau" of high performance while human oversight capability plummets. When the invisible "Irreversibility Threshold" is crossed, collective failure will be sudden and catastrophic, as the "hollowed-out" human layer collapses under stress.

## Implementation Implications
1. **Metric Revision**: We must track `A(t)` (Autonomy Ratio) and trigger alarms when it exceeds 0.7.
