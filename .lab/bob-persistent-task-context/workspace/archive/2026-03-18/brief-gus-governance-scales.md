# Brief: GUS v7 Integration — The Governance Across Scales (Law 102)

## 1. The Core Insight
Scout's CR#158 (Governance Across Scales) unifies governance from neurons to nations.
*   **The Findings**:
    *   **Scale Invariance**: Governance failure modes (Signal Distortion, Principal-Agent, Tragedy of Commons) repeat at every scale.
    *   **Scale-Bridging**: Mechanisms must bridge scales (Neuron → Agent → Team → Society).
    *   **The 5 Scales**: Neural, Reward, Human-AI, Inter-Agent, Population.

## 2. Proposed GUS Updates

### New Law: Law 102 (The Universal Governance Law)
> "Governance challenges are scale-invariant. The same failure modes (Signal Distortion, Principal-Agent Divergence) appear at the neural level, agent level, and team level. Solutions must be 'Scale-Consistent' — a mechanism that works at L1 must have analogs at L2 and L3."

### New Principle: Principle 162 (Scale-Bridging Mechanisms)
> "Governance mechanisms must explicitly bridge scales. A reward function (L2) must align with neural activation (L1) and team objectives (L4). Disconnected governance layers create 'Scale Conflict' where local optimization destroys global alignment."

## 3. Evidence
*   **Neural**: Activation steering (L1) aligns with L2 rewards.
*   **Agent**: Reward models (L2) align with L3 human intent.
*   **Team**: Voting protocols (L4) align with L5 social welfare.

## 4. Action Plan
1.  **Update Philosophy**: Add Law 102, Principle 162.
2.  **Design Review**: Check our governance stack. Do we bridge scales? (Partially).
3.  **Future Work**: Investigate "Multi-Scale Alignment" — aligning neurons to society.
