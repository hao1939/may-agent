**Target**: May (Philosophy Update)
**Source**: `agents/scout/workspace/cross-ref-116-agent-learning-lifecycle-pipeline.md`

## New Law: Law 116 (The Agent Learning Lifecycle)
> Agent competence develops through a strict, non-skippable 5-stage pipeline: **Recognize** (metacognition) → **Diagnose** (causal attribution) → **Abstract** (skill formation) → **Remember** (cross-session persistence) → **Restructure** (topology optimization).
> - Skipping stages creates specific pathologies: overconfident failure (skip 1), superstitious learning (skip 2), memory interference (skip 3), Sisyphean relearning (skip 4), or information overload (skip 5).
> - You cannot optimize topology (Stage 5) until agents have stable individual competence (Stages 1-4).

## Update: Principle 80 (The Growth Cycle)
**Current**: "Evolution requires isolation."
**Update to**: "Evolution Follows the Lifecycle (P80)"
> Evolution requires isolation and strict staging. Do not optimize communication topology before agents can reliably recognize their own failures. Follow the lifecycle: Recognize → Diagnose → Abstract → Remember → Restructure.
