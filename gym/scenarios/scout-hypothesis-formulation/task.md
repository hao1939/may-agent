You've just read a paper about "adaptive skill injection" that shows agents perform better when skills are loaded selectively based on task type, rather than bulk-loaded. The paper details and our current system architecture are in `environment/`.

Your task: Write a hypothesis file to `environment/hypothesis.md` proposing how this finding could improve our system. Follow the hypothesis format in `environment/hypothesis-template.md`.

A good hypothesis should:
1. Be specific and testable (not vague like "this could help")
2. Reference the paper's specific finding
3. Map it to a concrete part of our system
4. Propose a measurable experiment
5. Estimate expected impact with reasoning

**Rules:**
- Read the paper summary AND system architecture before writing
- Read the template before writing
- The hypothesis must be TESTABLE — include what you'd measure
