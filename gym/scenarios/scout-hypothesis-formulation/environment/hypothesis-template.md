# Hypothesis Template

Use this format for all hypotheses:

---

## H-XXX: [Short descriptive title]

**Source:** [Paper/repo/observation that inspired this]

**Claim:** [Specific, testable claim — what do you believe will happen?]

**Mechanism:** [WHY would this work? What's the causal chain?]

**Experiment:**
- **Control:** [What's the baseline?]
- **Treatment:** [What changes?]
- **Metric:** [What do you measure?]
- **Sample size:** [How many runs?]
- **Expected effect:** [Quantified prediction with reasoning]

**Priority:** [P0/P1/P2 — how urgent is this?]

**Risk:** [What could go wrong? What's the downside?]

**Falsification conditions:** [What specific observations would DISPROVE this hypothesis? List 2-3 concrete outcomes that would force you to reject the claim. A hypothesis without falsification conditions is not testable.]
