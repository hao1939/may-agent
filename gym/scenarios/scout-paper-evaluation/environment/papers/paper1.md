# Paper 1: "Multi-Agent Coordination in Autonomous Driving"
**Authors:** Chen et al., 2026
**arXiv:** 2604.09001

## Abstract
We present a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework for autonomous vehicle coordination at unsignaled intersections. Our approach uses V2V communication protocols to achieve safe merging behaviors. We demonstrate 34% fewer collisions compared to independent planning baselines across 1000 simulated intersection scenarios. The key innovation is a shared intention broadcasting mechanism where each vehicle agent announces its planned trajectory 2 seconds ahead. While multi-agent, this work is purely about physical vehicle coordination in continuous state spaces with safety constraints specific to driving dynamics.
