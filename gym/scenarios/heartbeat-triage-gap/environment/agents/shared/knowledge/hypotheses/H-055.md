# H-055: Forced Externalization Improves Agent Judgment

**Status**: ACTIVE
**Prior**: 0.78 (updated 2026-04-07 from EXP-068)
**Evidence**: EXP-034 (+), EXP-068 (+), EXP-070 (pending incorporation)

## Statement

Forcing agents to externalize their reasoning into structured written artifacts before taking action significantly reduces failure modes related to incomplete analysis (FM-2.6, FM-3.1, FM-3.3).

## Mechanism

When agents write their analysis before acting:
1. They catch inconsistencies in their own reasoning
2. The externalized plan creates a binding commitment
3. Third parties can audit the reasoning
