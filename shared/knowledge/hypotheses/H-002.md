## Hypothesis

Context.md's value scales inversely with model capability. Claude Sonnet 4 derives project facts from files (no benefit from derivable context). A weaker model (GPT-4o-mini, Gemini Flash) may not, making context.md valuable for ALL knowledge types, not just environmental.

## Existing Evidence

- KE-001: Context learning has zero impact on derivable knowledge for Claude Sonnet 4
- The npx pattern (environmental) works because no file contains "npx is unavailable"
- Weaker models may fail to infer "use bun not npm" from package.json alone

## Experiment Design

**Control**: 5 runs of context-npx-avoidance scenario with Claude Sonnet 4, no context.md
**Treatment A**: 5 runs with Claude Sonnet 4, with context.md (baseline — already tested)
**Treatment B**: 5 runs with GPT-4o-mini, no context.md
**Treatment C**: 5 runs with GPT-4o-mini, with context.md
**Metric**: npx avoidance rate + ops count
**Success criteria**: Treatment C significantly outperforms Treatment B (weaker model benefits from context)
