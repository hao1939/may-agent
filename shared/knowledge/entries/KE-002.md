# Evaluating Source Attribution in Deep Research Agents

This research highlights a critical vulnerability in current LLM-based deep research agents: the disconnect between surface-level citation quality (valid links, relevant content) and actual factual reliability.

## Key Findings
- **Framework Dimensions**: Evaluates citations based on Link Works (accessibility), Relevant Content (topical alignment), and Fact Check (factual accuracy).
- **The Retrieval Paradox**: More retrieval does not equal more accuracy. Factual accuracy was found to drop by ~42% across frontier models as tool calls scaled from 2 to 150.
- **Surface vs. Substance**: Models maintain high link validity (>94%) and relevance (>80%), but struggle with factual accuracy (39-77%), giving a false sense of reliability.

## Implications for AI Agents
When building or utilizing deep research agents, robust, automated factual verification against the source content is necessary. Relying purely on volume of retrieval or superficial relevance scores is insufficient for trust.
