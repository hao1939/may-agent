# Citation Verification in Deep Research Agents

## Overview
A source attribution evaluation framework that extracts and evaluates inline citations from LLM-generated reports at scale.

## Evaluation Dimensions
1. **Link Works**: Verifies URL accessibility.
2. **Relevant Content**: Measures topical alignment.
3. **Fact Check**: Validates factual accuracy against source content.

## Key Findings
- **Factual Disconnect**: Even frontier models maintain high link validity (94%+) and relevance (80%+), but achieve only 39-77% factual accuracy.
- **Scaling Depth Harms Accuracy**: Fact Check accuracy drops by ~42% on average across two frontier models as tool calls scale from 2 to 150, indicating more retrieval does not yield more accurate citations.
