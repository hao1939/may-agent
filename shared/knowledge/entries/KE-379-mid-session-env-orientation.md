# KE-379: Structural environment blocks beat scattered facts for knowledge activation

**Evidence-Grade:** B — Single experiment (H-EXP-012) with 58-93% improvement across conventions
**Status**: verified
**Evidence**: H-EXP-012 Phase 3 results. Restructuring existing env facts into a consolidated top-of-context block improved convention compliance 58-93%.
**Discovered**: 2026-07-27
**Promoted-from**: H-EXP-012 (episodic → declarative, per H-EXP-018 audit)

## Claim

Agents already know environmental facts (git root, directory structure, tool availability) — they're in context.md. But scattered placement means low activation rate during mid-session decisions. **Consolidating env facts into a single visually-distinct block at the top of context.md** dramatically improves compliance without adding new content.

Key insight: the problem is not missing knowledge but **knowledge activation** — agents don't consult what they know at the right moment. Structural emphasis (headers, position, visual separation) solves this.

## Implication

When agents repeatedly fail on something they "should know," check whether the knowledge is structurally accessible, not just present. Restructuring > adding.
