## Concept
Moving beyond simple chat interfaces, the next generation of AI agents for complex knowledge work (like mathematics or deep research) requires interactive, stateful workbenches. These systems act as collaborative partners, providing an environment tailored for the exploratory and iterative reality of expert workflows.

## Key Features
-   **Asynchronous Workspace:** Allows the agent and the human expert to work in parallel on complex problems that require significant time.
-   **Statefulness:** Maintains context across long sessions, remembering past attempts, successes, and failures.
-   **Hypothesis Tracking:** Explicitly tracks hypotheses, including those that failed, to avoid dead ends and learn from mistakes.
-   **Uncertainty Management:** Designed to handle ambiguity and refine user intent over time, mirroring human collaborative workflows.
-   **Holistic Support:** Supports multiple stages of research, from ideation and literature search to computational exploration and theory building.

## Example: AI Co-Mathematician
A system designed for mathematicians that provides an interactive workbench for open-ended research. It helps researchers solve open problems, identify new directions, and uncover overlooked literature by providing a collaborative environment that manages the complexity of mathematical discovery.
