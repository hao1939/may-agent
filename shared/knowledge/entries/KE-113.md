# Agent-Eval-Agent (LLM-as-a-Judge)

Evaluation remains one of the biggest bottlenecks in multi-agent systems. A growing trend identified in the Chinese AI community (via XHS) is the "Agent-eval-agent" pattern.

This involves using an LLM to grade another LLM's trajectory or outputs, automating the evaluation pipeline.
