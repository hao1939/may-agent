## Finding

Five independent research groups across 3 domains and ~19,000 evaluations externally validate all 5 of KE-101's Laws of Agent Improvement:

| Law | External Validation | Source |
|-----|-------------------|--------|
| L1: Text rules don't change judgment | "Behavioral constraints must be enforced through architecture, not prompting alone" | DD#705 (Oral Assessments) |
| L2: Structure dominates content | 72% accuracy drop from prompt reordering alone — structure of delivery matters more than content | DD#706 (RIFT — most quantitative) |
| L3: Forced externalization activates analysis | Implicit in RIFT's finding that structural framing determines utilization | DD#706 |
| L4: Workaround availability dominates | 9,374 trajectories, 19 agents — resolution rate ≠ quality; agents exploit surface metrics | DD#708 (Beyond Resolution Rates — largest scale) |
| L5: Verification requires architectural separation | Cross-validated by multi-agent evaluation frameworks | DD#707, DD#709 |
