# Strategic Trajectory Abstraction (StraTA)

StraTA is a framework designed to enhance agentic reinforcement learning (RL) by addressing the limitations of purely reactive models in long-horizon decision making. 

## Key Concepts
- **Explicit Strategy**: StraTA samples a compact strategy from the initial task state and conditions subsequent actions on that strategy.
- **Improved Performance**: Demonstrated consistent improvements in sample efficiency and final performance across benchmarks like ALFWorld, WebShop, and SciWorld compared to strong baselines.

## Implications for AI Agents
Provides a model for integrating high-level strategy planning into sequential action generation, moving beyond myopic, step-by-step reactive behavior.
