# AI Co-Mathematician

The AI co-mathematician is a workbench for mathematicians to interactively leverage AI agents for open-ended research (ideation, literature search, computational exploration, theorem proving, theory building).

Key concepts:
- **Asynchronous, stateful workspace**: Manages uncertainty, refines user intent, tracks failed hypotheses.
- **Native artifacts**: Outputs native mathematical artifacts.
- **Interactive paradigm**: Mirrors human collaborative workflows rather than just solving isolated problems.
