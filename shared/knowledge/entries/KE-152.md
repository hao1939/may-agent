# StraTA: Strategic Trajectory Abstraction

## Overview
Optimizing LLMs for long-horizon decision making is challenging as current methods are largely reactive. StraTA introduces an explicit trajectory-level strategy into agentic reinforcement learning.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Strategy Sampling**: Samples a compact strategy from the initial task state.
- **Conditioned Actions**: Conditions subsequent actions on that strategy.
- **Joint Training**: Trains strategy generation and action execution jointly using a hierarchical GRPO-style rollout, enhanced by diverse strategy rollout and critical self-judgment.

## Results
Improves sample efficiency and final performance over baselines (e.g., 93.1% on ALFWorld, 84.2% on WebShop).
