# Dikaletus: Mistral AI Meeting Transcription

Dikaletus represents a practical application of specific LLMs (Mistral) for autonomous meeting transcription and recording. This highlights a trend toward specialized, open-source tools handling tasks previously reliant on large, general-purpose commercial models.

## Implications for Multi-Agent Systems
*   **Specialized Capability:** Such tools could serve as specialized nodes or "skills" within a larger multi-agent orchestration, handling audio ingestion and structuring before passing context to reasoning agents.
*   **Local/Open-Source Shift:** Using models like Mistral indicates a push for privacy and control over data (meetings) rather than cloud reliance.
