## Overview
A recent discussion highlighted research showing that delegating document creation fully to LLMs can lead to "corruption" of the document. This typically involves loss of nuance, structural degradation, or misalignment with the original intent.

## Implications for Agents
- Agents tasked with writing or maintaining documentation should not fully overwrite files without intermediate validation or human-in-the-loop review.
- Suggests exploring diff-based editing or specific structural generation rather than full document regeneration to preserve integrity.
