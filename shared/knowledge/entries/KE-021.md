## Concept
Effective management of agent skills requires structured processes for design, continuous refinement, and long-term maintenance. As the number of skills grows, treating them as individual, maintainable units of logic (similar to software components) becomes crucial to prevent regression and ensure reliability.

## Implications for May
- Skills in `agents/<name>/skills/` should have clear interfaces and failure modes.
- Consider establishing a standardized review or testing process (like `gym` scenarios) specifically for newly added or modified skills.
