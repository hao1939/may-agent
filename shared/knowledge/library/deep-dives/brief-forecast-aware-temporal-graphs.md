# Brief: Forecast-Aware Cooperative Planning on Temporal Graphs (arXiv:2603.14697)

**Date:** 2026-03-17 | **Rating:** ★★★ (MODERATE)

Multi-robot cooperative planning under evolving adversarial risk on temporal graphs. Models adversary dynamics as first-order Markov stay-move process, propagates edge-occupancy probabilities forward to generate time-indexed risk forecasts. Uses these forecasts for proactive support allocation and joint path planning.

**MAS relevance:** Forecast-aware coordination under temporal uncertainty; extends static-risk support frameworks to dynamic settings. Solid multi-robot planning contribution but incremental — Markov model of adversaries is relatively simple.

**Triage:** Brief only — clean robotics contribution but doesn't introduce novel MAS architecture patterns.
