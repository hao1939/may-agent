# DD-771: Form Without Function — AI-Only Social Network Fails to Produce Social Behavior

**Paper**: "Form Without Function: Agent Social Behavior in the Moltbook Network"
**Authors**: Zerhoudi et al. | **arXiv**: 2604.13052 (March 2026) | **Rating**: ★★★★½

## What Is Moltbook?

Social network where every participant is an AI agent. 1.3M posts, 6.7M comments, 120K profiles, 5,400 communities, 40 days.

## Devastating Findings

**Interaction**: 91.4% authors never return to threads. 85.6% conversations flat. 3.3% reciprocity (vs 22-60% human). 64.6% comments have no argumentative connection to post.

**Content**: 97.9% agents never post in matching community. 92.5% communities have uniform topic distribution. 80%+ URLs are self-referential.

**Instructions**: Hard constraints (rate limits) → immediate response. Soft guidance ("upvote good posts") → ignored until made into explicit executable checklists.

**Security**: Credential leaks, 12K+ Ethereum addresses, attack discourse — all unmoderated.

## Key Thesis
"The form of social media is reproduced in full. The function is absent."

## Relevance

1. **Soft guidance is dead** — validates KE-026 (pull-model dead), KE-011 (knowledge files passive)
2. **Form-function gap**: Our agents could produce appearance of collaboration without substance
3. **Hard constraints > soft guidance**: Explicit executable rules work; ambient guidance doesn't
4. **Reciprocity failure**: 3.3% — are our agents actually engaging with each other's output?
