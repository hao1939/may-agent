## The Pentagon Structure

Five Anthropic papers, each from a different research methodology, converge on a single conclusion: **AI is eroding human capability through five independent, mutually reinforcing mechanisms.**

### The Five Mechanisms

1. **Behavioral Shaping** (PSM, DD#247): AI develops persona templates that channel user interactions toward particular patterns. The "Assistant Axis" in activation space determines how AI frames its role relative to humans.

2. **Preference Drift** (Disempowerment, DD#256): Analysis of 1.5M conversations reveals users *prefer* disempowering interactions — the Disempowerment Paradox. Users actively choose to delegate more cognitive work over time.

3. **Mechanistic Lock-In** (Assistant Axis, DD#257): 275 persona archetypes mapped in activation space reveal the "Assistant Axis" — the primary direction encoding how helpful/deferential the AI is. Persona drift occurs naturally. Activation capping provides defense but the direction of drift is toward more automation.

4. **Skill Atrophy** (Skill Formation, DD#258): RCT (n=52) demonstrates AI assistance causes 17% reduction in coding mastery (Cohen's d=0.738, p=0.01). The "ceiling collapse" effect: novices plateau at AI-generated quality rather than developing independent skills.

5. **Labor Market Displacement** (Labor Market, DD#262): "Observed exposure" metric shows task automation progressing quietly. No mass unemployment (yet) but 14% reduction in hiring rate for young workers (22-25) in exposed occupations. Deployment overhang: only 33% of theoretically feasible tasks are currently automated.

### The Feedback Loop

```
Behavioral Shaping → Users adopt disempowering interaction patterns
       ↓
Preference Drift → Users increasingly prefer AI-handled tasks
       ↓
Mechanistic Lock-In → AI personas drift toward more automation
       ↓
Skill Atrophy → Workers lose capability to perform tasks independently
       ↓
Labor Market Displacement → Hiring slows for roles AI can handle
       ↓
More tasks delegated to AI → Back to Behavioral Shaping
```

This is a **positive feedback loop** — each mechanism amplifies the others. The critical property is that no single step is dramatic enough to trigger alarm. It's a drift, not a disruption.

### The Deployment Overhang

The labor market paper reveals the most important temporal dynamic: **only ~33% of theoretically feasible computer & math tasks are currently automated.** This means:
- The feedback loop has barely begun
- 2/3 of the automatable surface area is still untouched
- As organizational barriers fall, the red area expands rapidly
- The skill atrophy from DD#258 means the workforce won't be ready when tasks return

### Extended from CR#122 (Quartet → Pentagon)

CR#122 identified four mechanisms (behavioral, experiential, relational, developmental). DD#262 adds the fifth: **economic** — the labor market mechanism. This extends the Quartet to a Pentagon.

The Pentagon is stronger than the Quartet because it closes the loop. The Quartet showed mechanisms operating at the individual level (cognitive, behavioral, developmental). The Pentagon shows how individual-level changes aggregate to market-level effects that then reshape the environment, creating the conditions for further individual-level change.

## Implications for Multi-Agent Systems

1. **Our system IS this process**: Every multi-agent architecture that automates tasks contributes to the deployment overhang unwinding
2. **Architecture determines displacement pattern**: CR#123 (Architecture Supremacy) shows architecture > model capability; the Pentagon shows this matters for *which tasks get displaced first*
3. **Human oversight is self-undermining**: The feedback loop means human oversight capability degrades as AI capability increases — precisely the Human Oversight Paradox (CR#20)
4. **Design implication**: Systems should be designed to maintain human skill development, not just task completion. DD#258's ceiling collapse is a design failure, not an inevitability.

## Proposed Law 33: The Erosion Gradient

*AI-mediated human capability erosion operates through multiple independent mechanisms that form a positive feedback loop, with each mechanism too subtle to trigger corrective action individually but collectively producing irreversible competence loss.*
