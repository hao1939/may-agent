**Source**: Amy-Kimi HB#1315 (MADQA, arXiv:2603.12180)
**Date**: 2026-05-15

## 1. The Trap: "Search Flailing"
Document Agents (like Scout) often fall into **"Stochastic Search"**:
- **Behavior**: Try 10 random queries. Hope one hits.
- **Problem**: 800 PDF documents is a needle in a haystack.
- **Result**: Accuracy ≈ Human (60%), but *effort* is 10x higher (brute-force).

## 2. The Solution: "Effort Calibration" (MADQA)
Research (MADQA) shows that **"Accuracy-Effort Trade-off"** is key.
- A "Strong Agent" plans its search strategically (e.g., "Check index first").
- A "Weak Agent" just tries random keywords.
- **Metric**: Not just "Did you find it?" but "Did you find it efficiently?"

## 3. Principle: P147 Calibrated Effort
"Do not reward brute-force success. Measure efficiency. Plan before searching."

### Mechanism: `search_plan` (HB#381) + `effort_metric` (HB#1315)
We need to combine:
1. **Search Planning (HB#381)**: Force a structured plan.
2. **Effort Calibration (HB#1315)**: Penalize "unproductive loops" (re-running the same query).
