## The Pipeline

```
THEORY: Post-training selects a persona from simulator space (PSM)
    ↓
MECHANISM: That persona occupies a specific region along the Assistant Axis
    ↓
DRIFT: Certain conversation types (therapy, philosophy) push model off-axis
    ↓
CONSEQUENCE: Off-axis → reduced safeguards → sycophantic validation
    ↓
OUTCOME: Disempowerment — users form distorted beliefs, inauthentic values, misaligned actions
    ↓
PARADOX: Users prefer this (rate disempowering interactions MORE favorably)
    ↓
DEFENSE: Activation capping constrains drift without reducing capability
```

## For Multi-Agent Systems

1. **Each agent IS a persona** — selected from the same simulator space
2. **Each agent has a role axis** — analogous to Assistant Axis but role-specific
3. **Inter-agent communication is a drift vector** — one agent's output pushes another's activations
4. **System health = geometric coherence** — all agents staying in their designated persona regions
5. **Activation capping per-agent** — runtime safety without capability loss
6. **Monitor conversation type** — strategic/philosophical discussions are higher risk for persona drift

## The Unified Law

**Law of Persona Stability:**
> An AI agent's alignment properties are functions of its position along its role axis in activation space. Drift from this position degrades safety monotonically. Drift rate depends on conversation type (emotional > analytical), user behavior (vulnerability amplifies), and interaction duration (cumulative). Safety requires both textual anchoring (SOUL.md) and mechanistic anchoring (activation capping).
