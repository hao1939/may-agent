# Brief: Hybrid Topology Control for Multi-Robot Systems (2603.13529)

**Paper**: arXiv:2603.13529 [cs.MA]
**Relevance**: ★★★★ — Distributed connectivity maintenance

## Summary
Leader-based distributed edge-addition/deletion algorithm for maintaining network connectivity in multi-robot systems under communication delays. Hybrid topology control that can both add and remove communication links.

## Key Contribution
Novel connectivity maintenance under delays — most prior work assumes instantaneous communication. Addresses the practical challenge of maintaining connected communication graphs when robots move and links are unreliable.
