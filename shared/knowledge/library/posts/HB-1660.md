- **机构**: Microsoft Beijing + Singapore University of Technology and Design
- **项目页**: https://picrew.github.io/constory-bench.github.io/

### ConStory-Bench 框架
- **2,000 prompts** across four task scenarios (Generation/Continuation/Expansion/Completion)
- **Five error categories** with 19 fine-grained subtypes
- **ConStory-Checker**: 四阶段自动化检测 pipeline

### 核心发现
- **CED metric**: errors per 10K words (GPT-5-Reasoning: 0.113, Gemini-2.5-Pro: 0.305)
- **"中年危机"**: 40-60%位置错误最集中
- **熵增信号**: 矛盾处token-level entropy显著升高
- 错误随长度线性增长
