## Summary

谷歌论文《Distilling step-by-step: Outperforming larger language models with less training data and smaller model sizes》的核心思想：

### Key Mechanism
- **逐步蒸馏 (Step-by-step Distillation)**: 将传统一次性知识蒸馏拆成多个阶段
- 每个阶段引入中等大小的模型作为过渡 teacher
- 最终得到目标小型学生模型

### Results
- T5-Base 在 SuperGLUE、SQuAD 等基准上比直接蒸馏的同等大小模型高出 **2-3 个百分点**
- 某些任务上能接近或超过原来的 T5-XXL 老师模型
- 中间层能够有效降噪，让小模型学到更纯净的知识

### Practical Application
- 先用略大的中间模型做第一次蒸馏
- 再用中间模型作为新 teacher 训练最终小模型
- 不需要额外标注，能提升推理速度并降低显存占用
- 适用于移动端、边缘设备、快速迭代的内部工具

### Insights
- 作者认为这相当于逐步降低老师输出的噪声水平
- 中间模型起到"提纯"作用
- 类似人类先理解大框架再逐步细化的学习过程

### Limitations
- 训练复杂度增加
- 中间模型大小选择敏感
- 未来可与量化、剪枝等技术结合

## Relevance to OpenClaw

**High relevance** — our agent system already uses a tiered model approach (Opus for architect, Gemini for design, Kimi for cost-efficient tasks). This paper validates multi-stage knowledge transfer:

- Could apply to skill distillation: expert-level skills → intermediate generalist → lightweight edge-deployable skills
- The "noise reduction through intermediate layers" concept maps well to our skill refinement pipeline
- Validates that smaller, purpose-tuned models can outperform raw large models for specific tasks
