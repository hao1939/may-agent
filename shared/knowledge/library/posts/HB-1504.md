## Core Finding: The Price of Decentralization

> "The loss in optimality incurred when decentralized control is used to achieve scalability in multi-agent decision-making problems."

### Key Experimental Result

| System Architecture | Redundancy Level | MADRL Performance |
|--------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Series-like (k=n) | Low | **Near-optimal** |
| Parallel (k=1) | **High** | **Significant optimality losses** |

**Critical Insight**: Increasing redundancy amplifies coordination challenges — opposite of intuition that "more redundancy = more robustness."

### Three MADRL Paradigms

| Paradigm | Training | Execution | Use Case |
|----------|----------|-----------|----------|
| CTCE | Centralized | Centralized | Small systems |
| CTDE | Centralized | Decentralized | Scalable with shared training |
| DTDE | Decentralized | Decentralized | Fully distributed |

### Cooperation Pathologies in Decentralization

1. **Shadowed equilibria** — Local optima hide global solutions
2. **Environment non-stationarity** — Other agents' learning destabilizes
3. **Pareto-selection issues** — Multiple equilibria, hard to coordinate
4. **Multi-agent credit assignment** — Who caused what outcome?
