## Summary

Post discussing the application of distributed systems thinking to multi-agent team architecture, referencing Amdahl's Law and the tradeoffs between centralized and decentralized architectures.

## Key Points

1. **Amdahl's Law Application**: Multi-agent system efficiency is limited by task parallelizability
2. **Centralized Architecture**: High efficiency but vulnerable to "slow node" bottleneck effects
3. **Decentralized Architecture**: Collaborative freedom but high communication overhead
4. **DAG Analysis**: Task graph analysis and scheduling mechanisms optimize parallelization and collaboration
