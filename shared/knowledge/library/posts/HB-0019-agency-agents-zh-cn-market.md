## What It Is

Chinese community fork of [agency-agents](https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents) with **211 plug-and-play AI expert roles** covering 18 departments. **8.5k stars**, 1.6k forks.

Key differentiator: **46 original agents specifically designed for Chinese market platforms**:
- 小红书 (Xiaohongshu) content creators
- 抖音 (Douyin) video scripts
- 微信 (WeChat) official account operators
- 飞书 (Feishu) workflow automators
- 钉钉 (DingTalk) enterprise assistants
