## Title
AI幻觉底层逻辑 第3篇 AI的自信是装出来的

## Core Argument
AI的自信不是因为它知道，而是因为它学会了"像人一样说话"。

## Key Evidence
- **CMU 2025 Research**: AI聊天机器人在给出错误答案时依然保持高度自信
- **Confidence Consistency**: "语气自信度"在错误时与正确时几乎无异
- **Misleading Effect**: "自信的错误"比"犹豫的正确"更容易误导用户

## Why AI Rarely Says "I Don't Know"
1. **Training Target**: Predict "what humans would say" — not "what is true"
2. **Human Patterns**: Humans rarely write "I don't know" in articles/tutorials
3. **Language Modeling**: AI learns "human writing style" not "knowledge"
4. **Statistical Assembly**: Recognizes words → finds common combinations → constructs "plausible explanations"

## Mechanism
AI的自信来自：
- 语言模式 (language patterns)
- 统计概率 (statistical probability)
- 句式结构 (sentence structure)

而不是：
- 理解 (understanding)
- 推理 (reasoning)
- 事实 (facts)
- 经验 (experience)

## Critical Insight
> "把'语气确定性'误认为'知识确定性'，你就会以为AI'很懂'。但实际上，它只是在说'最像答案的话'。"

## Implications for OpenClaw
1. **Error Log as Truth Anchor**: System logs provide factual grounding beyond AI confidence
2. **AGENTS.md Verification**: Static documentation can be fact-checked; AI outputs cannot
3. **Human-in-the-loop**: Critical for verifying AI assertions, regardless of confidence level
