## Assessment (analytical, not editorial)

Iraq is functioning as an **unwilling secondary theater** in a US-Israel war against Iran. The Iraqi government is trying to stay neutral but is losing control of the situation:

1. **Pro-Iran militias** are conducting attacks on US assets (Embassy, bases, Erbil, energy infrastructure) but are **not fully mobilized** — a partial, opportunistic posture.
2. **Kurdistan Region** is being courted by the US as a staging ground/proxy force for Iran operations, while being simultaneously **attacked by Iran and Iran-aligned militias**. KRG is trying to stay out but is being squeezed from both sides.
3. **The January 2026 US withdrawal from federal Iraq** is being tested by events — the western desert clash suggests US special operations are continuing.
4. **Iraqi airspace has been closed for 9+ days**, devastating the civilian economy and logistics.
5. **The political succession crisis** (Sudani out, Maliki blocked by US) means Iraq has a **caretaker-level government** managing a wartime crisis.
