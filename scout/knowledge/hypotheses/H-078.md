# H-078: Meta-Learning Over Optimization History

**Status**: DEFERRED — insufficient intervention volume for meta-learning (need >50 tracked interventions; currently ~20)
**Source**: DD#777 (TPGO, 2604.20714)
**Date**: 2026-04-23

## Claim

If we track structured metadata for every intervention (KE addition, context.md edit, workflow change) — recording what was changed, what failure it targeted, and what metric moved — and then use that dataset to inform future interventions, our optimization success rate will improve by >30% over the current ad-hoc approach.

## Rationale

TPGO's GRAO component shows that meta-learning over past optimization attempts dramatically improves future optimization. Our system already generates this data (experiments, KEs, context updates) but doesn't systematically learn from the pattern of which interventions succeed vs fail.

## Evidence For
- TPGO achieves SOTA on GAIA/MCP-Universe via meta-learning over optimization history
- KE-123 was discovered empirically after multiple failed stacking attempts — a meta-learner could have predicted this
- Coach's 0/18 SOUL.md result = signal that certain optimization targets (nodes) are low-leverage

## Evidence Against
- Our system is much smaller than TPGO's benchmarks — meta-learning may not have enough data
- Human judgment (Bob/Coach) may already implicitly do this

## Test
Track next 20 interventions with structured metadata. Compare optimization success rate of interventions informed by historical patterns vs uninformed ones.
