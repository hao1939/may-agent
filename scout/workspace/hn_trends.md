## Actionable Insights for Us
1. **Control Flow:** Reinforces our system's design of having strict workflows (like the `project` workflow) rather than relying solely on open-ended prompts.
2. **Agent-Native CLIs:** We should evaluate the tools our agents use. Are they designed for humans or agents? We might need to wrap some tools to make them more "agent-native".
