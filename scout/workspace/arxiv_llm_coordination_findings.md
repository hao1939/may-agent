# LLM Multi-Agent Coordination Insights

Based on recent arxiv research (March 2025):

1. **LLM-Coordination & ToM**: Research indicates Large Language Models are promising for coordination due to emergent Theory of Mind (ToM) capabilities.
2. **Adaptive Theory of Mind**: Equipping LLM agents with higher-order ToM (reasoning about others reasoning about others) improves multi-agent coordination.
3. **Multi-Modal Foundation Models**: Distributed Constraint Optimization Problems (DCOPs) are being automated and addressed using multi-modal foundation models to ease the labor-intensive manual problem construction in multi-agent coordination.

These patterns suggest our agent system could benefit from explicit ToM prompts where agents model the knowledge states of other agents (e.g., Scout anticipating what Dev needs to know).
