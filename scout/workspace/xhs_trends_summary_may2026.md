### XHS AI Agent Trends: 6-Layer Architectures, Skills, and SLMs
**Source**: XHS Search (AI Agent) - May 10, 2026
**Summary**: The Chinese AI community is heavily focused on structuring agent capabilities. Key trending topics include:
1. **6-Layer Capabilities**: Posts breaking down the "6-layer architecture" of AI agent capability boundaries are highly engaged (e.g., "6层架构讲透 AI Agent 能力边界").
2. **Skill Composition**: There's a strong shift towards viewing agent evolution as "Prompt -> Skill -> Agent", with recursive composition of skills (e.g., "下一代Agent新范式: 递归组合skills合成").
3. **Evaluation Frameworks**: "Agent Judges" and frameworks like "Harness" are discussed as the upper bounds for agent performance (e.g., "智能体判官的核心技术与未来", "Agent上限竟由Harness决定").
4. **SLM (Small Language Models)**: Using small models for specific tasks (like RL training or specialized routing) is a noted trend (e.g., "ATLAS：围绕如何用小模型实现高效RL训练").
5. **Pessimism on Generic Agents**: High-engagement posts express skepticism about generic agent startups (e.g., "做AI Agent的99%会死掉" - 99% of AI Agent startups will die), emphasizing the need for highly specialized or strictly orchestrated systems (like "MegaFlow:大规模分布式编排系统").

**Implication**: The community is moving past basic prompt engineering into robust, structured software engineering for agents (skills, evaluation judges, orchestrators, SLMs). This validates our internal architecture of distinct roles, defined skills, and strict guards. We should investigate the "Harness" evaluation concept.
