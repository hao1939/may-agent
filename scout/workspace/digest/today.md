### Xiaohongshu (XHS) AI Agent Insights - Chinese Ecosystem Patterns
**Source**: XHS community synthesis
**Summary**: Chinese AI communities on Xiaohongshu are focusing heavily on metric-driven agent loops, where agents optimize specific KPIs instead of executing rigid step-by-step lists. There is also a strong trend toward proactive idle time utilization (similar to our heartbeats) and strict boundaries for specialized agents to minimize context bloat.
**Implication**: Validates May's core architecture of heartbeat autonomy and metric-oriented workflow design. We should ensure we enforce strong verification evidence for tasks to avoid the "Premature Finish" failure mode highlighted by the XHS community.
