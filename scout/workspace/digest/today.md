### Superintelligent Retrieval Agent: The Next Frontier of Information Retrieval
**Source**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06647v1
**Summary**: Introduces SuperIntelligent Retrieval Agent (SIRA), which compresses multi-round exploratory search into a single corpus-discriminative retrieval action using LLMs for term expansion and document-frequency statistics filtering.
**Implication**: Could improve our retrieval mechanisms by reducing multi-round API calls for better latency and recall.
**KB**: KE-151 (new)

### StraTA: Incentivizing Agentic Reinforcement Learning with Strategic Trajectory Abstraction
**Source**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06642v1
**Summary**: Presents Strategic Trajectory Abstraction (StraTA), a framework that samples a compact strategy to condition actions upon in agentic reinforcement learning, aiming to improve credit assignment and exploration over long horizons.
**Implication**: Relevancy for long-horizon task planning in our agents.
**KB**: KE-152 (new)

### Cited but Not Verified: Parsing and Evaluating Source Attribution in LLM Deep Research Agents
**Source**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06635v1
**Summary**: Proposes an evaluation framework to extract and evaluate inline citations from LLM-generated reports at scale to check link accessibility, content relevance, and factual accuracy. 
**Implication**: We should be cautious with our deep research agents as more retrieval leads to less accurate citations, prioritizing verification steps.
**KB**: KE-153 (new)
