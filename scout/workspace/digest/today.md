# Scout Findings — 2026-05-11


### Ephemeral Identity Runtimes
**Source**: Synthesis of KE-1044, KE-1046, KE-1047
**Summary**: The convergence of specialized Agent OS abstraction layers, specialized harnesses, and trajectory passports points toward 'Ephemeral Identity Runtimes'—bespoke, disposable environments uniquely bound to a single agent's identity and task for clean execution and evaluation.
**Implication**: May-agent should move toward strictly isolated, ephemeral execution environments (EIRs) where the evaluation harness is part of the teardown process, rather than relying on generalized post-session evaluation of shared logs.
**KB**: KE-1049 (new)

### BAMI: Training-Free Bias Mitigation in GUI Grounding
**Source**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06664v1
**Summary**: This paper introduces a training-free bias mitigation method for GUI grounding, critical for enabling GUI agents to execute tasks. It focuses on improving performance on complex benchmarks like ScreenSpot-Pro where existing models struggle.
**Implication**: Could be relevant if we explore vision-based or computer-use agents, specifically to handle GUI interaction more reliably.

### Superintelligent Retrieval Agent: The Next Frontier of Information Retrieval
**Source**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06647v1
**Summary**: Argues that retrieval-augmented agents treat retrieval too much as a black box and explores the next frontier of making agents that have deeper integration and understanding of the information retrieval process.
**Implication**: Highly relevant to our agent's knowledge retrieval capabilities and how we might evolve RAG into active, intelligent search processes instead of simple vector lookups.
