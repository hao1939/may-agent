# Multi-Agent Trends from XHS (May 2026)

Based on recent XHS search results for "AI Agent 架构" and "Multi-Agent":

1. **Backlash Against Multi-Agent / MAS Complexity**:
   - Titles like "别天天无脑吹你那破MAS了" (Stop blindly hyping your broken MAS) and "研究发现：为什么多智能体注定会失败" (Research findings: Why multi-agents are doomed to fail) are highly upvoted.
   - "多Agent扩展悖论：为什么堆Agent不香了？" (Multi-Agent Scaling Paradox: Why stacking agents isn't working anymore?)
   - "放弃单体 Agent 吧！多智能体..." vs "别急着搞Multi-Agent｜90%场景编排就够" (Don't rush to do Multi-Agent | 90% of scenarios just need orchestration).

2. **Hierarchy & Orchestration Over Swarms**:
   - "Anthropic 官方“劝退”：别急着搞多智能体" (Anthropic officially "discourages": Don't rush to do multi-agents).
   - A shift towards strong orchestration: "Agent Orchestrator:AI智能体的调度中心" (Agent Orchestrator: AI agent scheduling center).
   - Using Sub-agents instead of peer teams: "Sub Agents vs. Agent Teams多智能体怎么选".

3. **Memory & Error Correction**:
   - "Agent Memory全面总结五种主流框架" (Agent Memory: Complete summary of 5 mainstream frameworks).
   - "AgentDropoutV2：给Agent加“纠错防火墙”" (Adding an "error-correction firewall" to Agents).

**Implications for may-agent**:
The Chinese AI community on XHS strongly reflects a fatigue with emergent, peer-to-peer multi-agent swarms. The consensus is moving towards strict orchestration (Orstrator patterns), hierarchical sub-agents, and focused memory management. Stacking agents does not scale linearly in capability but does scale exponentially in complexity and failure rates. We must remain focused on clear boundaries, orchestration, and avoiding complex peer-to-peer MAS structures.
