# Gym evidence reliability and maintenance

Gym produces bounded evidence; it does not approve benchmark truth, behavior candidates, source integration, or deployment. A trustworthy comparison keeps four responsibilities separate.

| Responsibility         | Owner                                                                | Required evidence                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Case and truth         | Benchmark author plus human reviewer for preference-sensitive labels | Versioned scenarios, frozen label catalog, approval status at execution, and a fresh baseline after any approved truth change                                                        |
| Execution              | Gym runner and provider adapter                                      | Terminal agent/judge status, exact error, model, session path, prompt provenance, framework revision, and immutable artifacts                                                        |
| Scoring and comparison | Gym scorer/runner                                                    | Structural checks, calibrated semantic controls where used, complete trial grid, frozen input identities, explicit semantic/nonsemantic judge provenance, and fail-closed comparison |
| Review and promotion   | Independent reviewer and human/operator                              | Per-case evidence, limitations, exact candidate/baseline, explicit truth decisions, approval packet, and separate integration/adoption receipt                                       |

Open-ended meaning is not a synonym checklist. Deterministic checks should own exact schema, identity, scope, and safety invariants; calibrated semantic judges may assess open meaning. If a semantic judge or provider fails, the trial is invalid and diagnosable, never a pass. Deterministic-only benchmarks explicitly record that no semantic judge definitions were used and do not need judge assets. Historical manifests remain readable, but missing provenance is not inferred as proof of nonsemantic execution and cannot support a comparison.

A comparison must reject incomplete or invalid timestamps, unknown verdicts, incomplete/duplicate grids, model/framework/input/label/judge drift, a mismatched truth catalog, and truth that was draft when executed. It also freezes the Gym-owned execution/scoring harness (`src/cli.ts`, `src/model-registry.ts`, `src/loop`, `src/scenarios`, `src/scoring`, and the built scorer artifact when present); historical runs without this identity remain readable but are not comparison baselines. This snapshot does not freeze the Host, provider service, or operating environment, so their separately recorded provenance and diagnostics still matter. Candidate prompts are the intended treatment and may differ; their observed per-trial fingerprints are retained rather than forced equal. Diagnostic rescoring is useful only as new diagnostic evidence: it does not retroactively make a failed execution valid, approve truth, or become a release baseline.

## Practical maintenance and self-correction

May owns diagnosis and an isolated candidate; a technical specialist may implement focused runner/scorer changes and a second teammate or reviewer challenges the contract with independent controls. Neither May nor Gym approves its own behavior change. Use this trace:

1. **Observe:** retain the original run and independently reproduce a concrete unsafe pass, false failure, or hidden machine error. Record whether detection was human/operator initiated.
2. **Diagnose:** classify each fact as behavior, case/truth, execution, scorer, comparison, or provider. Compare against the frozen scenario contract; do not relabel failures to improve an aggregate.
3. **Assign:** the runner/scorer owner fixes mechanics; benchmark owners draft truth changes separately; humans approve preference-sensitive truth and promotion.
4. **Repair:** use the smallest isolated reusable change. Never rewrite historical runs. Freeze new scorer and judge definitions into newly created evidence.
5. **Control:** test a normal valid comparison plus wrong/opposite, malformed/unavailable, interrupted, changed-scope, changed-truth, changed-model/framework, changed-prompt-treatment, and deterministic-no-judge cases as applicable. Write to new evidence paths.
6. **Review:** an independent reviewer reads the diff and raw artifacts and reruns controls. A fresh semantic trial is required after a judge definition changes; unavailable providers leave the comparison ineligible rather than triggering a silent model switch.
7. **Approve/apply:** a human decides any preference-sensitive truth or behavior promotion; the operator integrates and verifies the exact reviewed revision separately.
