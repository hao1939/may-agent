## Baseline contract used for judgment

- Agent App design says an app packages one accountable owner and optional hosting; agents/workflows do not edit task storage directly, and task ownership resolves from task/ancestor/app owner before workflow execution.
- Task Resource Engine design says `.state/tasks/state.json` is the sole mutable authority; `.state/tasks/tree.json` is disposable projection; queues carry task IDs only; handlers return `converged`, `waiting`, or `needs-owner` with evidence/actions/Conditions admitted atomically.
- Projects design says active projects should have open work or an immediate owner judgment path, and metrics/events may wake judgment but must not decide project meaning.
- Evaluation design says `evaluation.app` audits target apps, delivers findings to owners, and does not take remediation ownership for target-project domain problems.
