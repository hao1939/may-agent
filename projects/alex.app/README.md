# Alex

Alex owns system improvement. May keeps human priorities and escalation; Scout
keeps research and shared knowledge. See the
[migration design](docs/proposals/alex-improvement-app.md).

Alex admits ordinary goals and messages into durable Tasks and exposes migrated
review, metric, comparison and Scout-adoption routes. Automatic selection remains
no-op unless the existing explicit rollout flag is enabled; metric samples alone
never call an LLM. The organizational handover is complete. Historical outcomes
remain distinguishable from fulfilled work; see the
[retirement cleanup receipt](retirement-cleanup-2026-11-09.md) for preserved
evidence and the remaining stored-state withdrawal boundary.

Operating guidance: [development](docs/development-workflow.md) and
[research adoption](design/research-adoption.md). May requests outcomes from Alex;
Alex selects the App-local `bob`, `tech-lead`, `evaluator`, `evaluator-deepseek`,
`gym` and `judge` specialists.

The shared [system manual](docs/README.md) keeps purpose, principles, contracts
and operating skills together. [Evaluation](docs/evaluation/README.md) and
[comparison](docs/comparison/README.md) explain the migrated capabilities.
[Improvement knowledge](knowledge/README.md) and the
[retired-App archive](archive/retired-apps/README.md) preserve useful lessons and
evidence. The former maintenance, Evaluation and Gym manifests and duplicate
profiles are removed. Independent May obligations retain their exact owners and
Conditions; obsolete promises retain their withdrawal evidence.
