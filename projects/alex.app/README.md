# Alex

Alex owns system improvement. May keeps human priorities and escalation; Scout
keeps research and shared knowledge. See the
[migration design](../may-agent.app/docs/proposals/alex-improvement-app.md).

Alex admits ordinary goals and messages into durable Tasks and exposes migrated
review, metric, comparison and Scout-adoption routes. Automatic selection remains
no-op unless the existing explicit rollout flag is enabled; metric samples alone
never call an LLM. Alex's migration receipt records unfinished cutover and legacy
obligations before claiming the new organization is complete.

Operating guidance: [development](docs/development-workflow.md) and
[research adoption](design/research-adoption.md). May requests outcomes from Alex;
Alex selects the App-local `bob`, `tech-lead`, `evaluator`, `evaluator-deepseek`,
`gym` and `judge` specialists.
