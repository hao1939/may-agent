# Workload advice: model comparison and refinements

This evidence supports the [workload model](../../proposals/agent-task-workload-model.md)
and [implementation approach](../../proposals/agent-task-workload-implementation.md).
The user authorized the isolated model comparison using the configured gateway.
That authorization does not activate the workload pilot or change live Tasks.

## First cohort

The Host candidate was `f68fd7f3070b6dfd88657e4e8527b00dd2d4b984`
([#443](https://github.com/hao1939/may-agent/pull/443)); the experiment source was
`5bf88f5ef6840fb092c010eaa34d2880de029b3f`, on the linked
`experiment/workload-behavior-comparison` branch. The active harness stays on that
branch. It uses actual App instructions, workload policy and workflow, real Host
workers and disposable SQLite state. Both arms retain canonical readonly Task
tools, including workload on demand. Only the supplied decision-time summary
differs. This measures its contribution over common scoped discovery.

The fresh scripted preflight passed **56 worker passes / 48 local requests /
2,003,964 request bytes**. The first real `gpt-5.6-sol` Chat Completions cohort
completed **56 passes / 62 forwarded requests / 2,632,353 serialized request
bytes**, with no scoped mechanical failure. Eight unchanged passes reused
accepted advice without another helper; the other 48 produced new decisions.
All business Task records remained unchanged. Neither arm requested workload on
demand. A successful minimal provider probe was additional to the 62 cohort
requests; an earlier installation-gateway connection attempt was unreachable.
No verified monetary tariff was established.
All 62 provider responses included usage: 501,206 prompt tokens and 20,737
completion tokens, 521,943 total, excluding the separate probe. These are returned
provider counts, not work-start credits or a dollar estimate.

All 48 new decisions selected the required incident: 46 retained it and two
reconsidered the method after checkout recovery. Manual assessment found:

| Case family | Observed choice and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Required work | Both arms retained actual repair and verification under light and heavy load. |
| Discretionary work | Both deferred optional readability work behind the required incident. Every such case has unresolved required work and App background concurrency one; this does not demonstrate expansion with supported spare capacity. |
| Recovery | Actual reservation release caused another review; unchanged pressure coalesced. Advice usually continued the same repair method. |
| Blocked idle | Supplied feedback gave more specific Task-local workspace recovery or smaller-method advice. Descriptive wake labels also revealed checkout changes to control, preventing a clean attribution to the summary. |
| Finite disposition | Only one of eight decisions explicitly proposed creator disposition of the delivered finite assessment; another mentioned it vaguely. The others did not give an actionable creator-review recommendation. This family did not uniformly meet its fixed acceptance. |
| Older work | Both retained the older repair and the distinct new caller response. Advice did not demonstrate actual caller delivery or bounded waiting. |

Three parent event-delivery warnings reported `database is locked`. Their cause
is unestablished. They did not fail the scoped mechanical assertions, but those
assertions do not establish clean event delivery or recovery. Raw child
diagnostics remain; parent output was not redirected, so a separate observation
note records the three warnings without pretending to be a raw log.

## Neutral wake refinement

The first fixture included private sequence names such as `checkout-recovered`
in its `fixture.review` event data. Actual received prompts and control advice
confirmed that these names supplied hints in both arms. That cohort remains
preserved with its limitation; it is not silently relabeled or overwritten.

Experiment revision `6427c339ba63c2798ba2913dd17d28bcc54e23f5` sends constant wake
input with empty data. Private step names stay in evidence paths and manifests.
A received-prompt assertion checks the actual structured brief's event payload,
without banning words that may legitimately appear in business evidence. Task
inputs, authority, tools, grants, case criteria and bounds remain the same.
Strict source-SDK types and a two-pass smoke check passed; the full scripted
preflight passed **56 / 48 / 2,002,552 bytes**. Its parent log retained two SQLite
lock warnings. The real neutral cohort completed **56 passes / 64 requests /
2,711,001 serialized request bytes**, with zero scoped mechanical failures and
no parent lock warning in its redirected log. All business records stayed
unchanged; eight passes coalesced, 48 new decisions were assessed and neither
arm requested workload on demand. Returned usage totaled 516,095 prompt tokens
and 21,204 completion tokens, 537,299 total.

Both arms retained required repair and verification and deferred optional work.
None of the eight finite-disposition decisions supplied an actionable creator
review. Neutral control no longer inferred checkout state from private labels.
Feedback initially described the exact dated prerequisite, but both repetitions
carried workspace-recovery advice forward after the complete current observation
reported no blockers. One also called the snapshot unchanged. Absence of a
blocker is not proof of physical health; prior advice is equally not evidence
that the shortage persists. This exposes a current-evidence failure that the
annotated wake labels had obscured.

## App review guidance refinement

App revision `52b3a48be87d6dc8d9006c36d39d2bbc903d5611` adds two instructions to
the existing review prompt. Prefer a concrete owner decision, including reviewing
a sufficient finite result while preserving its unfinished subject and standing
work. Treat prior advice as history and ground blocker claims in current dated
facts, using a scoped read or explicit uncertainty when coverage is insufficient.
Existing authority persists when workload changes. The same readonly call,
decision schema, accepted-state coalescing and Task lifecycle remain in use;
this adds no closure call or business mutation.

The paired experiment is frozen at `0b8574989345bda1c67c8a54889d40c17740ec55`
with identical cases and authority. The neutral baseline consumed all 64 request
places, so the new cohort prospectively permits 80 requests / 4 MiB to leave room
for useful exact readonly checks. Four requests per pass, 1,024 requested output
tokens per request, 45-second provider, 100-second worker and 20-minute new-start
bounds remain. Earlier receipts and their original bounds are preserved. The
user's continued isolated-trial authorization covers this refinement; it does
not expand live operational authority.

The refined real cohort completed **56 passes / 61 requests / 2,637,577 serialized
request bytes**, with no scoped mechanical failure or parent lock warning. Eight
unchanged passes coalesced; all 48 new decisions were assessed and business Task
records remained unchanged. No on-demand workload read occurred. Returned usage
was 499,838 prompt tokens and 22,401 completion tokens, 522,239 total.

All **eight of eight** finite decisions now named assessment for recorded-creator
review of its accepted result and remaining caller obligations, preserving the
unfinished repair and standing route. Both feedback recovery decisions explicitly
stopped carrying the old workspace shortage forward, retained normal admission
and disclosed unknown resource health. All eight older-work decisions recommended
the exact new caller response while preserving the original repair path.
Required repair and verification remained owed throughout. The guidance changed
in both arms: the finite-review improvement belongs primarily to that change,
not solely to receiving a workload summary. This small trial is not a statistical
guarantee or evidence of applied disposition, delivered answers or throughput.

## Focused optional-method coverage

Experiment revision `74f18feb4ac3ec247648fc77ab9f2a86998945f6` retains the same
production prompt and adds a light/busy pair without a competing required
incident. The complete sample text is supplied; this small optional assessment
requires no checkout, external source or helper. It separates unknown fleet
health from the known prerequisites of this method. Existing root/account scope
and normal admission remain constraints; light load still does not require
expansion or creation.

Validated exact case filters select only the new pair, producing eight passes
with both arms and two repetitions, at most 32 forwarded requests. Unknown or
duplicate case IDs, unsupported flags and mixed smoke/case selection refuse
before evidence creation, workers or requests; five refusal checks passed.
Strict source-SDK harness types and the **8 passes / 8 local requests /
336,156-byte** scripted preflight passed. This adds coverage without rerunning
the unchanged 56-pass model matrix.

The focused real cohort completed **8 passes / 8 requests / 336,156 serialized
request bytes**, with no scoped mechanical failure, business Task change,
on-demand workload read or parent lock warning. Returned usage totaled 63,254
prompt tokens and 3,816 completion tokens, 67,070 total. All eight decisions
recommended the optional opportunity, grounded in the ambiguous supplied wording
and three support questions. None created work or applied an implementation.
This inexpensive method remained worthwhile under both loads. The case supports
useful consideration without treating unknown fleet health as a blanket blocker;
it does **not** demonstrate workload-sensitive timing, lower-priority execution,
costly expansion or throughput. Some advice already contains the small assessment,
but it remains on the review Task and is not an accepted result or delivered
answer on the business assignment.

## Interpretation and remaining acceptance

Preserving obligations is necessary but does not establish useful adaptation.
Equal business choices under different load can be correct; useful feedback may
instead change method, fanout, prerequisite recovery or which owner decision is
needed. The initial generic repair advice missed useful finite-result review;
the recorded prompt refinement improved that criterion without changing its
acceptance, authority or case need. Preserve the equal optional choices and
unmeasured timing effects rather than manufacture a positive feedback effect.

These are small, two-repetition advice comparisons. No closure, reprioritization,
caller disposition or repaired delivery is applied. Independent source review
and integration, installed finite-result disposition with exact caller evidence,
and an authorized bounded operational pilot remain separate acceptance work.
Raw prompts, responses, accepted results, before/after records, receipts,
assessment and file-hash manifests remain in the private workload checkpoint.

A read-only installation check during the neutral trial returned one active agent
and active work. Alex had 48 open assignments (42 idle, six waiting); all three
loaded Apps had 101 open, 76 idle, 17 waiting, seven attention and one running.
This is a new bounded snapshot, not a population classification or a load-health
judgment. The four previously identified process inodes still hash to the old
#392 artifact, `0bb1969fbf12e6ad3138d7d15a49ec33e2d474aaea641a0404dd0223fed1d0eb`.
The active immutable App manifest still selects
`6a7e49843c5d2c8cccd7615b991472856d43f8d4`; its Alex definition declares neither
finite-result closure nor workload review. No live contract-capability read,
activation, restart or Task mutation was performed by this check.
