The model

Arrival rate λ = 3j/i. Work capacity μ_work = 3k/i. With prompt closure, μ = μ_work; with a management limit, μ = min(μ_work, μ_close).

Load ratio ρ = λ/μ. Excess demand g = max(0, λ − μ). Fluid backlog N(t) = max(0, N₀ + (λ − μ)t).

This baseline accepts all arriving work without feedback. Color shows demand relative to capacity. The workload design uses that information to guide priorities, scope and discretionary expansion; pressure alone does not determine acceptance or completion. Random bursts can cause waiting even below capacity; equality leaves no headroom. The k input reflects capacity after reserving management bandwidth. Agents have equal workloads, and contribution costs are included in j or k. This is average-flow math, not a prediction of random queue length.
