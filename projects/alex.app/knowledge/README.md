# Improvement knowledge

Alex keeps useful system-improvement knowledge with the work it supports.
Current rules belong in the [design manual](../docs/README.md); these records
supply experience and hypotheses to check against current evidence.

- `retired-maintenance/` preserves the former specialists' analyses, methods,
  lessons and role context, grouped by their original contributor.
- [Evaluation criteria](../criteria/criteria.md), calibration and lessons retain
  the review method alongside the evaluator.
- [Historical trials](../archive/retired-apps/README.md) retain dated evidence
  and explain how to find previous source and operational artifacts.

Historical role instructions and scripts are reference material. They are not
installed agent identities, active skills, current authority or evidence that
an old finding remains unresolved. Promote useful lessons into their owning
manual only after checking their relevance and limits.
