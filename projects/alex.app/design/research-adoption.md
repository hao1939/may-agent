# Research adoption through Alex

**Supports:** [Useful completed work](../docs/1-principles/purpose.md).
Turn preserved research into a practical local recommendation, including a
supported decision to leave the system unchanged.
**Choice basis:** [Agent judgment and infra execution](../docs/1-principles/core-principles.md).
Alex judges local value; ordinary Tasks retain the work and its caller return.

Status: implemented in the migration candidate. See the
[migration receipt](../migration-receipt-2026-10-04.md) for activation and gaps.

Scout publishes a qualified finding with its source revision, artifact/hash,
claim, limits and possible application. Alex receives that evidence as ordinary
work. He can reuse a related investigation, ask Bob for a bounded contribution,
assign a distinct investigation or retain a no-change decision. A finding is
neither an implementation order nor an Evaluation report.

The weekly archive schedule assigns Bob one standing `research-adoption` Task.
It keeps a fixed outcome and acceptance; new archive observations remain saved
inputs. The schedule uses `catchUp: latest` to avoid replaying missed weeks.
Bob reads up to three records per review, keeps an accepted cursor and considers
at most one experiment within the current allowance. Closing the Task is an
owner decision; late schedule input does not authorize reopening it.

An explicit investigation uses an ordinary Alex `goal` with its own outcome,
acceptance and caller relationship. Callers normally omit assignment and let
Alex choose his helpers. App-local policy can assign `bob` directly. Changed
requirements do not overwrite the fixed standing contract.

Bob checks current requirements, code, linked work and source coverage before
proposing an experiment. State the expected benefit, smallest adaptation,
baseline, falsifying result, cost and stop condition. One bounded experiment
can call the Alex-local `tech-lead`; a durable independent outcome uses an
ordinary request. Source claims are not local results, and a design review is
not independent validation of its author's hypothesis.

Return evidence to Alex or the assigning caller through the saved relationship.
Keep durable reports at the caller's evidence root or the Task notes path in
`TASK.md`, with verified bytes and hashes. Retain source limits, failed trials,
current candidate, related work, consumed allowance and next action. Quiet
no-change, unsuitable, insufficient-evidence or unchanged deferral outcomes
are useful results; they do not require a polling loop or human message.

Alex decides whether further work is worthwhile and escalates unresolved help
or authority needs to May. May retains human commitments and decisions. Moving
Bob under Alex adds no application authority: research-generated candidates
still use the existing exact human approval and guarded application path.
Already authorized work retains its actual scope. Accepted research, approved
application and independently verified benefit remain separate facts.
