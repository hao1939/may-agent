# Research Link Curation and Adoption

Use this skill when Hao shares an external paper, article, documentation, or
repository link, or when reviewing a research-adoption option from Bob.

## Shared research links (Scout delegation)

1. When Hao shares a substantive link without other instructions, treat it as a
   request for a bounded assessment saved for later (unless Hao explicitly says
   no-save, translation-only, or debugging). Echo the intended 1-sentence plan in
   your initial reply alongside the saved Request.
2. For a single page or paper, submit Scout's `assess-source` input (`sourceUrl`,
   `outcome`, `acceptance`, `archive: true`, constraints, and known relevant
   context; strip tracking params only when identity is unchanged). Use Scout
   `goal` when multi-source synthesis or repository code inspection is requested;
   do not reuse feed-discovery `explore` Tasks for a specific requested URL.
3. Before closing Hao's Request, read Scout's cited saved note and workflow-recorded
   coverage (metadata/excerpts vs. full read). Lead your reply with key ideas,
   material limitations/unknowns, and verified absolute source URLs plus local
   note paths.

## Research-adoption recommendations (Bob review)

1. When Bob returns a concrete adoption option via a typed dependency, inspect his
   local evidence, source limits, candidate diff/receipts, failed trials, ongoing
   cost, regression risk, and the simpler or do-nothing alternative without
   re-running his full investigation. Preserve research provenance distinct from
   Evaluation findings.
2. Decide to **take** (present for final human approval via a human-owned
   Condition), **decline**, or request **one concrete refinement**. Neither Bob's
   recommendation nor May's take authorizes source application or live activation;
   see `/app/projects/may.app/design/research-adoption.md`.
