# Bob-owned research adoption

This is May policy for turning preserved research into a decision-ready local
option. It uses ordinary `goal` inputs, Tasks, dependencies, events, and the
existing improvement approval/application path. It is not a new App, queue,
registry, Evaluation report type, or automatic development trigger.

## Assignment and identity

A May goal may explicitly select an installed May team role with
`data.assignment.agent`. Omitting the assignment keeps the default `may` owner.
`may-bob` continues to support finite design and review goals; those goals keep
their admitted identity, outcome, acceptance, and Host-owned caller/dependency
return.

Research adoption adds procedure data, not another input kind:

```json
{
  "kind": "goal",
  "data": {
    "outcome": "the exact requested outcome",
    "acceptance": ["the exact requested acceptance"],
    "assignment": { "agent": "may-bob" },
    "research": {
      "adoption": "research-adoption",
      "mode": "targeted"
    }
  }
}
```

`targeted` creates a finite identity and preserves the admitted requirements.
Its caller is the Host's actual source/dependency correlation, never a payload
field that claims authority. `standing` maps qualified Scout handoffs and weekly
archive observations to the stable `research-adoption` Task. That Task has one
fixed desired specification; each changing lead remains in the Host-retained
admitted input/reconciliation facts instead of changing the Task spec. Closing
it is an owner decision, and late schedule input does not itself authorize
resurrection.

## Entry and bounded judgment

Scout may publish `scout.research.qualified` only after preserving and verifying
one plausibly locally relevant source record. The handoff includes its HTTPS
source, reviewed revision, exact artifact/hash, claim, reading/evidence limits,
possible local application, and optional related work. Its deterministic
transport identity deduplicates the same source revision and bytes. This event
is research evidence, never an Evaluation publication or implementation order.
Scout's daily digest remains presentation-only and unchanged.

A weekly input schedule uses `catchUp: latest`, so one newest missed slot can be
admitted after downtime without replaying a backlog. Bob reads at most three
archive records per review, continues from the cursor in the latest accepted
current-first result, and wraps after the final record. This recovers a missed
handoff while allowing older records to surface. Exact transport deduplication
is code-owned; semantic overlap, earlier negative results, and whether changed
evidence warrants rereading are Bob's judgment.

The pilot allowance is at most one candidate across related research and repair
work, at most one new experiment in a weekly allowance, and at most three source
records per archive review. Busy ownership or exhausted allowance yields a
visible deferral naming what would justify reconsideration. It does not produce
a retry loop. Already-supported, unsuitable, insufficient-evidence, negative,
and no-change outcomes are complete and useful.

## Investigation, delegation, and return

Bob owns applicability through the recommendation. He reads the actual current
requirements, design, code, linked work, and source coverage. Before delegating,
he states the expected local benefit, smallest adaptation, baseline, falsifying
result, check/comparison plan, cost bound, and stop condition. Source claims are
not local results. Removal, reuse, or no change can beat a new subsystem.

The `agents` capability permits one awaited, bounded same-App call to
`may-tech-lead` for an explicit experiment or candidate-preparation scope.
Tech-lead does not inherit the standing scan as an implementation assignment,
change parent requirements, deploy, or approve. Longer independent work uses an
ordinary finite dependency with its exact caller return. Bob may review design
conformance, but that does not independently validate his own hypothesis.

A recommendation presents current state first: opportunity, source identity and
limits, local evidence/design fit, baseline/candidate/diff and checks, failed
trials, risks and applicability limits, implementation and ongoing cost,
simpler/do-nothing alternatives, accountable owner, recommendation, and exact
next action. History remains behind links.

After concluding that a concrete candidate merits May's value judgment, the
standalone Bob owner returns `waiting` with one typed same-App dependency. Its
stable id binds the recommendation/candidate; its ordinary May `goal` input asks
May to take, decline, or refine that exact option and, only if worthwhile,
prepare the existing final-human-approval wait. The Host retains the exact caller
return, so parent placement is not mistaken for correlation. Bob reads and
retains May's accepted judgment before converging. No-change, negative,
insufficient, already-supported, and unchanged deferred scans remain quiet in
Bob's Task result. Bounded design/review contributions return only to their
supplied caller or workflow and never create this separate May judgment.

## May and human authority

May makes the value/cost/simplicity take, decline, or refine judgment. A take
means only that the concrete option is worth showing to the human. May may then
use the existing exact human-owned Condition and trusted plain-text Telegram
reply path. No candidate applies after May's recommendation, silence, rejection,
ambiguity, correction request, stale reply, or an agent-authored approval field.
The approved candidate, target, evidence, scope, Task generation, and proposal
must all match; changing any of them requires refreshed review and final human
approval. Duplicate delivery cannot duplicate application. Adoption and a
subsequent independent Evaluation remain separate evidence.
