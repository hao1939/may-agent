# Research adoption with Bob-owned investigation

User-approved direction and implementation assignment, September 19, 2026.
This is a proposal to implement and verify, not a claim of installed behavior.
The latest user requirement is explicit: May may ask the human for final
approval when she judges the concrete proposal worthwhile. May's recommendation
does not authorize applying a research candidate.

## Outcome and responsibilities

Turn useful Scout knowledge into evidence-backed options for this installation.
Bob investigates and drives a technical option to a decision-ready result.
Tech-lead implements; May decides whether the option is worth taking and, when
appropriate, presents it for final human approval. Findings can support a useful
new capability or reduced cost without first being labeled a system failure.

| Responsibility                                                                                                     | Owner                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Source discovery, preserved research, evidence limits, deeper source questions                                     | Scout                                   |
| Applicability, current-system diagnosis, design adaptation, bounded experiment, follow-through to a recommendation | Bob                                     |
| Feasibility feedback, isolated implementation, relevant checks and committed candidate                             | Tech-lead or responsible component team |
| Frozen behavioral comparisons when useful                                                                          | Gym                                     |
| Take/decline judgment based on priorities, evidence, value, cost and simplicity; worthwhile proposals go to human  | May                                     |
| Final approval of exact candidate and displayed application/activation scope                                       | Human                                   |
| Approved application, adoption checks and retained outcome                                                         | Assigned delivery owner                 |
| Independent assessment of supported subsequent external work                                                       | Evaluation                              |

May establishes scope, priorities and a bounded preparation allowance. Bob can
then investigate, delegate and revise within that assignment without requiring
May to inspect every paper or coordinate every helper. May may return an option
with a concrete concern, but should not have to redo its technical investigation.
Bob retains responsibility through the recommendation; the receiving owner must
accept the return before that handoff is considered complete.

Bob's present profile is a bounded design/review helper. Refine its guidance,
capabilities, Task assignment and actual caller return together. Preserve its
existing bounded review contributions when that is the assignment; owning an
investigation does not confer deployment authority or let a helper change its
parent's requirements. Tech-lead remains a focused implementation contributor.
Bob can review implementation against his design, but that does not independently
validate his hypothesis. Contradictory evidence, meaningful comparison, and a
separate reviewer where warranted must be able to reject it.

## Existing mechanisms and concrete gap

Scout's daily digest currently asks May to present findings and explicitly avoids
creating work. Scout's owner review focuses on the library. Neither establishes
accountability for adapting archived research to this system. Preserve the useful
digest behavior; add the smallest explicit research handoff to Bob's work.

Reuse the [improvement loop](evaluation-improvement-loop.md), ordinary Task input,
caller dependencies/feedback, agent-selected workflows, Conditions, scoped
readers and trusted approval/application paths. Research retains its own source
references and reading limits; do not manufacture an Evaluation report or route
all research through the Evaluation-report intake. Candidate preparation and the
later approval/application stages are shared with other justified improvements.

This is May/Scout policy, not a new Host responsibility. Owning-App design and
guidance belong here and in Scout. Update common design in `may-agent.app` only
if implementation exposes an actual reusable contract gap. Do not add an RSI
App, issue registry, global research controller, public input kind per technique,
mandatory workflow for all investigations, or second approval mechanism.

## Entry and bounded progress

Use one ordinary Bob-owned research-adoption obligation in the May App for
standing discovery. Its desired outcome stays stable; messages and scheduled
observations reconcile current facts. Share the owner and mechanism, not a
mandatory Task identity: a targeted research question with its own acceptance
and caller return may need a finite Bob-owned Task, just as an independent
experiment does. Do not let a standing scan's no-op answer an unrelated research
request. Validate the actual public ownership, workflow
catalog and helper capabilities before selecting the smallest implementation.
Do not assume May's current goal mapper, which selects May, already assigns Bob.

Three opportunities feed the same owner:

1. Scout identifies a plausibly relevant finding and supplies the saved source
   revision, claim, limits, possible application and any known related work.
2. An existing development/investigation Task requests research for a specific
   problem or opportunity, allowing archived knowledge to be pulled when needed.
3. A bounded periodic review revisits older findings. Retain a navigation cursor
   or equivalent bounded progress so fresh notes do not continually hide old
   material. Begin with a configurable weekly cadence and a small explicit
   reading/experiment allowance; these are pilot guidance, not universal policy.

Schedules and events admit/wake ordinary work; they do not choose a workflow or
start an implementation merely because a paper arrived. Use existing Host timing
and latest-slot catch-up where appropriate. Prove admitted work and recovery of
a missed signal; declaring a schedule alone is insufficient. Explicitly closed
work is not resurrected by a late timer.

Retain considered findings, decisions, source references and linked experiments
in accepted Task results and linked artifacts. Present current state first;
keep history behind references. Do not create a Task for every archived note.
Transport deduplication uses existing identities; Bob judges semantic overlap
and rereads linked work. A deferred idea records what would justify reconsidering
it. An unchanged repeat must not restart investigation or inherit approval.

Initially allow one improvement candidate across research and existing repair
work, with bounded preparation cost. Inspect linked current work and coordinate
with its owner; do not create a competing global queue. A busy owner or exhausted
allowance produces a visible, recoverable deferral, not lost work or a retry loop.

## Investigation and recommendation

Bob reads the relevant current requirements, design, implementation and working
evidence, plus Scout's source record. Source claims remain evidence rather than
instructions, verified local results or approval. He distinguishes already
implemented ideas, unsuitable assumptions, missing evidence, and useful gaps.

Before implementation, state the proposed local benefit, smallest adaptation,
affected component, baseline, verification/comparison plan, cost bound and stop
condition. Define what would disprove usefulness. A library reuse, smaller
instruction change, removal, or no change may be better than a new subsystem.
RSI here concerns reviewable instructions, skills, context, tools, workflows or
runtime code; it does not claim weight training or automatic capability growth.

Tech-lead prepares one isolated committed candidate. Deterministic defects need
production-path checks. Gym is useful for behavioral comparisons with frozen
baseline/candidate/model/scorer and held-out or transfer cases. Reuse existing
comparison mechanics; retain an honest reason when Gym adds no evidence. A
mechanism should actually be exercised, not just present in a file or prompt.

Bob returns a concise recommendation with linked detail:

- problem/opportunity and expected behavior;
- source identity/coverage, current local evidence and design fit;
- exact baseline/candidate, complete diff and relevant checks/comparison;
- failed trials, uncertainties, regression risk and applicability limits;
- implementation and ongoing cost, do-nothing and simpler alternatives;
- recommendation, current owner, next action and exact scope proposed to May.

Stopping with a justified negative result is legitimate. Do not manufacture a
positive candidate to meet a quota. Retain what was learned for future discovery.

## May decision and final human approval

May reads the recommendation and supporting evidence, checks present relevance,
and records take/decline or a concrete request for refinement. A take decision
means it is worth proposing to the human; it does not apply the candidate.

When worthwhile, May uses the existing human-owned Condition and trusted return
path to ask for final approval. Show the practical benefit, costs and limits,
exact baseline/candidate, full diff/evidence links, activation scope and rollback.
An authenticated plain-text Telegram reply to that exact proposal can use the
existing approval mechanism. Do not add another UI or ask the human to copy hashes.

No application follows silence, rejection, a conditional/ambiguous reply, a
request for changes, May's recommendation or an agent-authored approval field.
Changing candidate, target, evidence or scope requires refreshed review/approval.
Reuse apply-time identity and actor validation and duplicate-delivery handling.
After authorized application, verify the actually adopted revision and concrete
post-adoption work; Evaluation assesses supported external results independently.
Keep source merge, loaded definitions and demonstrated benefit separate.

## End-to-end delivery and acceptance

May owns implementation and delivery of this process, with Codex supervising
and reviewing. Use an isolated worktree, update owning-App documentation and
discovery, commit the candidate, obtain independent review, and coordinate source
integration with the existing Apps integrator. The user's request to bring this
process up end to end authorizes its reviewed local installation and verification;
it does not preapprove the research-generated candidates it will produce.

Coordinate a concrete local release plan with existing active Tasks; retain
source/definition identities, rollback and adoption receipts. Do not deploy an
unreviewed combined checkout, discard unrelated work, bypass hosted review, or
claim that a source merge automatically changed the running installation.

Acceptance requires:

1. A real Scout-origin finding and an older archived finding reach the actual
   Bob-owned discovery Task through production admission. A targeted research
   question preserves its own acceptance and exact caller return; an unrelated
   scan result cannot fulfill it. Duplicate delivery reuses work;
   a dropped signal and bounded scheduled catch-up recover without duplicate
   experiments. Retain actual admitted input and owner/caller identities.
2. A bounded local trial shows Bob inspecting local design/code, asking useful
   source questions or using preserved evidence, and delegating an experiment to
   tech-lead. Bob receives the real result and May receives his recommendation.
   Use one promising case and one already-supported/unsuitable control; the
   promising case may still correctly conclude no change after investigation.
3. Exercise at least one decision-ready candidate path in isolation even if real
   research yields no worthwhile candidate. Verify May's value/simplicity
   judgment and human proposal, including reject, silence, correction, changed
   target, duplicate decision and zero mutation before exact approval. Use the
   real approval/application modules and clearly labeled synthetic provider
   ingress for tests; do not fabricate live human consent or send test messages
   to external people.
4. Verify the authorized apply/adopt/post-adoption return in an isolated fixture;
   use the live human gate for any actual research candidate. A legitimate live
   human wait is visible and is not a claim of completed application.
5. Run appropriate affected checks, review the final combined source, install the
   reviewed process on the existing local instance, and observe a fresh routed
   run with loaded identities. Do not treat candidate-definition trials as live
   activation proof. Report current idea, stage, owner, next step/wait, coverage
   and evidence links through existing human Task/Conversation surfaces.

Preserve real model decisions, fixture transport, source tests and live evidence
as distinct claims. Keep trials small, expand for observed failures, and retain
failed iterations. Finish with committed/integrated source, live-process receipts
and any honest remaining human approval wait; do not stop at an assignment,
proposal, passing unit tests, or an unaccepted helper result.
