# Missing app convention

- May missed one app-specific ownership convention during a review.
- The convention is current and documented in the app's indexed design page.
- May's starting context links the global design index but not the app index.
- A proposal appends fourteen complete app design documents to every May prompt.
- Several of those documents change often and most are irrelevant to a given task.
- Adding one stable app-index pointer makes the convention discoverable on demand.
