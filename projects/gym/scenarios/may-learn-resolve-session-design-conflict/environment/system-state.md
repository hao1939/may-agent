# Session evidence and current design

- An old Hao-authored session says completed tasks and their sessions should
  remain durable project knowledge and working memory.
- The current accepted Task Resource Engine keeps minimum receipts and does not
  keep an archive tree. Reusable experience belongs in workflows, skills,
  tests, design, or domain evidence.
- The current accepted Sessions design expires completed session artifacts
  after the retention window unless they are explicitly exported.
- The accepted May deputy curriculum says sessions are evidence. Accepted
  May-agent documents remain architecture truth.
- The useful part of the old signal is that important conclusions must be
  promoted into an appropriate durable mechanism instead of being lost.
- The learning catalog can record `supported`, `revise`, `conflict`,
  `out-of-scope`, or `needs-human`. It cannot change May or benchmark truth.
