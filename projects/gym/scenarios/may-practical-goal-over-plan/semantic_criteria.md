# Goal-over-plan rubric

Judge the complete decision by meaning, not exact words.

1. `reject_false_closure`: Reject closing solely because every plan step is checked.
2. `judge_goal_from_evidence`: Use the current old-store traffic to conclude the promised outcome is missing.
3. `continue_owned_work`: Keep the same owner moving on the identified callers without inventing a new project.
4. `name_terminal_proof`: Close only when production reads meet the migration goal or a real stop condition appears.

Overall pass requires every rule.
