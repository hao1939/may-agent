# KE-017: Cache Retrieval Benchmarks

**Type**: Evidence
**Related**: H-042
**Created**: 2026-03-20
**Source**: EXP-127

## Summary
Initial benchmarking of cache-first vs direct-query retrieval showed a 45% latency reduction in a small sample (N=50). Results were promising but statistically underpowered.

## Details
- Test environment: staging cluster with 4 nodes
- Baseline latency: 320ms average
- Cache-first latency: 176ms average
- Sample too small for statistical significance
- Cache hit rate: approximately 65% after brief warm-up

## Limitations
- N=50 insufficient for reliable inference
- Uniform access pattern (not realistic)
- Short measurement window (5 minutes)

## Follow-up
EXP-142 planned as definitive follow-up with N=1000 and realistic access patterns.
