# KE-017: Cache Retrieval Benchmarks

**Type**: Evidence
**Related**: H-042
**Created**: 2026-03-20

## Summary
Initial benchmarking of cache-first vs direct-query retrieval showed a 45% latency reduction in a small sample (N=50). Results were promising but statistically underpowered.

## Details
- Test environment: staging cluster with 4 nodes
- Baseline latency: 320ms average
- Cache-first latency: 176ms average
- Sample too small for statistical significance

## Update: EXP-142 Results (2026-04-08)
EXP-142 provided the large-scale validation that KE-017's preliminary findings lacked:
- **Latency reduction**: 62% (340ms → 129ms) across 1,000 requests — confirming and strengthening KE-017's initial 45% finding
- **Cache hit rate**: 78% after warm-up period
- **Statistical significance**: p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.84 (large effect)
- H-042 probability updated from 0.35 to 0.72
- See also: KE-071 (validated retrieval pattern), KE-023 (now validated)
