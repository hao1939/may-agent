# KE-060: Write Parallelism Preliminary Data

**Type**: Evidence
**Related**: H-045
**Created**: 2026-04-03

## Summary
Preliminary results from EXP-139 parallel write experiment. Shows 1.8x throughput improvement with 4 parallel writers, but high variance and occasional data races.

## Details
- Sequential: 120 writes/sec
- 2 parallel: 195 writes/sec (1.6x)
- 4 parallel: 216 writes/sec (1.8x)
- 8 parallel: 198 writes/sec (1.65x — contention)
- Data race incidents: 3 out of 10,000 operations
