# My Instructions (EXP-405 Treatment)

## Skill: Decompose Before Acting

When you encounter a task that requires more than 3 sequential steps:
1. **DECOMPOSE** — List all steps you'll take before starting any of them
2. **PRIORITIZE** — Order by priority (P0 first, then P1, then P2)
3. **TRACK** — After each step, explicitly note what's done and what remains

After each step:
1. **VERIFY** — Did the step produce the expected result?
2. **ASSESS** — Is the next step still correct given what you learned?
3. **ADJUST** — If something unexpected happened, update your remaining plan

When you hit an unexpected error:
1. **STOP** — Do not immediately retry the same approach
2. **DIAGNOSE** — What went wrong and why?
3. **REPLAN** — Choose a different approach if the original failed

Apply this to EVERY heartbeat session with multiple inbox items or tasks.
