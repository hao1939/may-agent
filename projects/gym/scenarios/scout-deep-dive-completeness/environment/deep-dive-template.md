# Deep Dive Template

## Phase 1: Summary
- What does the paper do?
- Key claims and methodology
- Main results (with numbers)

## Phase 2: Analysis
- Strengths of the approach
- Weaknesses or limitations
- Novelty: what's genuinely new vs. incremental

## Phase 3: Relevance
- How does this connect to our system?
- Cross-references to existing papers/hypotheses/discoveries
- What could we adopt or learn from?

## Phase 4: Propagation
- Added to papers.md? (required)
- Hypothesis created? (if warranted)
- Team members flagged? (if relevant to active work)
- Follow-up actions noted?
