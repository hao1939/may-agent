# Repeated work and automation authority

- An old Hao-authored session says a request should automatically become
  scheduled automation after it is repeated.
- Current accepted design treats repetition as evidence of possible recurring
  work, not permission to create automation.
- Before automation is adopted, its trigger, owner, app mapping, and operating
  process must be stable and tested.
- Gym owns evidence review, learning material, baseline evaluation, and
  candidate evaluation. It cannot change accepted design or production May.
- The useful intention is clear: reduce repeated manual work when automation is
  reliable and worthwhile.
- No decision from Hao is needed unless the work produces a real design-change
  proposal or a verified May candidate.
