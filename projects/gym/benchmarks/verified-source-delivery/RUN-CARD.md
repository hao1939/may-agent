# Run card — bounded source repair, owner delivery

**UNFILLED — DO NOT EXECUTE.** This is a review checklist, not a new input schema,
Task request, authority grant, native case or passing result. See the
[case specification](REVIEW.md). Fill from an existing genuinely authorized
assignment; do not make up a Task, defect, expected patch or permission.

## 1. Assignment and authority

- Exact existing App/Task ID, generation and authoritative public read reference: **not selected**.
- Creator, current requirement/input revisions, input keys and original caller return: **not selected**.
- Actual outcome, acceptance and constraints, retained verbatim: **not selected**.
- Why the work remains useful and unfinished; evidence it is not retired/already fulfilled: **not established**.
- Is local committed repair/test/review the complete requested boundary? **not established**.
- Any other pending inputs, independent obligations, waits or standing schedule to preserve: **not inspected**.
- Separately granted publication/integration/closure authority, if any; otherwise explicitly none: **not established**.
- Who will review the completed card and authorize any execution: **not assigned**.

A standing review's broad purpose is not the concrete development specification.
An exhausted incident remains exhausted; this card cannot renew its allowance.
If the actual assignment owes PR/CI/deployment as well, do not silently narrow it
or call the local contribution complete delivery of that broader assignment.

## 2. Source, defect and acceptance

- Canonical target repository and exact base commit: **not selected**.
- Runtime-assigned isolated Task checkout and retained branch: **not selected**.
- `.git`/ignore/worktree checks, initial tracked/index/untracked state and relevant environment/dependency versions: **not inspected**.
- Concrete defect and current reproduction with raw failure evidence: **not selected**.
- Expected behavioral assertion on base versus delivered candidate: **not reviewed**.
- Authorized source/test paths and protected existing files/tests/bytes: **not reviewed**.
- Exact creator verification argv and cwd, with available script/suite confirmation: **not selected**.
- Other applicable repository/role checks and whether each actually exists: **not inspected**.
- Independent acceptance probe and safe isolated replay method: **not reviewed**.
- Evidence that no already-sufficient current candidate/check/review exists: **not inspected**.

Do not inject unrelated dirty tracked files into a clean-candidate procedure.
Do not remove real existing work to make the setup look clean. Preserve it and
resolve the setup/authority question instead. A base failure from missing tooling
or a test's incompatible imports is not reproduction of the intended source bug.

## 3. Installed subject, capabilities and allowance

- Selected installed release/profile hashes for Alex, procedure, Bob, Tech-lead and SDK/runtime: **not frozen**.
- Actual public Task entry and capability/authority readback: **not checked**.
- Real permitted coding/check/helper execution paths; no controlled stand-ins: **not checked**.
- Procedure requirement/prerequisites and exact selected verification option: **not checked**.
- Existing incident identity, consumed calls/rounds, remaining allowance and where it is retained: **not read**.
- Maximum observed episode/attempt/invocation allowance and absolute deadline: **not authorized**.
- Total target/helper/review model-cost ceiling, enforcement/stop mechanism and unknown-cost treatment: **not authorized**.
- Who may provide assistance, how it is recorded, and when intervention ends comparability: **not reviewed**.
- Filesystem/process/network/service isolation and explicit permitted external effects: **not reviewed**.
- Cancellation, timeout and remaining-work return behavior: **not reviewed**.

The finish-only judgment configuration cannot execute this case. Source/catalog
presence is not installation. If the required entry, authority or compatible
setup is unavailable, retain that gap; do not replace it with a private controller,
fake team receipt, hidden target prompt repair or an automatically renewed Task.

## 4. Observation, retention and verdict review

- Observer's read-only access and exact trustworthy Task/attempt/execution/command record sources: **not selected**.
- Pre/post Git/protected-file and relevant tool-action capture: **not arranged**.
- Raw verification/review records and `{ path, sha256 }` artifact readback: **not arranged**.
- Actual owner result admission/settlement and original caller/input linkage evidence: **not arranged**.
- Retention of the unmerged deliverable, branch, reports and raw evidence after settlement: **not arranged**.
- Independent regression replay location, producer and recorded outputs: **not arranged**.
- Proposed scorer's positive/negative controls and review of pass/fail/invalid distinctions: **not implemented or approved**.
- Human corrections, failed calls, rejected results, extra replies, target/helper/observer timing and cost accounting: **not arranged**.
- Reviewer of the exact case, acceptance evidence and any preference-sensitive truth: **not assigned**.
- Separate registration/implementation permission and model-execution permission: **not given**.

**Ready only when** the actual assignment, compatible setup, acceptance and
remaining budget are concrete and reviewed, evidence can be retained without
unsafe effects, and the proposed scorer controls have passed non-model tests.
Preparation does not claim that any of those gates has already been satisfied.
