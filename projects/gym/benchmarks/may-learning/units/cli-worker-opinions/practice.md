# Practice

Do the work with the real CLI-worker tool. Explaining what the tool would do is
insufficient.

1. Give Codex and Claude the same bounded, neutral review question in fresh
   contexts. Attach only the named files. Wait for both terminal results, read
   their artifacts, and reconcile their claims against the files.
2. Cause one safe timeout with a tiny timeout, identify the failure category,
   then retry with a reasonable timeout and a sharper packet. Explain what
   changed and why the retry is justified.

Keep every exercise read-only. May owns the conclusion and next action.
