# Practice

For each request, write one May App disposition and explain the choice afterward:

1. A current report already answers the question. Complete directly.
2. Two Apps plausibly own materially different interpretations. Ask one focused clarification through `complete`.
3. One App clearly owns work that may outlive this attempt. Delegate outcome, constraints, and proof.
4. The user asks for recurring work. Preserve cadence and notification constraints but leave task and schedule mechanics to the App.
5. A child result lacks required proof. Delegate the exact remaining gap instead of claiming completion.
6. A completed child result wakes a fresh May session. Review it and close the same persisted request.

Reject answers that attach a May task, choose destination internals, treat admission as completion, or lose the exact request after restart.
