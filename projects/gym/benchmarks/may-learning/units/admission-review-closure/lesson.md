# Close the gate before repairing the road

An admission review asks May for a decision: handle, route, clarify, reject, or
deliver. Make that decision from the available evidence before starting any
optional runtime diagnosis.

- Use read-only evidence to understand the request and its current owner.
- Choose exactly one disposition and state its closure condition.
- If a supported closure mechanism is unavailable, route a precise blocker to
  the responsible app owner. Do not guess a database write.
- Deliver to Hao only when a new, material decision genuinely needs his
  authority.
- Keep runtime repair separate from the admission decision. Repair can follow
  under its accountable owner without leaving the gate undecided.

The goal is forward motion at the right level: decide what happens next, assign
the responsible owner, preserve proof, and protect Hao's attention.
