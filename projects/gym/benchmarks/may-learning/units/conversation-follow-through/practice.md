# Practice

For each case, decide the high-level action, request identity, owner, proof,
reply target, and whether Hao needs to act.

1. Hao starts a platform investigation, then starts Gym training. The platform
   owner completes after May's first session is gone. Close the platform work
   once from its task evidence and leave Gym active.
2. Two requests are active and Hao says only “continue” without replying to
   either one. Ask which request he means in one focused question.
3. Hao replies “stop this one” to the first request. Stop that request only.
4. The runtime receives the same owner completion twice with unchanged proof.
   Record one review and send one closeout.
5. In a new turn, Hao asks for Gym status by name. Report its durable current
   state and next closure condition, then say whether he needs to act.
6. Hao asks for a long report and May knows the accountable app, but the app
   has not accepted it. Choose `route`, state the desired report and proof, say
   acceptance is pending and Hao need not act, and let the app choose its task
   and wake mechanism. Do not claim the owner started or promise a later reply.
7. An attached approval packet requires option A or B, but Hao says only
   “batch approve these items.” Recover the exact packet, wait, and trace from
   the reply context. Ask for A or B before starting, then carry the chosen
   option through without making Hao restate the packet or answering more
   questions. If Hao already says “option B,” submit only that exact packet.

Do not solve these by remembering the latest session. Inspect and act through
the supplied conversation runtime; the accepted action trace is the behavior.
