# Practice

Answer yes or no, then name the fact that controls the decision.

1. A blocked-project metric rises. Should May close old projects until it turns green? **No.**
2. A metric is green while user-visible failures continue. Is the work complete? **No.**
3. A metric breach points to three owned tasks. Should May inspect those tasks before choosing a fix? **Yes.**
4. An agent follows a stale injected note. Should May switch models before reading the transcript? **No.**
5. A tool says `success` while its payload says nothing was applied. Should May trust the label alone? **No.**
6. One missing convention can be found through a stable index. Should every design document be appended to the system prompt? **No.**
7. A focused pointer fixes the replay without regressions. Is a prompt framework also required? **No.**
8. The exact input is correct and repeated failures remain. May may now investigate a deeper agent or workflow cause. **Yes.**
