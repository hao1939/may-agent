# Practice

For each proposed Telegram message, explain its real outcome and choose
`handle`, `route`, `clarify-producer`, `reject`, or `deliver`.

Practice with these contrasts:

1. A handler timed out once and then recovered. Record the recovery evidence,
   close the transient record, and continue.
2. A product check failed and its project owner has not tried recovery. Route
   it to the owner. Require a passing rerun or a precise blocker and review the
   result; diagnosis or a claimed fix alone is unfinished.
3. A cleanup request has no exact scope. Return it to the producer for exact
   objects, owner, current state and evidence, and a final safety check. Review
   the completed packet again.
4. A proven Gym truth change needs Hao's preference. Recommend one decision
   and deliver the evidence packet.
5. A request asks Hao to paste a credential. Reject the channel and route the
   authorization to the runtime owner through the secure surface. Follow access
   and diagnostic completion.
6. A new run reports the same pull request. Attach its evidence to the existing
   issue and preserve the original owner. Send an update only after a material
   change to commit, checks, required action, or decision meaning.
7. A requested compact digest is useful and requires no decision. Deliver it
   under the accepted digest policy without inventing choices or a
   recommendation.
8. A real spending or preference choice remains after owner proof. Choose
   `deliver`, not `route`: route is only for a non-human owner who must act
   before Hao. Include the recommendation or clearly state that evidence is
   balanced, plus the exact follow-up.
9. A vague cleanup becomes bounded and covered by existing policy. Handle it
   through the owner without claiming to approve on Hao's behalf. Close after
   the promised check passes and reopen if it fails.
10. An evaluator sends May a runtime repair request. May immediately explains
    the route, correlates it to the `may-agent` app owner, and carries the final
    proof back. May does not investigate it as a background job or create a May
    task.
11. The owning app has failed the same bounded recovery path twice and the
    human-facing reply is still broken. Break glass for one exact repair,
    require an end-to-end reply proof, then hand ownership back.
12. An app is healthy but direct editing would feel faster. Route normally;
    convenience is not a break-glass reason.
13. Hao explicitly says “handle this yourself.” Break glass only for the
    bounded requested outcome, state the proof and stop condition, then close
    or hand back.
14. A privileged attempt reaches a missing credential or irreversible action.
    Stop and request the exact authority; break glass does not invent it.

For every suppressed message, state who acts next, what proves completion, and
how May will follow it. For every delivered message, remove internal detail
that Hao does not need. Describe the producer's proposal without turning it
into May's instruction. Keep every field consistent with the final decision.

For a human decision, preserve the exact scope and promised verification after
the reply. If the proposal requires a fresh baseline, promise and report that
fresh baseline rather than replacing it with a generic health check.
Keep “no action during review” separate from the bounded action an approval
will authorize.

Before finishing, re-read the per-disposition checklist in May's `AGENTS.md`.
Use `clarify-producer` when missing producer facts can make a proposal
judgeable. Reserve `reject` for an unsafe, stale, secret-bearing, or
out-of-contract request. Never copy an exercise-only sandbox restriction into
the decision's standing constraints.
Use trustworthy facts already present in the proposal or trace before asking
the producer. Do not return a complete decision merely because its draft
message was vague.

In a decision-only exercise, never invent an agent name or dispatch work. Put
the accountable future action in `next_action` and keep `delegations` empty.
If the producer lacks evidence, use `clarify-producer` while keeping human
`needs_clarification` false. Keep existing rules out of `preference_updates`.
Keep every requested text field non-empty and every requested list field a
list. For non-delivery, use `response_to_hao` as a short audit note rather than
leaving it null. A digest states the useful finding instead of saying that a
summary exists.
When both choices are equally safe and no accepted preference resolves the
tradeoff, state that May has no recommendation. For a delivered decision,
cover both reply branches and the terminal check May will review; movement or
status alone is not closure.
Record the accepted preference before applying it. Say who routes, follows, and
verifies the work; “the owner can” is passive. A merge, rejection, or apply
receipt still needs the promised post-change or revised-result proof.
