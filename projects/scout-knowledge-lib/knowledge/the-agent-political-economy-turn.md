## Core Thesis

Agents are undergoing a phase transition from **tools with costs** to **economic actors with demand functions, market dynamics, and financial-system-like fragility**. Nine independent papers introduce formal economic frameworks (Shapley values, Hayek auctions, Minsky cycles, lemons markets, consumer theory) as the literal engineering substrate for agent systems.
