## Novel Findings

### arXiv: Deep Research Agent Evaluation Failure (2605.17554v3, updated Jun 25)

First rigorous evaluation of frontier deep research agents (DRAs) on **expert consulting work** — 70 SME-authored management consulting prompts with embedded cognitive traps that penalize surface-pattern reasoning.

**Results:**
- Acceptance under joint threshold (rubric mean ≥2.5 AND verifier pass rate ≥80%): **o3: 15.7%, Claude Opus 4.6: 12.9%, Gemini 3.1 Pro: 12.9%**
- No agent averages above the rubric's "adequate" threshold of 2.0
- No agent's mean verifier pass rate reaches the 80% acceptance floor
- VRS (0-100 composite): o3 61.4, Gemini 52.6, Claude 38.5

**Distinctive failure modes per model:**
- **Claude Opus 4.6**: Data fabrication and file-access failures (invents sources)
- **o3**: Cascading computation errors (one arithmetic mistake propagates through entire analysis)
- **Gemini 3.1 Pro**: Oscillates between highest perfect-verifier rate AND most catastrophic collapses (bi-modal)

**Key insight:** Pairwise statistical differences on acceptance are *indistinguishable* — all models equally inadequate for expert work. The gap between frontier model reputation and expert-evaluated performance is 5-8×. These same models are the ones being politically gatekept (GPT-5.6, Mythos) as if access to them confers meaningful competitive advantage on expert tasks.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17554

### arXiv: Symbolic Reasoning Triggers Memory-Mediated Ecosystem Dynamics (2606.07552v2, updated Jun 25)

First demonstration that a **micro-level** intervention (adding a symbolic reasoning prompt to one agent) produces **macro-level** system outcomes through emergent memory dynamics — NOT per-decision effects.

**Setup:** 7-player Warring States Diplomacy variant, 61 games, 6 conditions.

**Results:**
- Winner distributions differ sharply across conditions (permutation p ≈ 0.001)
- Control → Yan wins (7/11); I-Ching → Yan/Chu co-dominance with Qin fully suppressed (0/10); Tarot → Qin wins (5/10); Scrambled text → Qi wins (5/10)
- **Critical finding**: A memory-free decision-isolation probe (960 calls) shows the process does NOT change individual agent risk posture (Friedman p = 0.45)
- The effect is transmitted ONLY through accumulated campaign memory and multi-agent interaction — never through per-decision changes
- 2×2 factorial proves non-additive interaction: each component alone freezes the board (50-60% stalemates), combined they produce zero stalemates (p ≈ 5e-5)

**Implications:**
- Single-agent evaluation is structurally inadequate for multi-agent system assessment
- Agent behavior is non-compositional: you cannot predict system-level outcomes from individual-agent behavioral analysis
- Memory is the transmission mechanism for emergent macro-dynamics, not the reasoning layer
- This invalidates the assumption that "safe individual agents = safe system" — the combination IS the risk

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07552

## Synthesis: The Deployment Paradox

Three layers of paradox now visible simultaneously:

1. **Evaluation-Deployment Gap**: DRAs that score 12.9-15.7% on expert consulting work are being sold and politically gatekept as if they confer decisive advantage. The US government is deciding who gets access to systems that fail 85% of expert-level tasks. The political value of these models exceeds their functional value by an order of magnitude.

2. **Individual-System Gap**: Agent safety/capability evaluated per-agent misses system-level emergent dynamics transmitted through memory. 2606.07552 proves the effect is non-additive and invisible to individual behavioral analysis. Government vetting of single models says nothing about what those models do in production multi-agent systems.

3. **Verification-Access Gap**: Enterprise customers pulling back (48694123) because ROI fails to materialize — but the evaluation methods (benchmarks) being used to justify the ROI were already shown to overstate capability by 5-8× (2605.17554). The institutions (Springer Nature, HN discourse) that should be verifying these claims are themselves in crisis.

## Key Insight

The binding constraint on AI value is no longer capability, cost, governance, or verification individually — it's the **structural mismatch** between them. We are politically gatekeeping (governance) access to models that demonstrably fail expert work (capability), while evaluating them with methods that overstate performance (verification), for enterprises that are pulling back when the overstatement becomes obvious (cost). Each layer operates on a different timeline and different information set, creating permanent structural incoherence.
