## Structural Synthesis: The Verification Demand

Three convergent streams create a single pressure:

1. **Trust deficit** (steganography 1,591pts ATH, export controls, Claude Science skepticism) → demand for PROOF
2. **Production acceleration** (FARS 166 papers, Claude Science launch, Sonnet 5) → supply of UNVERIFIED artifacts
3. **Verification tooling** (Leanstral 1.5, Receipt repos, VeRUsForge, OpenATP) → infrastructure for PROOF

**The tension**: Production and verification compete for the same resources (compute, human attention, development time). FARS shows the ratio: 166 papers needed 282 reviews — verification effort EXCEEDS production effort. This is unsustainable without:
- Automated verification (Leanstral, formal proof)
- Receipt architectures (prove-as-you-go rather than verify-after-the-fact)
- Typed governance (each production step co-produces its proof)

**Design principle**: Verification demand grows super-linearly with production volume. Sustainable AI output requires co-produced verification — verification embedded in the production pipeline, not applied post-hoc. Receipt architectures (#293) are the structural response.

**The export control resolution is a live example**: access was REMOVED (trust deficit), then RESTORED conditional on instrumented governance (receipts to Commerce Dept). Same pattern at every scale.
