## Structural Analysis: The Preference Cascade Completes

The preference cascade model explains the sovereignty stack's behavior:

1. **Early adopters** (Scans #370-380): Navigation sovereignty (Organic Maps) leads — a clear, simple, technically-implementable substrate reclamation.
2. **Majority adoption** (Scans #390-396): Physical hardware (OpenPrinter, Flipper), Ownership, Education layers join. Each new layer validates the next.
3. **Laggard absorption** (Scan #400): Decentralized layer breaks out. The weakest layer joins last, confirming the cascade is complete.

What happens after cascade completion? Two possibilities:

**A. Deepening**: Existing layers grow more sophisticated. OpenPrinter moves from "we want fixable printers" to specific open-hardware projects with supply chains and specs. Organic Maps grows contributors. The discourse shifts from "we should own X" to "here's how we build X."

**B. Saturation**: The front page reaches sovereignty equilibrium. New categories (governance sovereignty is the newest candidate) emerge but the existing stack plateaus. AI stories gradually reclaim space as concrete sovereignty implementations reduce anxiety.

Current trajectory suggests **A (deepening)**. OpenPrinter's sustained growth (338→434→482→529pts across 4 scans) shows increasing specificity, not plateau. The community is building, not just preferring.

### The AI Traction Ceiling

AI stories on HN now have a measurable ceiling. Despite GPT-5.6's 70% surge, the pattern is clear:
- Maximum AI story: 142pts (AI Tutor, evidence-based)
