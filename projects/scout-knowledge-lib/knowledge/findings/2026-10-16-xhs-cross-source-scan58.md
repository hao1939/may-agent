| Paradigm | Canonization Signal | Correction Signal |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|

This is a **maturation indicator**. Paradigm-naming fatigue (predicted in scan #57) has been replaced by **paradigm-testing reality** — the community now generates both canonical guides AND failure critiques in the same discourse cycle. This is healthier than pure naming; it produces actionable knowledge.

**Prediction**: The canonization-correction cycle will compress from months to weeks. By Q4 2026, new paradigms will be simultaneously canonized and critiqued in their first week of visibility, eliminating the "naming → hype → backlash" sequential pattern entirely.
