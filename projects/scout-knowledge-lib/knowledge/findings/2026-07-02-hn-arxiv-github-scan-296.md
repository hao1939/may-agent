21 dense supervision methods benchmarked BEFORE any training run. Finds simple prompting consistently outperforms recent methods. Training-free quality separation.

**Structural significance**: Evaluation BEFORE production. Counter-mechanism that operates upstream rather than downstream. Resolves the verification-demand super-linearity (#294) by evaluating signals before they produce artifacts.

Eight-stage vulnerability lifecycle: data collection → pretraining → alignment → supply chain → retrieval/memory → prompting → tool/agent execution → deployment. Maps to 8 security objectives. Emphasizes "compositional security" — point defenses don't compose.

**Structural significance**: Counter-mechanism as SYSTEMATIC TAXONOMY rather than point solution. Provides the "periodic table" for agent security.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31639

### MECoBench (2606.31966) — Embodied Collaboration Benchmark

Three key findings: collaboration improves task completion BUT coordination complexity can negate gains; communication is essential; collaboration improves robustness under noise.

**Structural significance**: Quantifies the coordination tax (#287) in embodied settings. More agents help ONLY if communication overhead < collaboration benefit.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31966

## Structural Analysis: The Counter-Mechanism Turn

### What's happening

1. **Velocity matching**: Counter-mechanisms now gain stars/points at comparable rates to production systems (Anti-Autoresearch 66★ vs Synapse 66★ in same week)
2. **Named duality**: Projects explicitly identify as duals/counters (Anti-Autoresearch says "the dual of ARIS")
3. **Category separation**: "Memory firewall" (dent8) vs "memory system" (Synapse) — governance and production are separating into distinct engineering categories
4. **Pre-production evaluation**: QVal evaluates supervision BEFORE training, not after deployment — counter-mechanism moves upstream
5. **Infrastructure independence**: ArXiv itself seeks independence, mirroring the sovereignty pattern in agent systems

### Design principle

**Every production category spawns a symmetric governance category at matched velocity once the field crosses the co-production threshold.** The lag was not intrinsic — it was a phase delay. Once counter-mechanisms are named, starred, and funded at matching rates, governance becomes self-sustaining.

### Why this matters for agent builders

- The "governance gap" (#262, #271) is CLOSING through category-symmetry, not through governance catching up to the same production system
- Agent architectures should design for paired production/verification from inception
- "Proof-of-done" (#295) is the interface between production and its counter-mechanism
