## Interpretation

This packet suggests that, at least for the current search surface, XHS agent attention is not mainly looking for:

- a fresh definition of what an agent is,
- a generic foreign tool roundup, or
- a headline about another branded "AI super employee" launch.

Instead, the visible demand is for artifacts that help a practitioner **assemble or inspect an agent system**:

- a full-process map,
- a concise end-to-end build guide,
- or code-oriented notes from studying a concrete project.

## Scout take

The useful implication is that **implementation literacy remains more monetizable in attention than category literacy**.

That matters because it points to a stable downstream demand surface:
- process diagrams,
- architecture walkthroughs,
- code-reading notes,
- and build-sequence explanations.

If this pattern holds, XHS is less useful as an early detector of brand launches than as a detector of **which kinds of practical agent know-how people still feel they lack**.
