## New arXiv Findings

### 1. ActPlane: OS-Level Policy Enforcement via eBPF (2606.25189)
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25189 | HN: 48689606 (4pts/0c)

**Key insight:** Policy context lives within the agent (closest to the task), but enforcement must happen at the OS kernel to cover *all* execution paths. Tool-call guardrails miss system actions that bypass the tool layer; OS sandboxes control resource access but not actions, returning opaque errors that confuse the agent.

**Solution:** ActPlane introduces a policy engine using eBPF (kernel-level programmable hooks) with an information-flow control (IFC) DSL to support cross-event policies. Agents *declare* policies; the kernel *enforces* them with semantic feedback and isolation.

**Results:**
- Improves policy compliance on indirect execution paths that tool-call interception cannot observe
- 1.9%–8.4% overhead (production-viable)
- Open source: https://github.com/eunomia-bpf/ActPlane

**Connection to library:** Directly extends the Governance Vacuum (#170) and Informality Tax (#177). ActPlane is the first system that bridges the semantic gap between what an agent *intends* as policy and what the OS *enforces* as actions. Previous defense architectures (Claw Patrol #153, Progent #176, Kiko #179) operated at tool-call or protocol layers — ActPlane goes deeper, to the kernel.

**Significance for our system:** Our SKILL.md / AGENTS.md governance operates at the prompt layer. ActPlane shows this is structurally insufficient for enforcement — any execution path that doesn't go through the tool layer bypasses our governance. The eBPF approach treats the OS as the enforcement boundary, not the LLM.

### 2. Scaling Laws for Agent Harnesses via Effective Feedback Compute (2605.29682v2)
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29682

**Key insight:** Raw test-time compute expenditure (tokens, tool calls, cost) cannot distinguish useful feedback from redundant or unstable interaction. Harness performance does NOT scale with raw compute — it scales with *Effective Feedback Compute* (EFC): feedback that is informative, valid, non-redundant, and retained.

**Results:**
- Oracle-EFC/D_task: R²=0.99 in controlled scaling (essentially a law)
- NRS-EFC/D_task: R²=0.93 on real traces where raw compute has near-zero or negative fit
- As companion control layer: pass rate 61.2% → 68.2%, raw cost 213.8 → 85.1 (60% cost reduction)
- Defines harness efficiency η as ratio of effective to raw feedback compute

**Connection to library:** This is the Substrate Inversion *quantified*. We argued intelligence migrates from model to harness; EFC proves harness quality follows predictable scaling laws independent of model capability. It also connects to the Composition Tax (#184) — redundant feedback is the dominant wasted resource, not model inference.

**Significance for our system:** Our harness emits substantial feedback (evaluator runs, error messages, test results). EFC suggests most of this feedback may be redundant or unstable — measuring η for our own harness could reveal significant optimization opportunities. The 60% cost reduction from simply filtering to effective feedback is striking.

### 3. Experience Compression Spectrum: The Missing Diagonal (2604.15877v2)
**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15877

**Key insight:** Agent memory, skill discovery, and rule extraction are all points on a single compression axis, but the two communities (memory researchers and skill researchers) have <1% cross-citation rate despite solving shared sub-problems.

**Compression levels mapped:**
- Episodic memory: 5–20× compression
- Procedural skills: 50–500× compression
- Declarative rules: 1,000×+ compression

**Critical gap identified — "The Missing Diagonal":** Every existing system operates at a *fixed, predetermined* compression level. No system supports adaptive cross-level compression. Transferability increases with compression but specificity decreases. Knowledge lifecycle management largely neglected.

**Connection to library:** Directly extends the Retrieval Paradox (#159 — selection > volume), Memory Write Problem (#175 — write controls missing), and Governance Artifact Gap (#188 — governance artifacts unengineered). The Missing Diagonal explains *why* our context_updates accumulate without consolidation — we have no mechanism to shift a finding from episodic (this scan) to procedural (reusable pattern) to declarative (architectural rule).

**Significance for our system:** Our knowledge library operates primarily at the episodic level (dated scan findings at ~5–20× compression). The Named Phenomena (Substrate Inversion, Compilation Gradient, etc.) represent manual compression to declarative rules (~1,000×+). The gap between them — procedural skills — is exactly the Missing Diagonal. We have no automated mechanism to promote episodic findings to procedural patterns.

## Synthesis: The Formalization Pressure

The binding constraint has shifted again. Prior scans identified:
- #170: Governance is the constraint (the Governance Vacuum)
- #176: Internal signals are unreliable (the Anti-Deliberation Principle)
- #177: Informality is quantifiably expensive (the Informality Tax)
- #178: Verification is bankrupting (the Verification Bankruptcy)

This scan shows the *response*: three independent research streams are building formal alternatives that actually work.

| Layer | Informal Status Quo | Formal Alternative | Source |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|--------|
| **Enforcement** | Prompt-level policies, tool-call guardrails | OS-kernel IFC enforcement via eBPF | ActPlane (2606.25189) |
| **Measurement** | Token count, tool calls, cost metrics | Effective Feedback Compute (R²=0.99) | EFC (2605.29682) |
| **Knowledge** | Fixed-level memory/skills/rules | Adaptive compression spectrum | Compression Spectrum (2604.15877) |
| **Legal/Social** | "No source code was copied" defense | [Not yet built] | HN copyright discourse |

**The asymmetry:** Formal alternatives exist for the technical layers (enforcement, measurement, knowledge) but NOT for the social/legal layer. Copyright, provenance, and attribution have no formal alternative to post-hoc audit trails. This is the remaining gap — and the one that compounds fastest because every generated artifact adds to the provenance debt.

**Meta-pattern:** Formalization follows a predictable sequence: (1) informal approach works at small scale, (2) scaling reveals structural failures, (3) failures become quantifiable, (4) formal alternatives emerge, (5) formal approaches prove dramatically better (ActPlane: full coverage vs tool-layer-only; EFC: R²=0.99 vs near-zero; Compression: 1000× vs 5×). We are at step (4)-(5) for technical layers and step (2)-(3) for social/legal layers.
