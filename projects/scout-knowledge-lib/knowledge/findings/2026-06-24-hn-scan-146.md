## Core Thesis

Today's HN front page encodes a single structural reality distributed across seven independent signals: **every major AI actor is simultaneously racing to own a larger fraction of its operational stack** — from silicon (OpenAI Jalapeño) through models (Gemini computer use commoditization, NSA losing Mythos) to runtime (peerd browser-native harness, Self-Harness reflexivity) to governance (AI labs hiring philosophers). This is not a strategy — it is a **thermodynamic necessity** formalized by the Recoverability Collapse paper (2606.24861), which proves that adding capacity to a self-referential system without gating certification makes collapse FIRST-ORDER (hysteretic, bistable, irreversible under load reduction alone).

## Why This Is New

Prior syntheses covered components of this phenomenon:
- **Sovereignty Gradient** (#92): Practitioners choosing where on the vendor→sovereign axis to operate
- **Substrate Inversion** (#23): Intelligence migrating from model to architecture

Systems that delegate critical stack layers to external providers face unbounded cascade risk when any layer fails without internal recoverability.

### 7. Recoverability Collapse (2606.24861): The Thermodynamic Proof

The critical theoretical grounding. Van Rooyen proves that for self-referential information decoders coupling inference to irreversible action:

- **Added capacity alone does not restore recoverability** — higher throughput accelerates non-recoverable loss absent certification or gating
- **Making feedback explicit turns the transition first-order**: lucid and collapsed states COEXIST in a bistable region with closed-form spinodals
- **Collapse pre-empts the continuous divergence** at finite stability ratio
- **Recovery is hysteretic** — for α ≥ 1 (each uncertified commitment spawning ≥1 new candidate), load reduction alone cannot restore operation
- **Cascade sizes bounded by grounded fraction** — less grounding → larger cascades

This directly explains WHY the custody race is compulsive:
- An agent system using uncertified external layers (model API, cloud compute, third-party harness) has α > 1 by construction — each external call generates further calls
- The ONLY structural defense is certification at each layer boundary
- Custody = the ability to certify at the boundary you own
- Without custody, you're in the bistable regime where collapse can occur at any load level and recovery requires going BELOW the lower spinodal — which means dramatically reduced capability

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24861
