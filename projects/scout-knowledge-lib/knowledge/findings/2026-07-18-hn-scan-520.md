### "What AI did to StackOverflow in a graph" (69↑, rising)
- **URL**: https://data.stackexchange.com/stackoverflow/query/1953768#graph
- **Signal**: Data Explorer query visualizing SO's question/answer volume collapse post-LLM. Empirical graph showing the displacement curve.
- **Relevance**: Concrete data point for the **knowledge-asymmetry** thesis — LLMs absorbed SO's corpus, then diverted the question flow, hollowing the source. - **Connection**: Reinforces the "abstention-decay" pattern — as fewer humans ask/answer, the quality of future corrections declines. The model's training data source is drying up.
