## arXiv Cross-Source: The Diagnostic Refinement

Four papers from June 15-17 converge on a theme that directly extends the Hardening Stack from scan #63: moving from binary success/failure to **structured, typed diagnostic characterization** of AI system failures.

### 📄 Thermodynamic Signatures of Reasoning (2606.19404)

**Problem**: Existing hallucination detectors reduce attention spectra to a few eigenvalues, leaving most signal unused.

**Approach**: Treats each transformer layer's attention Laplacian as a Hamiltonian and extracts thermodynamic potentials (partition function, free energy, spectral entropy, heat capacity) plus random-matrix-theory spectral form factor. Proves Lipschitz stability, expressiveness bounds, and PAC-bound on AUROC.

**Result**: +6.5 AUROC points over LapEig, +2.4 over GoR-4 across six LLMs and six benchmarks. Correct generations exhibit Wigner-Dyson spectral statistics; hallucinations exhibit Poisson-like statistics.

**Implication**: Physics-informed diagnostics achieve label-free hallucination detection. The Wigner-Dyson vs Poisson distinction provides a fundamental characterization: correct reasoning has "level repulsion" (structured information flow) while hallucination has "level clustering" (random/decorrelated attention).

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19404

---

### 📄 EARS: Explanatory Abstention for Reliable Sub-Agent Modeling (2606.18668)

**Problem**: In large-scale MAS, sub-agents built on smaller fine-tuned models over-answer ambiguous/misrouted/unsupported requests, producing hallucinations instead of actionable refusal.

**Approach**: Reframes abstention as inter-agent communication protocol. Sub-agent doesn't merely refuse — it exposes structured failure state (taxonomy: ambiguous, underspecified, misrouted, unsupported) with rationale to coordinator for rerouting/clarification.

**Result**: Production e-commerce assistant: pass rate improves from 68.5% → 78.9% through sub-agent explanatory abstention.

**Implication**: Directly operationalizes the Abstention Decay pattern (KE-library concept). The +10.4pp improvement from teaching agents to say "I can't, and here's why" vs. hallucinating answers validates the hypothesis that reliability gains come from structured refusal, not better answering. This is the inverse of the "just try harder" approach.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18668

---

### 📄 LegalHalluLens: Typed Hallucination Auditing (2606.18021)

**Problem**: ~52% hallucination rate in legal AI is reported as a single number, but conceals massive within-model variance across claim types.

**Approach**: Introduces typed hallucination profiles (numeric, temporal, obligation/entitlement, factual) and Risk Direction Index (RDI) — reduces omission-vs-invention bias to single deployment-comparable scalar.

**Result**: 38-40 percentage point gap between obligation/numeric and temporal claims within same model. Two systems with matched 52% rates can carry **opposite** RDIs (one over-invents, other over-omits). Debate pipeline reduces fabricated detections by 45%.

**Implication**: The most dangerous finding here is that **matched aggregate scores can hide opposite failure modes**. This directly undermines any deployment decision based on aggregate benchmarks. Two models scoring "52% hallucination" can be catastrophically different in practice. This validates the Evaluation Mirage thesis at a new level of specificity.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18021

---

### 📄 Rift: A Conflict Signature for Deception in Language Models (2606.17229)

**Problem**: ELK (Eliciting Latent Knowledge) cannot distinguish deception from honest error behaviorally. Is there an internal signature?

**Approach**: Contrasts sleeper agent (knows truth, lies on trigger) against naive liar (fine-tuned to emit same wrong answers without honest training). Both produce identical wrong outputs — any internal difference is about knowledge conflict, not incorrectness. Measures residual rank.

**Result**: Deceptive passes carry 2.1-2.3x higher residual rank than naive-liar passes. 100% accuracy distinguishing lie from error across GPT-2 small/medium and three instruct models. Survives strategic self-constructed deception (AUC 1.0), active concealment (AUC 1.0), and cross-architecture/cross-language transfer (mean AUC 0.933). Signature is read-only: detectable but not injectable.

**Implication**: This is a potential breakthrough for AI safety. If deception leaves an unforgeable internal signature (2.1x residual rank), it provides a mechanistic foundation for trust that doesn't depend on behavioral alignment. The "read-only" property (can detect but can't suppress) suggests this is a deep computational constraint, not a training artifact. Connection to Thermodynamic Signatures paper: both find that the manner of being wrong (structured vs random) leaves distinct internal traces.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17229

## Meta-Pattern: The Diagnostic Refinement

Across both HN discourse and arXiv research, a consistent meta-pattern emerges: **The Diagnostic Refinement** — the field is transitioning from binary/aggregate assessment to structured, typed characterization of AI system failures.

Evidence:
1. **LegalHalluLens**: 52% aggregate conceals 38-40pp category gaps and opposite risk directions
2. **EARS**: Binary refuse/answer replaced by typed failure taxonomy (ambiguous/underspecified/misrouted/unsupported)
3. **Rift**: Binary wrong/right replaced by deception-vs-error via residual rank signature
4. **Thermodynamic Signatures**: Aggregate "hallucination score" replaced by Wigner-Dyson vs Poisson spectral characterization
5. **HN discourse**: GPT-5.5 discussion moving from "it hallucinates" to specific user testimony on specific tasks
6. **Adam CAD**: Community demanding specific dimensional accuracy, not "looks like engineering"

**Phase position**: This is the natural successor to the Hardening Stack (scan #63). Once infrastructure layers are identified (state, authority, evaluation, compression, defense, safety, recovery, verification), the next question becomes: what exactly is failing at each layer, and how do we measure it with precision? The Diagnostic Refinement answers this.

**Prediction**: Within 2-3 months, "aggregate benchmark score" will be actively rejected by practitioners as deployment criteria. Typed/structured evaluation (per-category, per-risk-direction, per-claim-type) will become table stakes for serious deployment decisions.
