## Synthesis: The Significance of Steady State

Three scans ago (#405-406), the sovereignty stack was volatile — big arrivals, big departures, political governance driving spikes. Now the stack is stable and growing at a uniform ~3% per scan across every pillar. This is structurally more significant than explosive growth, for three reasons:

**1. Steady state proves internalization.** Volatile growth requires catalysts (EU regulations, CEO admissions, security incidents). Steady growth means the community's preference for sovereignty is now baked into browsing behavior. They upvote OpenPrinter because they upvote OpenPrinter, not because a new regulation made them think about printer repair rights.

**2. Concentration increases but doesn't narrow.** Five stories carry 96.7% of the sovereignty stack — but they span five different domains (maps, printers, games, hardware, education). The community didn't narrow to one obsession; it selected one champion per domain and is building behind it.

**3. The cost bridge is working.** "When AI Costs More" (+12.8%) is the fastest-growing story on the page in relative terms, and it directly connects sovereignty to AI by providing an economic argument for local-first infrastructure. When it crosses 100pts — imminent — the sovereignty stack will formally incorporate an economic rationale for the first time.

**The front page has reached dynamic equilibrium.** Not stasis — everything is still growing — but equilibrium: the same stories, in roughly the same order, each slightly larger than before. The debate is over. The building continues.
