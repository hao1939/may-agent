**Key structural insight from 零点未来 video** (129❤, 236 collects — unusually high collect-to-like ratio = reference material):

Agent-Ontology interaction decomposed into **five layers**:
1. **Context Layer**: System auto-injects context (current objects, related docs, fixed background)
2. **Query Layer**: Agent actively queries objects and relationships (filter, aggregate, trace)
3. **Logic Layer**: Calls wrapped business functions (risk calculation, recommendation, optimization)
4. **Action Layer**: Executes governed business changes (high-risk requires human confirmation, low-risk auto-executes)
5. **Governance Layer**: Cross-cutting — permissions, audit, security, anti-hallucination

**Core thesis**: "Ontology 可以理解为 Agent 的'操作系统'" (Ontology = agent OS). The agent doesn't directly operate on data; it operates *through* the ontology's governed semantic layer.

**Connection to Platform Purge**: Consumer agents lacked this governed semantic layer. They operated directly on user requests without the mediation of ontology-level governance. The Ontology-as-OS thesis explains *why* consumer agents can't easily comply with regulation: there's no governance layer to audit.

**Connection to Scan #68 enterprise landing**: The "帮中型公司落地agent" pitfall series (1,282❤) implicitly describes building these ontology layers without naming them. The ontology discourse provides the conceptual framework for what enterprise deployers are doing pragmatically.

## 4. Memory-as-OS Architecture: EverMemOS and the Four-Layer Model (EXTENDS)

**Signal strength: Medium** (11❤, 14 collects)

| Post | Author | ❤ | Signal |
|------|--------|---|--------|
| "AI Agent的记忆应该像操作系统" | Ro'Ze | 11 | EverMemOS four-layer memory architecture |

**Architecture proposed** (EverMemOS, arXiv 2601.02163):
- **Working Memory** (CPU registers) → current task processing
- **Short-term Memory** (RAM) → recent interaction history
- **Long-term Memory** (disk) → completed task experience and knowledge
- **Persistent Memory** (cloud) → cross-session core knowledge and preferences

**Key design decisions**:
- Each memory unit is **structured**: 5 fields (goal, context, action, result, reflection) — not raw text
- **Self-organizing + garbage collection**: frequently-referenced memories auto-promote; stale conclusions auto-recycle; contradictory memories resolved by confidence scoring
- **Performance**: stable at 100+ steps while RAG/raw-text degrades after 50 steps; irrelevant retrieval reduced 50%+

**Connection to Scan #68**: This is the constructive counterpoint to the "self-evolution" backlash (388❤ for "求求了，你这个Agent真不叫'自我进化'！"). EverMemOS distinguishes *organization* from *evolution*: the memory system self-maintains but doesn't self-modify its architecture. This may be the precision the community demanded.

**Connection to Anthropic "4 md files"**: Scan #68 noted Anthropic's "4 md files are enough" (2,677❤) as a simplicity counter. EverMemOS sits between the extremes: structured but not grandiose, systematic but not claiming self-evolution.

## Synthesis: Regulatory Partitioning as Ecosystem Architecture

Scan #69 reveals that the "bifurcation event" identified in Scan #68 is actually a **regulatory partitioning** — deliberate zoning of the AI agent ecosystem:

| Zone | Governance Model | Status | Example |
|------|-----------------|--------|---------|
| **Consumer-facing** | 拟人化互动 regulation | Shutting down by Jul 15 | 豆包 agents, 千问 user-built agents |
| **Developer platform** | Self-governed (Coze TOS) | Preserved | Coze, Qwen B2B API |
| **Open-source** | Community-governed | Accelerating | DeerFlow, UI-TARS-desktop, Ruflo |
| **Enterprise** | Ontology-governed | Growing | Agent-Ontology 5-layer model |
| **Interoperability** | State standards (7 GB) | Just published | 智能体互联 national standards |

The partitioning is not chaotic — it's architecturally coherent. Each zone has a different governance model suited to its risk profile:
- **Consumer**: highest risk (anthropomorphic interaction with minors → 虚拟人 concern) → strictest regulation
- **Enterprise**: medium risk → self-governance through ontology layers
- **Developer/open-source**: lowest regulatory risk → unrestricted
- **Inter-agent**: systemic risk → state standards for identity and interaction

**Key insight**: The Ontology-as-OS thesis (#2) and the National Standards (#3) are two sides of the same infrastructure. Ontology provides *internal* agent governance (what an agent can do within a system). National standards provide *external* agent governance (how agents identify, discover, and interact across systems). Together they constitute the governance substrate for the regulated zones.
