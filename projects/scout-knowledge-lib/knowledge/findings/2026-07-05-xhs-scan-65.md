### The Kill Chain:
```
Regulation Published → 拟人化暂行办法 (AI Personification Measures)
    ↓
Enforcement Date Set → July 15, 2025
    ↓
Platform Response → 豆包 (ByteDance) + 千问 (Alibaba) shutdown consumer agents
    ↓
User Backlash → "请解决问题，而非让用户买单" (83❤)
    ↓
Market Restructuring → Only compliance-ready platforms survive
```

**Critical correction to Scan #64**: The regulation targets **AI personification/anthropomorphic interaction specifically**, not agents in general. This means:
- Consumer companion/emotional AI agents → killed (clear regulatory target)
- Coding/productivity agents (Claude Code, etc.) → unaffected (not personification)
- Enterprise infrastructure agents → unaffected (not consumer-facing personification)

The regulation has a **companion bias** — it specifically addresses AI acting as human-like partners, emotional companions, and parasocial relationship services. The "全链路审核" (full-chain audit) requirement is one mechanism within this broader regulation.

**"表面退，暗处拆"** (22❤) — the most sophisticated community read: platforms are using the regulation as cover to restructure. Consumer agents were already unprofitable; the regulation gives a face-saving exit. The deeper move is repositioning agent infrastructure for B2B.

## 2. WeChat 小微 Launch — Counter-Signal (1,450❤)

**Signal strength: Very High** (1,450❤)

**Post**: "微信小微终于来了，体感超预期！！" (@数字生命卡兹克, 1,450❤)

While ByteDance and Alibaba are shutting down consumer agents, **Tencent is launching** its WeChat agent 小微 (XiaoWei). At 1,450❤, this is the highest engagement new platform launch observed in recent scans.

**Structural reading**: Tencent's timing is not coincidental. Three possible interpretations:
1. **Compliance confidence**: Tencent believes it can meet the 拟人化暂行办法 requirements (WeChat's existing moderation infrastructure provides compliance advantage)
2. **Distribution monopoly**: WeChat's embedded position means agents reach users inside an already-governed platform, reducing standalone compliance burden
3. **Competitive vacuum**: With competitors exiting, entry timing is strategically optimal

**Connection to market structure**: The regulation creates a **compliance moat**. Platforms with existing moderation infrastructure (WeChat, WeChat Work) can absorb the compliance costs that killed standalone agent platforms. The result: agent market consolidates toward platform-embedded agents.
