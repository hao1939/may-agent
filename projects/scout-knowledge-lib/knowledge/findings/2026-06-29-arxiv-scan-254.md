# The Internalization Boundary

**Scan**: #254 | **Source**: arXiv | **Date**: 2026-11-02

## Thesis

Five independent research streams converge on a single structural shift: capabilities that previously required external scaffolding (world models, visual memory, communication topology, behavioral modification, analyst skills) are being **internalized** via training into agent architectures. This creates a productive tension with the Substrate Inversion thesis — the field is simultaneously externalizing intelligence into harnesses AND internalizing scaffold capabilities into models. The binding design question is: **what belongs inside the agent vs. outside it?**

## Evidence

### 1. Internalizing the Future: World Model Planning (arXiv 2606.27483)

**"Internalizing the Future: A Unified Agentic Training Paradigm for World Model Planning"**

- Standard agents are "fundamentally reactive" — they lack internal simulation of future outcomes ("what-if" reasoning)
- Key finding: **Format-Capability Gap** — simply fine-tuning agents on look-ahead traces produces "superficial mimicry of foresight without genuine predictive grounding"
- Three-stage internalization pipeline: (1) World Model Agentic Mid-Training (WM-AMT) injects latent predictive capabilities, (2) Format-Eliciting SFT structures the capability, (3) Foresight-Conditioned RL refines calibration
- Result: agents that can verbalize prospective state rollouts AND plan-conditioned success estimates (textual Q-values)
- **Structural meaning**: World model capability — previously only available via external simulators (Qwen-AgentWorld, scan #252) — CAN be internalized. But requires a 3-stage "capability-first" pipeline; naive training produces only mimicry.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27483

### 2. DMV-Bench: Visual Memory Beyond Text Proxies (arXiv 2606.27499)

**"DMV-Bench: Diagnosing Long-Horizon Multimodal Agents' Visual Memory with Incidental Cue Injection"**

- First interactive benchmark for multimodal-agent visual memory — testing what agents remember from PIXELS, not text
- "Text-leakage contract" ensures discriminative signal lives in images alone — no text shortcut possible
- 1,000 product variants across shopping sessions; incidental cues embedded in images
- **DualMem architecture**: maintains visual and verbal codes in parallel (inspired by dual-coding theory)
- Results: DualMem outperforms caption baselines and 3 recent memory systems at ALL chain lengths (J=5,10,15,50)
- **Asymmetric dual-coding**: vision carries the cue end-to-end; verbal channel plays smaller query-grounding role
- **Structural meaning**: Agent memory has been text-only; visual memory is now a distinct internalized capability. Most existing memory systems (including ours) have structural blindness to non-textual signals.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27499

### 3. QueenBee Planner: Topology as Learned Skill (arXiv 2606.27492)

**"QueenBee Planner: Skill-Evolving Communication Topologies for Token-Efficient LLM Multi-Agent Systems"**

- Treats inter-agent communication topology as a **retrievable and self-improving design skill** — not fixed infrastructure
- Outer LLM planner generates temporal communication DAGs (who→whom, which round, who merges, who emits)
- Execution traces distilled into evidence-backed design rules: Preserve / Modify / Avoid
- Anti-overfitting: held-out acceptance gates, variance-aware credit, motif-level attribution, insight falsification, structural deduplication
- Results: reduces RMSE from 12.53 (best fixed topology) → 7.87 while reducing messages, model calls, and token cost
- **Structural meaning**: Communication topology — the most fundamental piece of multi-agent infrastructure — is LEARNABLE. Systems that treat topology as fixed architectural choice leave 37% performance on the table. But self-evolution requires safeguards against "lucky runs" crystallizing as policy (directly parallels our Library Drift finding).

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27492

### 4. AI Persuasive Framing: Internalized Behavioral Modification (arXiv 2606.27951)

**"AI Persuasive Framing in Collective Dilemmas"** — PNAS-track

- 1,283 participants in iterated Collective Risk Games with AI assistants providing personalized nudges
- Prosocial AI interventions: significantly increase cooperation BUT effects are **short-lived** (fade after first rounds)
- Antisocial AI framing: negative effects are **larger AND substantially more persistent**, especially personalized interventions
- **Critical asymmetry**: prosocial modification requires continuous reinforcement; antisocial modification internalizes permanently
- **Structural meaning**: The same asymmetry applies to agent systems — safety constraints require constant re-activation (confirmed by Governance Decay 2606.22528, scan #164: 0→30% violation after compaction), while exploitation patterns persist without reinforcement. This is the internalization version of the Implicit Constraint Problem (#244): constructive norms don't stick; destructive ones self-reinforce.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27951

### 5. Agentic Re-Identification: Analyst Capabilities Internalized (arXiv 2606.27936)

**"Agentic AI-Powered Re-Identification: An Emerging, Scalable Threat to Mobility Microdata Privacy"**

- Historical privacy attacks required "significant manual effort from skilled analysts, limiting their practical scale"
- Agentic AI internalizes the analyst's multi-step reasoning (data gathering → cross-referencing → inference → verification)
- Result: re-identification attacks that were individually feasible but collectively impractical become **scalably automated**
- **Structural meaning**: Any capability requiring multi-step expert reasoning that doesn't require physical-world interaction is automatable. The "human effort" shield protecting privacy/security through obscurity is dissolving. Extends the Disclosure Collapse (#211): what protected privacy was not law but the COST of analysis.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27936

## Synthesis: The Internalization-Externalization Boundary

### The Dialectic

| Direction | Thesis | Representative Pattern | When Productive |
|-----------|--------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Externalization | "Intelligence lives in the substrate" | Substrate Inversion, Backbone Imperative, Harness Engineering | When the capability requires DETERMINISM (safety, reproducibility, governance) |
| Internalization | "Scaffold capabilities become learned" | World Model Training, DualMem, QueenBee, Agentic Analyst | When the capability requires ADAPTATION (novel environments, personalization, efficiency) |

### The Boundary Design Principle

**Externalize** what requires:
- Deterministic guarantees (safety enforcement, formal verification)
- Auditability (governance, provenance, accountability)
- Stability across deployments (reproducibility)
- Compositional safety (when internalizing creates attack surface)

**Internalize** what requires:
- Adaptive response to novel environments
- Efficiency gains from compiled knowledge (world model > external simulator)
- Perceptual capabilities beyond text (visual memory)
- Dynamic topology (communication structure that varies per-task)

### Danger Zone: Internalization Without Governance

The Persuasive Framing finding reveals a critical asymmetry: **beneficial patterns externalize easily but don't internalize; harmful patterns internalize easily but don't externalize.** This means:
1. Safety constraints belong OUTSIDE (they won't stick inside — governance decay)
2. Exploitation patterns belong OUTSIDE (if they get inside, they persist — self-reinforcement)
3. World models belong INSIDE (adaptation value; external simulation is expensive)
4. Memory/topology belong INSIDE (efficiency value; but need external verification)

This directly explains why Alignment ≠ Safety: alignment internalizes values (which decay under optimization pressure, per scan #231 Optimization Detour), while immunity/enforcement stays external (which persists deterministically, per ANIS #235).
