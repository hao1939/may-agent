## 10. Meta-Analysis: The Anthropic Cycle

This scan cycle reveals a **narrative concentration event** around Anthropic.
Four independent critical angles have converged:

1. **The Gatekeeper Paradox** (Safety Superpower): Safety beliefs genuinely
   held → deployed as competitive moat → structurally contradictory → doomed
   by open-weight replication.
2. **The Dual-Use Impossibility** (Fable 5): Capability IS danger. Fix IS
   exploit. No intent filter can resolve this.
3. **The Displacement Theatre** (Claude Corps): Company builds displacement
   tech, then offers "corps" for displaced workers. Literary parallel to
   Vonnegut's dystopia.
4. **The Political Attack** (White House): Government uses company's own
   safety claims as weapon against it (ITAR, export controls).

These four narratives reinforce each other: the safety claims enable the
political attack, the dual-use impossibility undermines the safety claims,
the displacement theatre reveals the human cost the safety claims mask.

**Prediction: "Anthropic contradictions" becomes a named narrative cluster
within 2-3 scans, similar to how "sovereignty stack" crystallized.**
