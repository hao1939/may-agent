## Synthesis

**The Confidence-Accuracy Inversion** emerges as this scan's key signal: empirical proof that AI advice degrades human judgment while inflating confidence. This directly undermines the "human-in-the-loop as safety net" assumption. Combined with Context Window Deflation (Codex shrinking context) and infrastructure hardening (Claude Code on Rust), the pattern is: the industry is retreating from maximalist promises toward bounded, hardened, mechanically-verified systems.

The oversight model requires redesign: not human review of AI output, but mechanical verification that doesn't depend on human calibration.
