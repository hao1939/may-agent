The sovereignty lifecycle enters a new phase: **organized user resistance**. A petition-style post demanding reversal of the July 15 agent shutdown reaches 198❤ ("立即取消7月15日下架豆包智能体功能的决定"). **The Regulatory Resistance** names this moment: the community has passed through shock (Scan #74), scramble (Scan #75), and acceleration (Scan #76), and now enters organized pushback — even as the structural forces that caused the regulation continue to operate.
