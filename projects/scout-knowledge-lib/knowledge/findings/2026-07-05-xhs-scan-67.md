# XHS Scan #67: The Consolidation Turn (2026-07-05)

## Theme: The Community Consolidates

After the Normalization Plateau (#64), Regulatory Kill Chain (#65), and Engineering Turn (#66), the XHS Chinese AI community has entered a **consolidation phase**. The characteristic behaviors are: building theoretical foundations, mass-educating, optimizing costs, and resetting frameworks. The community is no longer discovering new paradigms — it is organizing, teaching, and economizing what it already has.

## 1. Theoretical Unification: Action Space Theory + Cybernetics Grounding (NEW)

**Signal strength: Medium-High** (combined ~1,800❤)

Two posts by 持续学习妹妹 introduce **Action Space theory** as a unifying framework for the Harness concept zoo:
- "基于Action Space理论统一Harness体系概念" (22❤) — attempts to reconcile Loop Engineering, Harness Engineering, Skill Engineering, and Context Engineering under a single theoretical model.
- "从Action Space 角度理解Agent Harness" (12❤) — companion post applying the framework.

This pairs with the still-resonating "Harness其实就是控制论" (1,739❤ by 碳基智) — the argument that all agent orchestration is fundamentally cybernetics (feedback loops, error correction, goal-seeking under uncertainty).

**What this signals**: The paradigm-naming fatigue (observed in Scan #64) is triggering **consolidation into meta-theories**. When the community exhausts its appetite for new terms (Loop Engineering, Skill Engineering, etc.), it starts looking for unified frameworks that subsume them all. Action Space theory and cybernetics are two candidate foundations.

**Prediction**: At least one of these meta-theories will gain >200❤ traction within 3 scans as concept fatigue pushes demand for unification.
