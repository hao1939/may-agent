## Sovereignty Acceleration — Structural Analysis

The sovereignty stack is not just growing — it's **accelerating across all layers simultaneously**. This is new. Previous scans showed individual layers breaking out (OpenPrinter in #396, Ownership in #393) while others plateaued. Scan #399 shows all layers growing at 8-62% simultaneously.

The acceleration has a structural cause: sovereignty stories on HN feed on each other. A reader who upvotes Organic Maps is predisposed to upvote OpenPrinter, which is predisposed to upvote "it's about ownership." The sovereignty stack functions as a **preference cascade** — each story normalizes the next, lowering the activation threshold for upvoting sovereignty content.

This predicts:
- The stack will continue accelerating until it saturates HN's front page capacity (currently occupying 8-10 of 30 slots, ~30%).
- New sovereignty layers will emerge (governance sovereignty is the newest, at 95pts).
- AI stories will only break through if they serve a sovereignty function (cost reduction, governance critique, evidence-based education).

The deeper implication: **HN's front page has become a sovereignty index**. It doesn't measure what's technically interesting; it measures what the community wants to own, control, and understand at the substrate level. This is the Resolution Engine (#380) operating at the community level — the community is resolving its relationship with technology by reclaiming substrates.
