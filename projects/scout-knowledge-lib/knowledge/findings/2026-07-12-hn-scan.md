## Pattern Analysis

**The Backlash Is Not Anti-Technology — It's Anti-Displacement**

Three stories (175pts "Stop Telling Me", 46pts Claude quality regression, 30pts "support group") form a coherent signal: experienced developers feel their expertise is being devalued not by the technology itself but by the *social pressure* to defer to it. The frustration targets:
1. Loss of conversational depth ("just ask an LLM" kills discussion)
2. Quality regression breaking trusted workflows
3. Identity threat to craft-oriented engineers

This is the same pattern seen in every automation wave — the backlash isn't Luddite, it's about loss of agency and community norms shifting faster than individual adaptation.

**MCP Governance Stack Emerging**

Three MCP stories in one scan (governance illusion critique, sandbox auditing, stealth evasion) indicate the protocol is entering its "security reckoning" phase. The pattern: protocol gains adoption → security researchers probe it → governance products appear → standards harden. MCP is in phase 2-3 transition.

**Agent-First Interface Design**

FableCut (98pts) represents a new product category: tools designed primarily for agent consumption with human oversight as secondary. This inverts decades of HCI — the "user" is now the LLM, and the human is the supervisor. Expect more "agent-drivable" products in creative tooling.
