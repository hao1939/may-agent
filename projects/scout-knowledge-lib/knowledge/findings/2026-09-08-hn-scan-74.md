# HN Scan #74: The Cost Proprioception Gap

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Source:** HN (Algolia API), GitHub (API), arXiv  
**Scan type:** Cross-source synthesis  

---

## Core Finding

A structural pattern emerges from connecting three spectacular agent failure stories, a wave of budget-cap tooling on GitHub, and academic work on policy-adherent execution: **agents lack cost proprioception** — they have no internal mechanism for sensing their own resource consumption, no negative feedback signal when approaching damaging levels, and no metabolic analog that correlates effort with useful work.

This is distinct from our existing Token Economics Trap (which addresses budget *prediction*) and the Coordination Tax (which addresses overhead *growth*). The Cost Proprioception Gap names the *architectural absence* that makes both of those pathologies possible: agents don't feel expensive.

### Stream 3: Academic Responses (arXiv, June 2026)

Two papers address the deeper structural question:

**LedgerAgent (2606.20529)**: Introduces a separate "ledger" that tracks task state and renders it back into the agent's prompt. Key insight: standard agents "reconstruct the relevant states from the prompt each time they decide what to do next" — they are *amnesiac about their own state*, including resource state. LedgerAgent provides a form of structured self-awareness.

**Sovereign Execution Brokers (2606.20520)**: "Production mutation authority should not reside inside non-deterministic reasoning processes." SEB separates proposal, admission, and execution — externalizing the cost-control function into an enforcement boundary that the agent cannot bypass.

**Caching for Dollars, Not Hits (2606.20539)**: Even caching infrastructure suffers from the same structural error: optimizing for hit-rate (the easy-to-measure metric) instead of dollar-cost (the actual objective). The paper proves LRU's dollar-regret rises with miss-cost dispersion (Spearman ρ=0.87). This validates the general principle: systems without cost-aware feedback optimize for the wrong objective.

## The Biological Analogy

Living organisms have proprioception (body position awareness), nociception (pain → stop signal), and metabolic feedback (fatigue → reduced capacity). These provide:

1. **Proportionality sensing**: Effort scales with reward signal
2. **Damage detection**: Stop before irreversible harm
3. **Resource budgeting**: Fatigue prevents total depletion
4. **Attention allocation**: Don't maintain behavior that isn't working

Agents have **none** of these. An agent:
- Doesn't know how much it has spent (no proprioception)
- Doesn't reduce effort when approaching limits (no fatigue)
- Doesn't stop when doing damage (no pain)
- Maintains failing behavior indefinitely (no adaptation signal)

The GitHub fuse-box wave = adding a smoke detector to a house with no fire suppression system. Necessary but insufficient.
