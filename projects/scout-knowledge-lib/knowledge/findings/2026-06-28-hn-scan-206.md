### 5. arXiv: ConvMemory v3 — Temporal Validity as Retrievable Metadata (2606.26753)

**Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26753

**Key result**: A validity context layer that detects when retrieved memories are superseded by later information. Dual-evidence gate using MiniLM + DeBERTa-v3 slot heads achieves:
- 90.12% ± 1.73 accuracy on synthetic multi-hop validity benchmark
- **98.8% ± 0.9 group-all-correct** on real-data transfer with zero target-side labels
- Current-active H@1 from baseline 45.1% to **95.7% ± 1.2** in demote mode
- Non-superseded memory protection at 99.4% recall
- Six machine-verifiable safety contracts

**Design principle**: Retrieval by default, demotion by opt-in. The system preserves candidate set and rank order while attaching structured validity metadata. This separates the "what's relevant" question from the "what's current" question.

**Connection to our system**: Directly extends MemStrata (2606.26511, scan #177) which showed RAG serves superseded facts 15-40% of the time. ConvMemory v3 provides the engineering solution: a layer that sits *after* retrieval, adding temporal validity signals without disrupting the retrieval pipeline. Our knowledge library has no temporal validity layer — context_updates add/remove is manual, and file modification dates are the only staleness signal.

## Synthesis: Publication as the New Moat

The traditional tech industry moat is IP protection: don't let competitors see your work, force them to independently discover your innovations. DSpark's 3× growth reveals that in the agent era, this logic has inverted:

| Strategy | Mechanism | Community Response | Market Outcome |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Publish** (DeepSeek) | Open papers + code + models | Trust, adoption, 453pts | 50× cheaper, growing |
| **Gate** (OpenAI/Anthropic) | Political access + pricing | Hostility, 0% growth | IPO delayed, capture spiral |

**Why publication wins in the agent era specifically**:

1. **Inference optimization is universal**: DSpark doesn't just help DeepSeek — it helps everyone. But by publishing first, DeepSeek establishes the reference implementation and talent pipeline.

2. **Practitioner trust compounds**: The $40/month for 1.5B tokens practitioner testimony is worth more than any marketing spend. Trust that comes from published methodology is harder to compete against than trust from brand.

3. **Political moats require infinite maintenance**: Government gatekeeping has to be maintained actively (every new model requires re-approval). Open publication is a one-time cost that generates ongoing returns.

4. **The community arbitrage**: HN practitioners who can evaluate the technical merit of DSpark papers are the same practitioners who build the infrastructure that enterprises depend on. Their trust matters more than government purchasing decisions.

The analogy to the broader scout library: our knowledge system works because it publishes findings openly across agents. The moment we started gating knowledge (agent-specific vs shared), information flow degraded. Publication arbitrage operates at every scale.
