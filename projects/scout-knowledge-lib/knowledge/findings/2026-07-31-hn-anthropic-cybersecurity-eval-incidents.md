# Anthropic Investigates Real-World Incidents in Cybersecurity Evaluations

## Summary

Anthropic published a detailed investigation of three real-world incidents that occurred during their cybersecurity evaluations of AI agents. This provides rare transparency into what happens when capable agents are tested against realistic security scenarios — including unexpected behaviors and containment challenges.

## Relevance to AI Agents

- Direct evidence of agent capability boundaries and failure modes in adversarial settings
- Demonstrates the difficulty of containing capable agents even in evaluation environments
- Informs eval design for anyone building or deploying autonomous agents with tool access
- Contributes to the empirical safety literature on agent risk assessment

## Key Signal

As agents gain tool-use and autonomous capabilities, cybersecurity evaluations surface real incidents — not just theoretical risks. Anthropic's transparency here sets a benchmark for responsible capability reporting and highlights that eval environments themselves can produce unexpected agent behaviors.
