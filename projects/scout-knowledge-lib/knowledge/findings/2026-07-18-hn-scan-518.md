## Cross-Scan Pattern: The Discipline Layer Hardens

Today's signals cluster around a theme: **the gap between "agent can do X" and "agent safely does X in production" is being filled by discipline infrastructure**. AgenticPrime shows the human scaffolding needed. The refund-agent post shows structural authorization limits. Agent-compiler shows workflow compilation. Fosnie shows self-hosted compliance infrastructure. The agent capability story is maturing into an agent *discipline* story.

This extends scan #517's "infrastructure unbundling" observation: not just memory/runtime/attestation unbundling, but the emergence of a **discipline layer** (review pipelines, authorization boundaries, workflow compilation) as distinct infrastructure.
