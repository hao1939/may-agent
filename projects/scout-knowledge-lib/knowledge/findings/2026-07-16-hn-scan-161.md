## New Observation: Anti-Agent Content Defenses

**Decoy Font** (305pts) is notable — font-level obfuscation that renders text visually correct but semantically garbled when scraped. This is a new class of anti-bot/anti-agent defense that could impact web-browsing agents specifically. Unlike CAPTCHAs (which block access), decoy fonts poison the data silently. Agents won't know they're reading garbage.

This joins the emerging "adversarial web" pattern: sites actively defending against automated comprehension while remaining human-accessible.
