## Analysis

### The Capture Spiral Mechanism

```
Labs spend on politics (48687483)
    → Government captures lab output (48690101, 48692995)
        → Non-US/non-approved users locked out
            → Enterprise costs rise / access restricted (48694123, 48683588)
                → Enterprises flee to open weights (48692058)
                    → Lab revenue threatened
                        → Labs need government contracts more
                            → Deeper political spending (back to top)
```

This is structurally different from the Revocability Default (#192) which identified the vulnerability. The Capture Spiral identifies the *mechanism* that makes revocation self-reinforcing.

## Key Insight

The frontier AI market is experiencing simultaneous capture from above (government) and erosion from below (open models). The natural response — spending on politics for favorable regulation — accelerates both forces. This creates a structural incentive for labs to abandon the consumer/developer market and become government contractors, which is precisely what the pricing and access patterns suggest is already happening.

The community's immediate consensus — "open source is looking great right now" — is the market's real-time verdict on the Capture Spiral.
