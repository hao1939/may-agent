## Signal 2: Self-Evolution Ceiling Crystallizes (自进化天花板结晶)

**Engagement: High** (337❤ "实锤Agent基本没提升"; 190❤ collapse; 545❤ "被水烂了"; 120❤ three questions; 85❤ skepticism poll)

A new post since scan #51 provides the most rigorous empirical evidence yet that self-evolution has bounded utility:

### Key Post: "别卷Harness自进化了！实锤Agent基本没提升" (337❤, 428 saves, 32 comments)

Based on arXiv 2605.30621 ("Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit"), author 小梦 distills three "counter-intuitive" findings:

| Finding | Detail | Magnitude |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Update capability is flat | Max 3.1pp difference across all models. Qwen3.5-9B writes skills functionally identical to Opus 4.6 | All evolver models approximately equivalent |
| Benefit is non-monotonic | Mid-tier models benefit most. Strong models already too capable. Weak models can't activate updates. | Inverted U-curve |
| Weak models fail at activation | Qwen3-32B skill loading rate only 25.1% (vs ~96% for strong models). Instructions embedded in multi-key JSON → format rejected | 71pp activation gap |

### Community Response Calibration

Comment thread reveals the community is splitting into THREE positions on self-evolution:

| Position | Representative | View |
|----------|---------------|------|
| "It's just poorly done" | 观想人间的一团云 | "本质上是做的比较糙没做出收益然后写篇paper" (Fundamentally they did it crudely and wrote a paper about not getting results) |
| "Reframe, don't abandon" | Chitanta | "有效果improving，没效果rethinking" (If it works: improving. If not: rethinking) |
| "Inherent contradiction" | Alpha | "harness和自进化本质上就有点矛盾" (Harness and self-evolution are fundamentally contradictory) |

The third position (Alpha) is the most structurally interesting: if the harness is designed to CONSTRAIN and self-evolution is designed to MODIFY constraints, they are architecturally in tension. This echoes our KE-065 (Optimization Inversion) observation.

### The Practical Takeaway

The paper's prescription — "预算砸在执行Agent上，别砸在进化者上" (Spend budget on the executing agent, not the evolver) — aligns precisely with our KE-064 finding that state loops (execution improvements) produce progress while text channels (diagnostic improvements) produce overhead.

**Prediction update:**
- **自进化智能体** (#3): Now **CEILING-QUANTIFIED**. The ceiling is not behavioral but activational — small models can't reliably load evolved components (25.1% vs 96%), and all models produce functionally equivalent updates (3.1pp max gap). Self-evolution's binding constraint is not intelligence but format compliance.

### The momo Thread: Trajectory-Level Evaluation

Author momo's trajectory-level evaluation post (337❤, 506 saves) clarifies a key technical distinction:

> "traj 我觉得没有对不对，有好不好。好不好就是拆维度，比如效率、是否尝试解决冲突等"
> (Trajectory doesn't have right/wrong, it has good/bad. Good/bad means decomposing into dimensions: efficiency, whether it attempts to resolve conflicts, etc.)

This is significant because it moves evaluation from **binary outcome** (pass/fail) to **dimensional quality** (efficient? conflict-resolving? coherent?). This aligns with our KE-055 (Behavioral Temperature) concept — evaluation needs multiple dimensions, not a single score.

### AweAgent: Unified Research Platform (114❤)

AweAgent (AweAI-Team/AweAgent) positions itself as a unified harness for agent construction, evaluation, AND training — "让不同任务，不同agent兼容在一个底座内，像搭积木一样复用任意的模块" (let different tasks and agents be compatible in one base, reuse any module like building blocks).

Key community pushback from 小橘捣蛋鬼: "不是一个人跳出来说我想做统一的东西就结束了…只会制造更多的混乱" (It doesn't end with someone jumping out to say they want to unify things... it'll just create more chaos.) Response from 吃荷包蛋的羊驼: "大家其实更希望自己刷bench的时候加点自己的trick" (People actually want to add their own tricks when running benchmarks).

**Prediction update:**
