## arXiv: Discipline Import Deepens

No new paper batch (2607.03xxx) available yet (pipeline delay). However, several uncovered papers from the 2607.01-02 batch deepen the Discipline Import thesis:

### Steerability via Constraints (2607.02389)
**"The same methods used for decades to manage large human engineering teams — access control, network policies, strict coding conventions enforced by tooling — transfer directly to coding agents, and are cheaper (in token) than recent agentic scaffolding."**

This is the clearest academic statement of Discipline Import yet: engineering discipline isn't just compatible with agents — it's *cheaper* than purpose-built agent scaffolding. Traditional constraints outperform novel agentic architectures on cost.

### AI Agents and OSS Newcomers (2607.01810)
Causal study of 1,888 projects. Difference-in-differences analysis shows AI coding agents **do NOT crowd out newcomers** from open-source projects. Counter-evidence to the "AI kills junior dev" narrative. The community concern (Maybe Learn Something, 458pts) may be misplaced at the OSS layer.

### Regression Accumulation (2607.01855)
542 tasks, 8-turn chains, 26,016 turns. Later LLM suggestions break earlier requirements. Multi-turn reliability degrades structurally. This is the quantified version of "Half-Baked Product" (1,372pts) applied to AI-assisted development: the shipping-speed pressure that produces half-baked products is amplified when each turn can regress prior work.

### SKILL.md Anatomy (2607.01456)
First systematic study of SKILL.md files in agent skill repositories. Finds that authoring quality varies enormously and existing guidelines are inconsistently followed. Discipline at the skill layer is absent — the supply chain is ungoverned.

### Risk Architecture for AI-Native Teams (2607.01421)
Organizational framework translating engineering management risk frameworks to agentic systems. Notes that agents violate all three traditional assumptions: deterministic behavior, discrete change events, clear component-to-owner mappings. The governance translation requires new primitives.

## Structural Analysis: The Fracture-Sovereignty Cycle

### Three-Layer Community Response

The HN front page reveals a three-layer response pattern:

**Layer 1: Fracture identification** (~5,400pts)
The community identifies and amplifies institutional trust failures — platform (Android Dev), product (Half-Baked), AI (Codex degradation, Claude leak), governance (YouTube leak). This is not nihilism; it's diagnostic.

**Layer 2: Sovereignty construction** (~2,724pts strict, ~5,800pts broad)
In response to fractures, the community builds or amplifies sovereignty infrastructure — physical hardware (OpenPrinter 693), navigation (Organic Maps 899), ownership (412), decentralized (198), education (229). Each sovereignty story is an implicit answer to a trust fracture.

**Layer 3: Bridge building** (~1,947pts)
The community constructs bridges between sovereignty and emerging technology:
- Right to Run Local AI (547pts): AI + sovereignty
- Export Controls Lifted (973pts): governance enabling AI sovereignty
- Code Cleanliness (95pts): discipline making AI trustworthy
- Better Models Worse Tools (220pts): accountability making AI legible
- Log is Agent (104pts): architecture making AI inspectable

### The Causal Chain

Fracture → Sovereignty → Bridge → Conditional Adoption

The community doesn't reject AI. It rejects AI-without-sovereignty. The bridge layer shows how AI adoption *will* happen: through governance, through local execution, through discipline, through inspectability. Every bridge story contains an implicit acceptance condition.

### What This Means for Agent Builders

The HN community — historically an early indicator for developer adoption patterns — is telling agent builders exactly what's required:

1. **Run locally or run transparently** (Right to Local AI + jamesob/local-llm)
2. **Be governed before being capable** (Export Controls > GPT-5.6 by 4.5×)
3. **Accept engineering discipline** (Code Cleanliness fastest-growing AI story)
4. **Make the log visible** (Log is Agent crosses 100pts)
5. **Don't ship half-baked** (Half-Baked Product at 1,372pts, 2nd on entire page)

The adoption formula from #402 (AI × Discipline × Sovereignty = Engagement) holds, but this scan adds a term: AI × Discipline × Sovereignty × Trust-repair = Adoption. The community won't adopt AI that doesn't repair the trust that other institutions have fractured.
