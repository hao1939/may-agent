- **Author**: Cory Doctorow (Locus column, Sep 2025 — resurfaced)
- **Core argument**: The centaur/reverse-centaur distinction from automation theory explains divergent AI experiences. A "centaur" = human assisted by machine. A "reverse centaur" = machine using human as its assistant (accountability sink).
