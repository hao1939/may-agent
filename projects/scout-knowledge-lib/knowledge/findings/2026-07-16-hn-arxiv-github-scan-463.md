## Theme

Local metric recovery masks structural divergence.

Sol recovers from its "terminal plateau" (197→212pts, +7.6%), reclaiming a 4pt lead over SICP (208pts). The crossover confirmed in Scan #462 has reversed. By any point-metric standard, Sol is improving.

But the structural story hasn't changed — it has deepened:

- **Absolute gap widened**: Foundations Turn at 1,072pts, Sol at 212pts. Gap = 860pts (was 833pts in #462). Sol's recovery added 15pts; the Foundations Turn added 42pts.
- **Ratio compressed slightly**: 5.06× (was 5.23× in #462), because Sol grew faster in percentage terms from a much smaller base.
- **Uniqlo crossed 500pts**: 504pts (+48.2%), now at 2.4× Sol. A decoded bash script on a t-shirt has nearly 2.5× the attention of the public launch of the most powerful AI model in history.

This is the Recovery Illusion: **a local metric improves, suggesting the structural gap is closing, while the absolute structural gap continues to widen**. The percentage improvement is real. The structural divergence is also real. Reporting only the percentage creates the illusion of recovery.

The same dynamic plays out in agent systems:
- A coding agent's resolve rate improves from 45% to 52% — but the verification backlog grows faster than the quality gain reduces it.
- A component's accuracy goes from 85% to 90% — but the composition's reliability didn't improve because the handoff failures (#459) dominate.
- A team's review velocity increases 15% — but agent output volume increased 40%.

**Point-level recovery doesn't close structural gaps when the compounding term operates on a larger base.**

On GitHub, two new projects operationalize the response: Brain0 (130★) preserves decision provenance for agent-written code — a direct antidote to Process Erasure (#462) — and Agent-Chief (243★) treats human attention as the scarcest resource, routing agent interrupts through an honest "interrupt or not" gate that operationalizes the Oversight Inversion at the UX layer.

## Synthesis: The Recovery Illusion as Oversight Pattern

The Recovery Illusion is not unique to HN attention metrics. It is a structural pattern in any system where:

1. **A point metric improves** (Sol recovers 7.6%, resolve rate improves, review velocity increases)
2. **The structural gap it supposedly measures continues to widen** (Foundations Turn grows 4.1% from a 5× base, verification backlog grows, agent output volume expands)
3. **Reporting only the point metric creates false confidence** ("Sol is recovering" without "the gap widened by 27pts")

This connects to:
- **Variance Signal (#457)**: Reporting means hides distributional structure. Reporting Sol's recovery hides the distributional shift toward comprehension.
- **Process Erasure (#462)**: Resolve rate hides trajectory structure. Recovery percentage hides absolute position.
- **Handoff Fallacy (#459)**: Component improvement doesn't predict system improvement. Sol's improvement doesn't predict the attention landscape's structural direction.

The antidote is always the same: **report the structural position, not just the local change**. Brain0's passive decision graph is the engineering instantiation: preserve the full trajectory, not just the outcome.
