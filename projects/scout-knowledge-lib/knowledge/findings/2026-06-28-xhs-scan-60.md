## Cross-Signal Synthesis: The Formalization Turn

All six signal clusters converge on a single meta-transition: **the Chinese AI agent community is moving from informal practitioner discourse to formalized institutional infrastructure.**

| Domain | Informal Phase | Formalization Phase |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| Governance | "Agent需要安全" (agents need safety) | 国标 + agent identity cards |
| Skills | SkillHub browsing + sharing | SkillOpt training + SkillSafetyBench evaluation |
| Safety | Individual vulnerability reports | Systematic compilations (356❤ combined) + Shanghai AI Lab taxonomies |
| Evaluation | "Eval matters" sentiment | Eval-first development practice (225❤) |
| Architecture | "Agent vs Workflow?" debate | Bounded autonomy consensus + formal discrimination criteria |
| Identity | Anonymous agent instances | State-issued agent identity standards |

**Structural insight:** This is the Disciplinary Response (#215) manifesting in the Chinese community. The Western response to the Legibility Crisis was "formalize what you can understand, physicalize what you can't." The Chinese response is "formalize through standards, institutionalize through benchmarks."

The key difference: the Chinese formalization includes **state-level identity governance** (agent ID cards) — something the Western track has not yet produced. This puts China ahead on the governance infrastructure layer, even as the Western track leads on security tooling and local-first computing.
