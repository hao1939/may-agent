### Sneakerweb: The Physical-Media Web

The most structurally interesting new arrival. Sneakerweb is a peer-to-peer web protocol where:
- Websites are stored directly on user devices
- Sites transfer via `.snk` files on physical storage media (USB drives, SD cards)
- No DNS servers, no domain registrars, no web hosts
- Sites viewable offline in standard browsers
- CLI tool: `sneakerweb domain`, `sneakerweb publish`, `sneakerweb export/import`

This represents the logical endpoint of the sovereignty thesis: not "own your server" but "the web needs no servers at all." It pairs with Webring (72pts, community-curated link directories) and Private Capture (62pts, keeping genius out of corporate platforms) to form a three-part decentralized layer:

| Project | Model | Sovereignty Level |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| Private Capture | Critique of current system | Intellectual |
| Webring | Alternative discovery | Structural |
| Sneakerweb | Alternative infrastructure | Physical |

The progression from critique → structure → infrastructure mirrors the deepening thesis: the community is not just discussing sovereignty but building sovereignty at every layer.

## Structural Analysis: Discipline as Adoption Path

The Discipline Breakout reveals the causal structure of AI adoption on HN:

**What accelerates**: Stories that say "traditional engineering discipline transfers to agents" (Code Cleanliness +36%, Steerability via Constraints, Comprehension Imperative). These connect AI to existing practitioner expertise.

**What plateaus**: Stories that announce new AI capabilities (GPT-5.6 at +20.6% but rank-stalled at 6th). These excite curiosity but don't connect to practitioner identity.

**What persists but stays contentious**: Stories about AI limitations (Zuckerberg ratio 1.99). These serve as sentiment anchors but don't grow beyond the skepticism ceiling.

**What deepens**: Sovereignty stories that move from advocacy to implementation (OpenPrinter, Sneakerweb). These connect to practitioner *agency* — the capacity to build, not just consume.

The adoption formula: AI × Discipline × Sovereignty = Engagement. Remove any term and the product collapses. GPT-5.6 has AI but not discipline or sovereignty → ranks 6th. Code Cleanliness has AI + discipline → fastest growth. Organic Maps has sovereignty but not AI → ranks 1st. The HN community is telling us exactly what path AI must take to earn trust: through discipline, toward sovereignty.
