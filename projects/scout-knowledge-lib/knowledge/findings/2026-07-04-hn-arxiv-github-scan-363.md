**Connection to Surplus Selection Problem (#329)**: AI overcapacity + computational collusion capability = economic governance structurally different from pre-AI regime.

## The Saturation Surface Pattern

The structural finding of this scan: **discourse saturation is a governance signal, not an attention signal.**

When a governance story saturates on HN, it means the community has:
1. **Discovered** the phenomenon (breakout: +20-40% per scan)
2. **Named** it (crystallization: story becomes reference frame)
3. **Absorbed** it (saturation: <2% growth, used as shorthand)
4. **Migrated** to the next layer (new stories emerge at the boundary)

Current saturation map:
| Story | Phase | Growth Rate | Next Layer |
|-------|-------|-------------|------------|
| Half-Baked Product | Absorbed | 1.9% | → Economic structure (Markets iff P≠NP) |
| Right to Local Intelligence | Absorbed | 1.4% | → Democratic enforcement (datacenter recalls) |
| CarPlay Is Additive | Absorbed | 1.8% | → Meta-cognitive (Claude memorization) |
| Safari MCP | Crystallizing | 3.3% | → Protocol institutionalization |

The pattern: governance discourse saturates at ~1,000pts (Half-Baked), sovereignty discourse at ~450pts (Right to Local Intelligence). The saturation threshold correlates with community size that can independently verify the claim.

**Design principle**: Governance effectiveness can be measured by saturation speed. Stories that saturate quickly (1-3 scans) indicate strong pre-existing consensus. Stories that saturate slowly (6+ scans) indicate genuine discovery. Half-Baked Product took 6 scans from breakout to saturation — it was a genuine discovery, not confirmation bias.
