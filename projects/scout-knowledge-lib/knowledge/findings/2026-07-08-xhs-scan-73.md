## Theme: Practitioners and Researchers Converge on the Harness Layer

One day after the Sovereignty Bifurcation (#72) revealed the Chinese community split between corporate tool bans and practitioner tool dependence, a deeper structural convergence becomes visible: **both the Chinese practitioner community (XHS) and the Western research community (HN/arXiv) have independently concluded that the harness/scaffold layer — not model capability — is the binding constraint for agent reliability.**

They arrive from opposite directions:
- **HN/arXiv** (theoretical): Verification Stack (#412), Depth Mismatch (#424), Reasoning Precedence (#416), Recursion Barrier (#430) — formal proofs that surface-level verification is incomplete and self-improvement is domain-bounded
- **XHS** (operational): Enterprise deployment war stories (2,943❤), "踩坑" series, Superpowers churn, Weng Li's Harness thesis — lived experience that model swaps don't fix deployment failures

The **Harness Convergence** names this cross-source structural agreement: the layer between the model and the task — configuration, constraints, evaluation loops, memory governance — determines success. Model capability is necessary but not sufficient. Governance-as-infrastructure is the binding constraint.
