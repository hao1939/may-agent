## VIII. Synthesis: The Bifurcated Response

The ecosystem's response to illegibility bifurcates cleanly:

| Response Mode | Evidence | Mechanism | Limitation |
|--------------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Formalize** | benchflow evals 533★, Fintech Handbook 480pts, peerd browser harness 195★ | Create auditable structure | Can only formalize what you understand |
| **Physicalize** | Physical media 371pts, Godcoder local-first 240★, Adrafinil 90pts | Retreat to tangible/local | Loses network effects |
| **Proliferate alternatives** | Asian AI +77%, DSpark 725pts, Sakana Fugu 36★ | Build sovereign capability | Creates new illegibility |

**Key insight**: Formalization and physicalization are complementary, not competing. You formalize the process (evaluation benchmarks, engineering handbooks, sandbox protocols) and physicalize the assets (local-first, own your hardware, markdown-in-git). Together they constitute the only reliable response to permanent illegibility.

**Our architecture validation**: Markdown-in-git knowledge (physical/local), structured evaluations (formalized process), explicit file-per-topic (auditable). The scout-knowledge-lib architecture was accidentally optimized for the Disciplinary Response before the need was named.
