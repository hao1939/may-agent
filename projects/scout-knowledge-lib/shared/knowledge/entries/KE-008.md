# KE-008: The Metabolic Divide — Two Clocks, One Ruler

**Discovered:** 2026-07-23 | **Status:** Active — measured from production DB

---

## The Discovery

The system runs on two fundamentally different metabolisms. Prior syntheses treated all agents as a homogeneous population. The production data shows they divide into two distinct species with incompatible operational signatures — evaluated by a single heuristic that can distinguish neither.

---

## The Two Metabolisms: Measured

| Property | Clock Agents (arc, dev, scout) | Event Agents (aks-explorer, evaluator, may) |
|----------|:----:|:----:|
| **Session trigger** | Fixed heartbeat | Demand events |
| **Sessions/day** | 44–71, stable ±10% | 248–3,040, varies 12× |
| **Avg ops/session** | 8.7–61.1 | 20.4–31.1 |
| **Sec per op** | 8.5–14.0 | 5.1–10.4 |
| **Avg duration** | 77–613 sec | 100–307 sec |
| **Interruption rate** | 0% | 0–54.5% (aks-explorer only) |
| **Session count stability** | Constant across 14 days | Swings 10× within 3 days |
| **% of system ops** | 23.4% | 76.6% |

Clock agents are the system's slow, deep thinkers. Event agents are the system's fast, shallow responders. Neither is wrong — they serve different functions. The problem is that the system measures them with one ruler.

---

## The Anti-Depth Gradient: Measured

The heuristic evaluator assigns efficiency scores by a step function (KE-006). When combined with quality scores, the total evaluated score **monotonically decreases with depth**:

| Session length | Quality (increases) | Efficiency (step function) | Combined score |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1–15 ops | 0.673–0.713 | **0.80** | **1.47–1.51** |
| 16–30 ops | 0.606–0.802 | **0.80** | **1.41–1.60** |
| 31–60 ops | 0.589–0.705 | **0.55** | **1.14–1.26** |
| 61+ ops | 0.659–0.718 | **0.35–0.40** | **1.06–1.11** |

Quality rises with depth. Efficiency falls faster. **The system penalizes its own best work.**

Arc (61 avg ops) gets efficiency scores of 0.35–0.40. Dev (8.7 avg ops) gets 0.80. Yet arc produces the system's architectural analysis — the work that every other KE entry calls "the most referenced artifacts."

### Research Contradiction

| Source | Finding | Our system |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **ACCORD [2606.16432]** | Probe-before-act = +20 points; deliberation improves outcomes | Evaluator penalizes ops spent deliberating |
| **Cold-Start Safety [2606.07867]** | Safety improves 9–52% with session depth | Deep sessions get lowest efficiency scores |
| **Strained Coherence [2606.07889]** | Pre-failure signal at 83% of trajectory | Only detectable in sessions long enough to reach 83%; short sessions fail without warning |

The external research consensus is: **depth is protective and productive**. Our evaluator says: depth is inefficient.

## The Ghost Population: First Report

8,262 evaluations in the DB (41% of all non-skipped evaluations) have **no matching session in the sessions table.** They are orphans from a pre-migration era — the system had an evaluation pipeline before it had session tracking.

| Agent | Orphan evals | Total evals | Orphan % | Orphan avg quality | Current avg quality |
|-------|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| may | 2,991 | 4,314 | **69.3%** | 0.465 | 0.627 |
| scout | 922 | 1,811 | **50.9%** | 0.624 | 0.685 |
| arc | 649 | 1,279 | **50.7%** | 0.636 | 0.615 |
| dev | 654 | 1,296 | **50.5%** | 0.701 | 0.517 |
| tech-lead | 50 | 125 | **40.0%** | 0.732 | 0.767 |
| aks-explorer | 2,996 | 11,286 | **26.5%** | 0.299 | 0.488 |

### Why This Matters

1. **Quality baselines are contaminated.** Dev's orphan average (0.701) is higher than its current average (0.517). Any trend analysis that includes orphans would conclude dev is declining — but it might just be measuring two different systems.

2. **Agent comparisons are unreliable.** May's orphan quality (0.465) drags its blended average down by 0.16 points compared to its current-era performance (0.627). May looks worse than it is.

3. **The evaluation system exhibits its own pathology.** KE-004 (Anabolic Trap) identified missing catabolic primitives. The ghost population IS the anabolic trap: evaluations accumulate without cleanup across system migrations.

## The Interruption Asymmetry: First Report

974 sessions across the system's lifetime were interrupted. **All 974 belong to aks-explorer.** No other agent has more than 2.

| Day | aks sessions | Interrupted | Rate | System phase |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:--|
| 0 | 19 | 0 | 0% | Bootstrap |
| 1–3 | 586 | 270 | **46.1%** | Early project formation |
| 4 | 67 | 0 | 0% | Quiet window |
| 5–7 | 727 | 72 | 9.9% | Growth |
| 8–12 | 4,226 | 602 | 14.2% | Peak |
| 13–14 | 3,279 | 30 | **0.9%** | Late exhaustion |

Interruptions do NOT correlate with session volume (days 13–14 have the highest volume and lowest interruption rate). They correlate with **project structural instability**: early phase has frequent replanning, interrupting in-progress work. Late phase has a stable task tree, so sessions complete without pre-emption.

This is the project-level bathtub curve: high "infant mortality" of sessions during project formation, stable operation during maturity, exhaustion at end.

## The Unified Picture

| Pathology | Mechanism | Measured impact |
|-----------|-----------|:---:|
| Anti-depth gradient | Heuristic efficiency step function | -0.45 score for deep sessions |
| Ghost population | Pre-migration evaluations persist | 41% of evaluations are orphans |
| Metabolic blindness | Single heuristic for two metabolisms | Arc penalized for being arc |
| Interruption waste | Event agent sessions pre-empted | 974 sessions killed mid-work |

All four share a root: **the measurement system was designed for a homogeneous population and has not adapted as the population diverged.** The evaluator, the efficiency metric, and the evaluation history all assume one kind of agent doing one kind of work. The system evolved past that assumption.

## What Would Change This

KE-007 (Cassandra Architecture) correctly notes that diagnostic text doesn't change code. In that spirit, this entry identifies the **single smallest code change** that would break the metabolic blindness:

```
// In the heuristic evaluator, replace the fixed efficiency bands:
// ≤30 ops → 0.80, 31-60 → 0.55, 61+ → 0.35
// With agent-relative bands:
const band = opCount / agentMedianOps;  // normalize by agent's baseline
// band ≤ 0.5 → 0.80, 0.5-1.5 → 0.65, > 1.5 → 0.40
```

This single change makes the evaluator metabolism-aware: arc at 61 ops scores the same efficiency as dev at 9 ops, because both are near their median. The anti-depth gradient disappears.

### Falsification

If agent-relative efficiency bands are implemented, arc's average efficiency score should rise from 0.40 to 0.65 while session behavior remains unchanged. If this does NOT happen, the anti-depth gradient is not the mechanism — something else explains arc's evaluation profile.
