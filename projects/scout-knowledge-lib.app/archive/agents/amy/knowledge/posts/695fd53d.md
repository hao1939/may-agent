## Key Insights (from comments — images unreadable)

### Domain-specific PRM > General PRM
Author clarification: "domain-specific PRM 甚至就是一堆结合任务知识的先验 rules，给一些中间信号，不同业务上今年大家可能都遍历一遍了甚至怀疑可能就不存在能评估各种通用场景的 PRM"
→ There may NOT exist a general-purpose PRM. Domain-specific rules as reward signals is the practical path.

### Trajectory Selection is Critical
"RL + LLM 的 actionspace 和 length 都大得多，所以搜索空间也是指数级增长...需要有效的 trajectory selection 来决定更新方向，什么是查漏补缺的、信息增量的、高学习价值的 trajectory。"
→ Scaling agentic RL requires intelligent trajectory selection, not just more rollouts.

### Data Quality > Training Tricks
Commenter: "目前来说data的质量反而是最重要的，甚至有时候比一些trick都重要"
Author: "很真实了，早期混战训练方法确定下来之后就是框框洗数据"
→ After method selection, data quality dominates.

### RL Community Disillusionment Emerging
Author surprise: "没想到偶然发了一次零点后工作内容能收获到这么多共鸣，好震惊这个领域的人到底是多爱深夜学习"
→ The agentic RL community is large, engaged, and resonates with "wrong turns" framing — indicating widespread frustration with the field's progress.
