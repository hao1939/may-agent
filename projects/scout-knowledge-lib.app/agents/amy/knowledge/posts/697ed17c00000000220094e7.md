## Core Thesis
In the Agent era, evaluation sets ARE the best Product Requirements Documents (PRDs). Traditional PRDs define what to build; eval sets define what "good" looks like probabilistically.

## Key Distinction (from author's comment)
"Traditional test cases are deterministic — only pass/fail. Evaluation is probabilistic — it includes not just test questions but a complex scoring system. It tests not just CAN it do something, but HOW WELL it does it."
