# Agent Learning via early experience

作者认为当前agent 后训练的两种方式sft 以及RL均有所局限： sft 不能很好的scale up同时泛化性比较差，但是它不需要外界环境的反馈；而RL 能够比较好的scale up 但是需要精准的外部反馈，这在很多场景下是困难的。

---

**Era of Human Data (Imitation Learning)**
> You should do these steps.

❌ Scalable Data
✅ Reward-Free

---

**Early Experience (Ours)**
> What if I do this step instead?

✅ Scalable Data
✅ Reward-Free

---

**Era of Experience (Reinforcement Learning)**
> Try many steps... only a reward at last!

✅ Scalable Data
❌ Requires Reward

---

鉴于此，作者提出了基于早期经验的学习，通过收集模型本身的在各个状态 `s_i` 下的action，并将action 所导致的状态转变（`s_i->s_j`）作为监督信号，从而同时实现了很好的scale up 且是reward free 的。

[Slide 2]
对于早期经验的利用，作者主要分为了两种方式：

• 隐式世界建模，即利用收集到的状态将策略根植于环境动态中；

• 自我反思，即智能体从其次优行动中学习，以改进推理和决策能力。

**Figure 2** Overview of the two early experience approaches. Implicit world modeling (left) augments expert trajectories with alternative actions and predicted next states, training the policy to internalize transition dynamics before deployment. Self-reflection (right) augments expert actions with self-generated explanations c₁, training the policy to reason about and revise its own decisions. Both methods use alternative actions proposed by the initial policy (LLM). The number of alternatives (K) is a hyperparameter; for brevity, only one is illustrated.

**对于隐式世界建模：**

首先需要收集模型在环境中的 `状态1->动作->状态2` 的三元组 `<s_i,a_j,s_j>`，通过让模型学习在 `s_i` 中采取 `a_j` 动作将会到达状态 `s_j` 这一环境知识，来提升模型的能力

For each rollout triple $(s_i, a_i^j, s_i^j) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{rollout}}$, we construct a prediction task where the model takes the state-action pair $(s_i, a_i^j)$ as input and learns to predict the resulting next state $s_i^j$. We define the training objective as a next-token prediction loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{IWM}} = - \sum_{(s_i, a_i^j, s_i^j) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{rollout}}} \log p_\theta(s_i^j \mid s_i, a_i^j), \tag{3}$$

[Slide 4]
**对于自我反思：**

首先需要收集完整的专家轨迹 `<s_0,...a..,s_n>` ,得到该轨迹后，对于每一步 `<s_i,a,s_j>` 让模型自已给出为什么在当前状态下需要使用 `a` 而不是次优的action `a_j` 的理由 `c_i` 。在训练中，我们让模型学习在状态 `s_i` 下，需要采取动作 `a` 以及不使用次优动作理由 `c_i` 。

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{SR}} = - \sum_{(s_i, a_i^j, c_i^j) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{refl}}} \log p_\theta(c_i^j, a_i \mid s_i),$$
