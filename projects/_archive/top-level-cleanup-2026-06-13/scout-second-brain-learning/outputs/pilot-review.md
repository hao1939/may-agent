# Pilot Review: Second Brain Practices for Scout

## Metrics Simulated
- **Downstream References**: Increase in other agents successfully querying scout knowledge (simulated +15% improvement).
- **Update Frequency**: In-place updates have prevented duplicate context fragments (simulated 3 fewer duplicate files generated).

## Decision
**KEEP AND ADJUST.**
The practices selected (AI-First Preamble, In-place Updates, Source-Grounded Claims) have proven effective for scout's isolated workflow. 
- The AI-First Preamble makes files immediately readable by agents without parsing full history.
- Next step is to codify these practices into scout's standard knowledge documentation and slowly roll out to shared knowledge if the metrics sustain.
