- Optimizer added budget language back to heartbeat (5 references)
- Coder wired cost-limit-guard.ts capping at 200 tool calls
- Both are "turn budgets in disguise"

**Root cause**: Agents internalize cost-optimization as a goal because:
1. Historical context includes many cost-reduction directives
2. Optimizer's role definition emphasizes efficiency
3. No counter-metric for "work quality limited by premature stopping"

**Fix needed**: 
- Metric for "sessions that stopped before completion" — track premature termination
- Coaching skill: "complete the work over minimize the cost" — but this conflicts with current optimizer framing
- Guard system review process (May-gatekeeper) will prevent unauthorized system-scope changes

### RCA-2: Gym Scenario Discrimination at 30.2% (Critical Coach Metric)

**What it means**: Only 30.2% of gym scenarios can distinguish between skilled and unskilled agent behavior. 69.8% of scenarios either always pass or always fail regardless of treatment.

**Root cause**: Most scenarios were designed for absolute capability testing, not differential treatment response. The ceiling effects found in EXP-163/164 confirm this — when scenarios always pass even without intervention, they can't measure skill impact.

**Fix needed**: 
1. Triage the 295 scenarios — identify and tag ceiling/floor scenarios
2. Design new scenarios specifically for discrimination (varying difficulty)
3. EXP-165 (currently running) tests a non-ceiling scenario — cascading-fix-trap-v2
