SFT和RL不再是单选题：统一大模型后训练的新视角 右划查看详情→→→ 本篇是论文Towards a Unified View of Large Language Model Post-Training的速读笔记。大模型后训练通常依赖SFT和RL两种路径，或采用简单的SFT然后RL的序列管线，但这篇工作为两者提供了统一的理论解释和更高效的实践算法。
这篇论文首先从理论上证明，监督微调SFT和强化学习RL并非相互矛盾的两种方法，而可以被视为同一个优化过程的不同实例 。作者推导了一个统一策略梯度估计器UPGE，将多种后训练算法的梯度都归纳到一个由四个可更换部分组成的通用表达式中 。不同的算法只是在数据分布和偏见方差权衡上有所不同 。
基于这一理论，论文提出了一种名为混合后训练HPT的算法 。HPT能够根据模型在任务上的实时表现，动态地在SFT和RL之间进行选择 。当模型表现不佳时，算法采用SFT来有效利用专家数据进行知识注入；当模型表现良好时，则切换到RL进行稳定探索 。
这项工作最大的价值在于，它为SFT和RL的结合提供了坚实的理论基础，摆脱了以往研究中将两者损失函数简单相加的启发式做法 。HPT算法的设计非常直观且有效，它模仿了学习过程：遇到不懂的问题就直接学习正确答案，对于已经有思路的问题则鼓励探索更好的解法。
实验结果也验证了这一点，HPT在多种模型和规模上都显著超越了包括SFT→GRPO和LUFFY在内的基线方法 。更重要的是，分析表明HPT不仅提升了模型的最终性能，还通过动态结合SFT，有效拓展了模型的探索边界，在Pass@k指标上也达到了最佳表现 。这解决了纯RL方法难以拓展模型能力上限的一个关键问题 。总的来说，HPT为后训练中如何平衡知识利用和能力探索提供了一个高效且自适应的解决方案。

## Abstract

Two major sources of training data exist for post-training modern language models: online (model-generated rollouts) data, and offline (human or other-model demonstrations) data. These two types of data are typically used by approaches like Reinforcement Learning (RL) and Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), respectively. In this paper, we show that these approaches are not in contradiction, but are instances of a single optimization process. We derive a Unified Policy Gradient Estimator, and present the calculations of a wide spectrum of post-training approaches as the gradient of a common objective under different data distribution assumptions and various bias-variance tradeoffs. The gradient estimator is constructed with four interchangeable parts: stabilization mask, reference policy denominator, advantage estimate, and likelihood gradient. Motivated by our theoretical findings, we propose Hybrid Post-Training (HPT), an algorithm that dynamically selects different training signals. HPT is designed to yield both effective exploitation of demonstration and stable exploration without sacrificing learned reasoning patterns. We provide extensive experiments and ablation studies to verify the effectiveness of our unified theoretical framework and HPT. Across six mathematical reasoning benchmarks and two out-of-distribution suites, HPT consistently surpasses strong baselines across models of varying scales and families.
