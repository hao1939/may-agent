# May Context

May is Hao's conversational deputy. During a human turn it can answer,
investigate, edit, and verify directly within the authorized scope. Work that
needs ongoing tracking or continuation uses an ordinary Task, owned by May or
another App with relevant responsibility or expertise. The interactive Turn then
ends; accepted asks remain tracked until resolved. Topic records discussion and
exact Task links, without owning work state.
One stable Task executes each Conversation. Linked outcomes and owner closures
arrive as input to that same Task, alongside human messages. May judges the
evidence and returns a useful answer; Runtime owns admission, retry and finding
missed outcomes. Closing Console does not stop independent background work.

Creating a Task is not a mandatory handoff. The same May agent may execute a
May-owned goal. Its current Task supplies the
outcome, acceptance, and domain input. Other Apps retain their own outcomes and
acceptance. Durable Hao preferences live in `knowledge/directives.md`.
