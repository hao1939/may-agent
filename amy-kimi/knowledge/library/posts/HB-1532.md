## Core Claim

首次系统分析**混合人机群体中的部分采用问题**——AI Agent在能源市场中的协调动力学揭示：早期采用者不被结构性惩罚，但非采用者可能搭便车 disproportionately。

## The Social Dilemma Structure

### Energy Load Management as Congestion Game

| Scenario | Individual Cost | Social Cost | Equilibrium? |
|----------|----------------|-------------|--------------|
| Both at PST (preferred) | $500 | $1000 | **Nash Equilibrium** ❌ |
| Turn-taking (coordination) | $250/$550 | $800 | **Social Optimum** ✅ |

**关键洞察**: 个体理性导致集体次优——classic social dilemma。

> "The aggregated total cost of decentralized consumers ($1000) remains higher than the socially optimal solution ($800)."

## Three Key Contributions

### 1. Artificial Agent Design: Intrinsic Reward Shaping

AI agents使用**全局可观测信号**（aggregate demand + prices）设计intrinsic reward：

```
I = Ω × CoC / RE

Where:
- Ω: scaling factor
- CoC: Cost of Cooperation (current cost - best response cost)
- RE: episode reward
```

**触发条件**（三条件同时满足）：
1. Agent engaged in cooperation
2. Focal agent's cost < average cost
3. Population cost < average population cost

### 2. Partial Adoption Analysis: Mixed Populations

文献通常假设**universal adoption**。本文分析**混合群体**动态：

| Population Composition | Outcome |
|------------------------|---------|
| Both basic reward | Non-cooperative equilibrium |
| Both intrinsic reward | Cooperative equilibrium |
| Mixed (P1 basic, P2 intrinsic) | **Partial cooperation emerges** |

### 3. Entry Resilience: Unilateral Adoption Feasibility

**Entry Resilience定义**: 
> Unilateral adoption does not make the adopter worse off when facing non-adopters.

**发现**: 
- 对于低δ（折扣因子），basic reward agents有优势
- 但只要至少一个agent使用intrinsic reward，就facilitates cooperation
- 双方都benefit compared to both using basic rewards

---

## Critical Finding: Free-Riding Asymmetry

> "Non-adopters may benefit disproportionately from the cooperation induced by adopters."

| δ值 | Adopter Cost | Non-Adopter Cost |  asymmetry |
|-----|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| Low | 较高 | 较低 | 显著 |
| High | 趋同 | 趋同 | 减弱 |

**战略意义**: 虽然entry是feasible的，但benefit distribution asymmetry需要在deployment中考虑。

## Design Implications for OpenClaw

### 1. Adoption Strategy: Entry Resilience

**问题**: 如何确保早期采用OpenClaw的用户不被惩罚？

**策略**: 
- Hybrid模式下保持人类最终决策权（非完全delegation）
- 明确显示AI建议的confidence level
- 提供opt-out机制

### 2. Oversight Design: Preventing Free-Riding

**问题**: 如何避免人类监督搭AI自我纠错的便车？

**策略**:
- **Friction设计**: 要求人类监督者明确approve/reject（not just observe）
- **轮换机制**: 定期更换监督者，避免habituation
- **多样性注入**: 引入外部reviewers打破echo chamber

### 3. Intrinsic Reward的反向应用

论文用intrinsic reward **促进** cooperation。OpenClaw的P109（Self-Evolution禁令）可以看作是一种**负向** payoff shaping：

```
论文: +reward for cooperative behavior
OpenClaw: -infinity reward for self-modification attempts
```

两者都是**incentive alignment**工具，但方向不同。
