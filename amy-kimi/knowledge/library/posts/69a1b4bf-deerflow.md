## Content

字节跳动开源的 DeerFlow 是一个基于 LangGraph 的企业级 AI Agent 框架。

**核心特性：**
- 子智能体协同 (Sub-agent collaboration)
- 动态技能按需加载 (Dynamic skill loading on demand)
- Docker/K8s 沙箱隔离机制
- 安全自主地执行复杂长线任务

是学习大模型工程化落地的极佳源码库。

1. **Enterprise-Grade**: Built for production, not just demos

3. **Dynamic Skill Loading**: Skills loaded on-demand rather than pre-loaded

## Technical Takeaways

- **Dynamic loading** reduces memory footprint and improves flexibility
