# HB#1683: BiCC - Bilateral Context Conditioning for GRPO

**Source**: arXiv:2603.13134v1 [cs.AI] 13 Mar 2026  
**Title**: When Right Meets Wrong: Bilateral Context Conditioning with Reward-Confidence Correction for GRPO  
**Authors**: Yu Li, Tian Lan, Zhengling Qi  
**Code**: https://github.com/Skylanding/BiCC

## Abstract

Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) has emerged as an effective method for training reasoning models. While it computes advantages based on group mean, GRPO treats each output as an independent sample during optimization and overlooks a vital structural signal: the natural contrast between correct and incorrect solutions within the same group. This paper presents a contrastive reformulation of GRPO and proposes two key innovations:

1. **Bilateral Context Conditioning (BiCC)**: Enables model to cross-reference successful and failed reasoning traces during optimization
2. **Reward-Confidence Correction (RCC)**: Stabilizes training by dynamically adjusting advantage baseline using reward-confidence covariance

## Core Contributions

### 1. Contrastive Reformulation of GRPO

The paper mathematically shows that GRPO objective implicitly maximizes the margin between policy ratios of correct and incorrect samples:

```
J_GRPO(θ) = E_q[σ_q · (ρ̄_clip+ - ρ̄_clip-)]
```

Where the pairwise contrastive form reveals GRPO optimizes over all positive-negative pairs, yet each sample is evaluated independently without awareness of other outputs.

### 2. Bilateral Context Conditioning (BiCC)

**Key Idea**: Let the model observe incorrect attempts when evaluating correct ones, and vice versa.

**Mechanism**:
- Partition group outputs by reward: O+ = {o_i | r_i = 1}, O- = {o_i | r_i = 0}
- Construct augmented contexts:
  - x+ = [q; O-] (correct solutions observe failure traces)
  - x- = [q; O+] (incorrect solutions observe successful traces)
- Conditioned importance sampling ratios:
  - ρ_i+ = π_θ(o_i|x+) / π_θ_old(o_i|q) for o_i ∈ O+
  - ρ_j- = π_θ(o_j|x-) / π_θ_old(o_j|q) for o_j ∈ O-

**Theoretical Grounding**: Based on Learning Using Privileged Information (LUPI; Pechyony and Vapnik, 2010) - opposite-partition samples serve as privileged context available only during training, enabling contrastive learning with zero inference overhead.

**Results**: BiCC yields consistent gains of 0.3-1.9 percentage points across settings, with larger gains on weaker base models.

### 3. Reward-Confidence Correction (RCC)

**Problem**: Standard GRPO uses group mean as baseline, which is only optimal when importance weights are independent of rewards. In practice, models assign higher probability to outputs they consider correct, inducing correlation between rewards and policy confidence.

**Solution**: Approximate variance-minimizing baseline under importance sampling:

```
b* ≈ E[R] + 2·Cov(R,δ)
```

Where δ = log π_θ - log π_ref

**Corrected Advantage**:
```
A_i_RCC = r_i - R̄ - 2·Cov̂(R,δ)
```

**Results**: RCC reduces gradient variance by 25-35%.

## Empirical Validation

- **Models**: Qwen3-4B, Phi-4-mini
- **Benchmarks**: Math500, AMC 2023, AIME 2024, AIME 2025
- **Key Finding**: Cov(R,δ) increases monotonically throughout training, reaching 0.066 for Qwen3-4B and 0.138 for Phi-4-mini
- **Distribution Analysis**: Clear separation between correct and incorrect samples in δ distribution, with mean differences of 0.27 and 0.56 respectively

## Compatibility

BiCC can be applied uniformly across GRPO family:
- Standard GRPO
- Dr.GRPO (with KL divergence)
- DAPO (asymmetric clipping)
- GSPO (token-level)
