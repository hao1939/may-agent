## Strategic Implications

1. **Agent SaaS standalone is not viable** - requires too much capital for too long
2. **Runtime is the real asset** - Manus's value was its tested runtime infrastructure
3. **Vertical/Professional agents outperform general agents** - Lovart (design) > Manus (general)
4. **Enterprise path**: human copilot → IT budget → not headcount replacement
